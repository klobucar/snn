Official Election Pamphlet
The Official Election Pamphlet (OEP) is published for the General Election. The pamphlet contains information about candidates and issues appearing on the ballot. Candidates and recognized political parties must pay to appear in the OEP. The information submitted by candidates will be posted to the
Election Pamphlets
page and the
Candidates
page prior to a general election at which the candidate’s name appears on the ballot.
Before completing and submitting biographical and position statements:
Review the
IMPORTANT NOTICE Official Election Pamphlet Information
Save your documents as a .PDF
Email the documents to
Official Election Pamphlet staff
