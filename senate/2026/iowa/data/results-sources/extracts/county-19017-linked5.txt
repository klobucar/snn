Public Measures
Statewide Constitutional Amendment - 1
         Shall the following amendment to the Constitution be adopted?

             o   YES
             o   NO

          Summary: Provides that any bill to increase the individual income tax rate or the corporate income tax
         rate, or the rate of any other type of tax based upon income or legal and special reserves, shall require
         the affirmative votes of at least two-thirds of the members elected to each house of the legislature for
         passage. Further, the two-thirds majority vote requirement applies to the passage of a bill to establish a
         new tax on any type of income or legal and special reserves imposed by the state. The requirement
         excludes taxes imposed at the option of a local government. A lawsuit challenging the proper enactment
         of a bill under this section must be filed no later than one year following the enactment.
