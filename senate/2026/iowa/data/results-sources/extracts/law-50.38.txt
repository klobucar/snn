  50.38 Time of state canvass.
  Not later than twenty-seven days after the day of the election, the secretary of state shall
present to the board of state canvassers abstracts of votes cast at the election showing the
number of ballots cast for each office and a summary of the results for each office, showing
the votes cast in each county. The state board of canvassers shall review the results compiled
by the secretary of state and, if the results are accurately tabulated, the state board shall
approve the canvass.
