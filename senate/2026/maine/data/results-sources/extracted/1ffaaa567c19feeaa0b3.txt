Voter Registration is available for new voters to register or for existing Fort Fairﬁeld voters to change
                             their residence address or name as follows:
                 Online at https://registertovote.sos.maine.gov (available until 10/29/26)
                 By Mail by submitting a completed voter registration card along with a photocopied ID and proof of
                  residency. (After October 13th, voters must register in person).
                 At the polling place on election day (there may be lines so please plan accordingly)
                 At the Fort Fairﬁeld Town O ice during regular business hours (M-F 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.)

                                 To register vote you will need to provide:
Proof of identity (including, but not limited to a current and valid driver’s license, state ID, military ID, passport,
        certiﬁed birth certiﬁcate, signed social security card, veriﬁed unique identifying numbers OR other o icial documents)

Proof of residency (any piece of mail or document showing your Fort Fairﬁeld residence address, or an oath of
                                     residency when proof of identity can be provided)

                                  Absentee Ballots may be requested by:
   The voter by telephone by calling 472-3800 (deadline 10/29/26)
   Written request to Town Clerk, 18 Community Center Drive, Fort Fairﬁeld (deadline 10/29/26)
   Visiting the Town O ice in-person (M-F 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.) (deadline 10/29/26)
   Online at http://www.maine.gov/cgi-bin/online/AbsenteeBallot/index.pl (deadline 10/29/26)

                               The deadline to vote an in-person absentee ballot is
                                          Thursday, October 29, 2026
    (with the exception of incapacitation/illness or unexpected absence on election day sworn under oath).

  Completed absentee ballots must be received by 8 p.m. on November 4 th and may be
returned by mail, in person to the Town O ice, or deposited in the secure drop box located
                      outside of the main entrance to the Town O ice.
