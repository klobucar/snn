                                               STATE OF MAINE
                                  Warrant for the Hollis Special Town Meeting
                                              November 05, 2024


STATE OF MAINE                                                                         COUNTY OF YORK


To Any Resident in the Town of Hollis in the County of York, State of Maine.

Greetings:

In the name of the State of Maine, you are hereby required to notify and warn the inhabitants in said
Town of Hollis qualified to vote in town affairs to assemble at the Hollis Center Church, 388 Hollis Road,
in said Town of Hollis on Tuesday, the fifth day of November A.D. 2024 (November 05, 2024) at 6:45
a.m. in the forenoon to act on Article 1. The polls will then open at 7:00 a.m. (seven o’clock in the
forenoon) and close at 8:00 p.m. (eight o’clock in the evening) to act on Articles 2-5 by secret ballot
voting.

(All proposed amendments and the proposed Shoreland Zoning Map are available for review and
inspection at the Town Clerk’s Office, on our website and will be available at the Special Town
Meeting.

Article 1:        To elect a moderator by written ballot to preside at said town meeting.

                  Martha Huff Town Clerk opened the Special Town Meeting at 6:49 a.m. with
7 voters present and numerous voters in line in the lobby.
 Harlan Huff made the motion to waive the reading of the warrant and it was seconded by
Lila Wilkins. Article 1 Nominations opened for Moderator. Harlan Huff made the motion to
nominate Lila Wilkins and seconded by Carole Deschambault with no further motions made.
The Town Clerk asked those wishing to vote for Moderator to come forward. Lila Wilkins was
elected with 7 votes. Lila Wilkins was given The Moderator Oath by Martha Huff, Town Clerk.
Lila Wilkins Special Town Meeting Moderator & Election Warden ( Warden appointed by the
Select Board on October 31, 2024 ) declared the polls open at 7a.m.
.

The Registrar of Voters will hold regular Town office hours two weeks prior to the election, Tuesday,
Thursday and Friday, 9am-4pm and Wednesday 11am-7pm.

The Registrar of Voters will be available at the polls to correct any errors, correct a name or address on
the voting list and to accept registrations of persons eligible to vote and to accept new enrollments.

A person who is not registered to vote may not vote in any election.

A voter not enrolled in a political party may not vote in a primary election.
