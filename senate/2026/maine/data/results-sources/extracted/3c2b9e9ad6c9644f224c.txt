REGULAR MEETING                            AGENDA                               June 8, 2026

1. Roll Call Councilor Penny York and City Attorney Mary Costigan were excused from
attending this meeting. All other members were present.
2. Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag All present joined in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
3. Public Forum of not more than 30 minutes (5 minute limit each speaker)
4. Council/Manager Response to Public Comment
5. Meeting Notice
6. Reading of the Record
7. Reports:
    a. City Manager’s Report
    b. City Attorney’s Report
    c. Other Official’s Report: Revenue Commission Report
    d. Mayor’s Report

8. General Discussion to allow the Mayor and Council to engage in a discussion of any items
of interest and/or ongoing initiatives.
9. Consent Agenda: None.
10. Licenses & Permits: None.

11. Resolves:
#13 Appointments – Healthy Aging Working Group & Planning Board                Mayor Lachman
A Resolve appointing members to the newly established Healthy Aging Working Group and the
Planning Board.
Motion to Divide the Question to consider the appointment of Timothy Avirett Separately. Vote:
4-0.
Motion to confirm the appointments of the remaining appointees. Vote: 4-0.
Councilor Davis asked to be allowed to abstain from voting on Mr. Avirett’s appointment because
Mr. Avirett is his father-in-law. Vote: 3-0.
Motion to confirm appointment of Mr. Avirett. Vote: 3-0-1 (Davis abstaining).
#14 Commendation – Rockland Fire & EMS Department                                  City Council
A Resolve recognizing and commending the Rockland Fire & EMS Department for its efforts in
battling the fire at the Robbins Lumber Mill on May 15, 2026.
Passed. Vote: 4-0.
#15 Adopting Fair Housing & Equal Opportunity Resolution                           City Council
A Resolve supporting and advancing Fair Housing in Rockland, and a policy statement on Equal
Opportunity Employment, which are required of municipalities receiving CDBG funds.
Passed. Vote: 4-0.

12. Ordinances in Final Reading & Public Hearing:
#12 Chapter 19, Sec. 19-208 Eliminating the Philbrick Ave. Contract Zone Councilor Davis
An amendment to the City’s Zoning Ordinance eliminating the Philbrick Avenue Contract Zone
because changes to the Zoning Ordinance have made this Contract Zone completely obsolete.
(Postponed 05/11/2026).
Postponed to 07/13/2026 Regular Meeting for required Planning Board hearing. Vote: 4-0.
#13 Zoning Map Amendment – 435 & 441 Old County Road                           Councilor Kalloch
An amendment to the City’s Official Zoning Map changing the zone classification of the parcels
located at 435 & 441 Old County Road from Residential B Zone to Rural Residential RR1 Zone.
(Postponed 05/11/2026)
Postponed to 07/13/2026 Regular Meeting for required Planning Board hearing. Vote: 4-0.
#15 Chapter 19, Sec. 19-206 Permitted Uses; Studios                          Councilor Callahan
An amendment to the City’s Zoning Ordinance allowing Studios as a permitted use in all zones
where the use is compatible with the purpose of the zone, other existing uses, and in conformance
with the Comprehensive Plan. (Postponed 05/11/2026)
Postponed to 07/13/2026 Regular Meeting for required Planning Board hearing. Vote: 4-0.
#17 Ch. 2, Sec. 2-214 Use of Affordable Workforce Housing Funds                 Mayor Lachman
An amendment to the City’s Administration Ordinance specifying that a public hearing and City
Council approval be necessary before any borrowed Affordable Workforce Housing Funds are
spent for any purpose, and establishing a definition of “Affordable Workforce Housing”.
Passed. Vote: 4-0. Eff. Date: 07/08/2026.

13. Ordinances in First Reading:
#18 Zoning Map Amendment – C1 to Res. B Park Street                       Councilor Kalloch
An amendment to the City’s Official Zoning Map changing the zone classification of the parcel
located at 270 Park Street from Commercial 1 Zone to Residential B Zone to allow for housing
development.
Passed. Vote: 4-0. Public Hearing Date: 07/13/2026 at 5:30 p.m.
#19 Authorizing Design-Build Lease Purchase Agreement – Police Facility Councilor Davis
Ab Ordinance authorizing a Design-Build Lease Purchase Option Agreement between the City
and Rockland Harbor Park LLC on Suite 215 Rockland Harbor Park Center, 12 Water Street, to be
used as the new location for the Rockland Police Department.
Passed. Vote: 3-1 (Kalloch). Public Hearing Date: 07/13/2026 at 5:30 p.m.

14. Orders:
#41 Establishing Rockland Person of the Year Award                                City Council
An Order establishing the Rockland Person of the Year Award to formally recognize and celebrate
individuals whose contributions to our community exemplify dedication to community, inspire
civic pride, and reflect the values and spirit of the City of Rockland.
Postponed to the 07/13/2026 Regular Meeting. Vote: 4-0.
#42 Public Hearing/CDBG Application – Public Services Grant                      City Manager
An Order holding a public hearing and authorizing the submission of and, if awarded, acceptance
of a Public Services Grant to be used to fund a Community Navigator position through the Pen
Bay YMCA that would assist people from low to moderate income households to receive available
services they are eligible for but may not be aware of.
Passed. Vote: 4-0.
#43 Authorizing Reserve Fund Expenditure – Fish Pier Reserve                 Councilor Kalloch
An Order authorizing the expenditure of up to $20,000 from the Fish Pier Reserve Account to
complete the electrical repairs at the Fish Pier
Passed. Vote: 4-0.
#44 Amending Harbor & Waterfront Fee Schedule                                     Councilor Kalloch
An Order amending various fees charged by the Harbor & Waterfront Department.
Passed. Vote: 4-0.
#45 Accepting Grant Funds – Ed McDonald Safety Grant                                   City Manager
An Order accepting an Ed McDonald Safety Grant from the Maine Municipal Association Workers
Compensation Fund in the amount of $2,940.67 to purchase safety equipment or services to assist
in reducing the frequency and severity of workplace injuries.
Passed. Vote: 4-0.
#46 Accepting Grant Funds – Remote Work Thru Libraries                                 City Manager
An Order accepting the amended award of an additional $4,438 to the Remote Work through
Libraries program from the Maine State Library to the Rockland Public Library, bringing the total
grant award to $30,073.
Passed. Vote: 4-0.
#47 Authorizing Fee Waiver – Blues Festival                                         Mayor Lachman
An Order authorizing the waiver of $3,200 of the park rental fee for the North Atlantic Blues
Festival to make of for the loss of revenue to the festival that will result from the relocation of the
City food vendors from Middle Pier to an area in Harbor Park typically available to festival
vendors.
Passed. Vote: 4-0.
#48 Authorizing Reserve Fund Expenditure – Harbor Improvements                         City Manager
An Order authorizing the expenditure of up to $25,000 from the Harbor Port Development Reserve
Account for infrastructure repairs and improvements at the Harbor.
Passed. Vote: 4-0.

15. Adjournment. Adjourned without objection from the Council at 6:45 p.m.
