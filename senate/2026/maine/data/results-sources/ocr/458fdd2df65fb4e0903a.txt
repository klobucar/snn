Center Drive School election results from naming the room that was formerly referred to
as the Town Annex Meeting Room.

Motion: Curtis Second: Elkin Vote: Yes-4
The Town Librarian, Kelly Brooks, and special guest from the Eagle Gazette, Reese
Boynton; appeared to let the board know that the name chosen by a landslide win; was
The Orangetown Meeting Room. A handout was given; as well as a suggestion to now
paint the room orange. Town manager, Backman, requested that the Eagle Gazette

members pick out different shades of orange and that he will supply the paint chips to
choose from.

11B: Orrington Public Library report- Month of October

Brooks states that event tickets are all going very well. The Dove Christmas Tree is
now up and full of doves to choose from. This is the libraries version of an angel tree,
and encourages people to come in and grab a dove or two. The food bank has had a

really great turnout of recipients; they’ve been able to donate quite a few Thanksgiving
dinners.

10. Discussion on Town Office hours of operations surrounding the holidays.

Motion: Curtis Second: Elkin Vote: Yes-4
Backman states with Christmas being on a Wednesday it will be hard to grant an extra
day off; recommends that we keep normal business hours for the days followin
Christmas Day. The 2nd Selectmen meeting in December would fail on the 23" of the
month; the meeting will remain on the schedule for now; as long as there are no time
pressing issues; it would be easier to cancel a meeting rather than re-scheduling one.
