MUNICIPAL TOWN BUDGET WARRANT
MUNICIPAL REFERENDUM
TOWN OF MECHANIC FALLS

June 10, 2025

To Michelle Emery a resident of the Town of Mechanic Falls, in the County of Androscoggin,
State of Maine,

GREETING:

In the name of the State of Maine, you are hereby required to notify and warn the
inhabitants of the Town of Mechanic Falls in said county and state, qualified by law to vote
in town affairs, to meet at the Town Hall in said Town on Tuesday, the 10th day of June,
2025 A.D., at seven forty-five o'clock in the morning until eight o’clock in the morning, then
and there to act upon Article 1.

Article 1: To elect a moderator by written ballot to preside at said meeting.

Then, to act by secret ballot on Articles 2 through 31 as set out below, the polling hours
therefor to be from eight o'clock in the morning until eight o'clock in the evening, said
articles being the following:

Article 2: Shall the Town fix the date the first half of FY 2025-2026 annual taxes to be due and
collectible on or before October 15, 2025, and the second half of taxes to be due on or before May 15,
2026, pursuant to 36 M.R.S.A., Section 505(2)? Yes 221 No 39

Article 3: Shall the Town set a rate of 7.5% interest to be charged on taxes unpaid after approved due
dates, pursuant to 36 M.R.S.A., Section 505(4)? Yes 180 No 81

Article 4: Shall the Town set the interest rate to be paid by the Town on collected then abated taxes
at a rate that is 4% less that the rate charged for unpaid taxes for FY 2025-2026, pursuant to 36
M.R.S.A., Section 506(A)? Yes 191 No 66

Article 5: Shall the Town authorize the Tax Collector or Treasurer to accept prepayments of taxes not
yet committed, and pay no interest thereon, pursuant to 36 M.R.S.A., Section 506? Yes 215 No 44

Article 6: Shall the Town authorize the Town Council to approve any necessary abatement amounts
from Overlay and to accept any Supplemental taxes owed for FY 2025-2026? Yes192 No 64

Article 7: Shall the Town apply $811,945 of anticipated general revenues (excluding Homestead and
BETE Reimbursement) to the overall approved town budget for FY 2025-2026?
(Council and Budget Committee Recommend “Yes”) Yes 223 No 38
