Town of Naples

Special Town Meeting Minutes

Date: September 28, 2026
Time: 6:00pm
Location: Naples Town Office

ARTICLE 1

To elect a Moderator to preside over said meeting.

Ted Shane nominated Roy Gedat to serve as Moderator. The motion was seconded by William
Adams.
The Select Board voted 5—0 in favor of the nomination.

Jessie Pratt, Town Clerk, administered the oath of office to Roy Gedat, who then assumed the
chair and presided over the remainder of the meeting.

ARTICLE 2

To see if the Town will vote to adopt the Moderator’s Manual, as prepared by the Maine
Municipal Association, as the parliamentary procedures governing this meeting.

Ted Shane made a motion to accept with a second from Kevin Rogers.

The vote was unanimous.

ARTICLE 3

To see if the Town will vote to approve a proposed amendment to the Naples Land use
Ordinance, specifically Section 504.3.d.v & vi., by increasing the maximum building height
from 35' to 40' in the Commercial Zoning District

Jim Gratello made a motion to approve Article 3. The motion was seconded by Kevin Rogers.
The vote was taken, and the Moderator declared the motion passed by unanimous vote, with no
opposition.
