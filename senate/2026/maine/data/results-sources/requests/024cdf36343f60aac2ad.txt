City Clerk 




 

 




The City Clerk serves in a number of areas:
Clerk to the City Council, preparing agendas and minutes
Keeper of records, including vital records such as birth certificates, marriage certificates, and death certificates
Supervisor of elections within the City of Brewer
Agent of the City Council responsible for issuing business licenses
