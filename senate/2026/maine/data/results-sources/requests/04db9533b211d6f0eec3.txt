Clerk’s Office
The Blue Hill Clerk’s Office provides a wide range of services, many of which are available only to Blue Hill residents. We strongly encourage you to read the page on your transaction before coming to the Town Hall to make sure that you are eligible and that you have everything you need. If you have any questions, please call the Town Office or email the Town Clerk or a Deputy Clerk.

Payment Options
Cash
If you need us to break a $100 bill, be prepared for a short wait
Check or money order
Made out to “Town of Blue Hill”
Please make sure that your phone number is on the check/money order
If you make a mistake while writing your check, please initial beside the correction
Returned check fee is $35
Credit or debit card
Adds a 2.5% portal charge (minimum $1)
We do not have tap-to-pay
Apple Cards do not work with our payment processing system
Can be taken by phone with some transactions
Proof of Residency Options
All options must show your physical address, not a PO box!
Driver’s license or State ID
Lease or real estate purchase agreement
Utility or tuition bill
Bank statement
Current hunting or fishing license
Voter registration (we can look it up)
Payroll or government check
Rent or mortgage payment receipt
Real estate tax or sewer bill (we can look it up)
Income tax return or W2
Vehicle registration (we can look it up by plate #)
Receipt of public benefits
Official mail with USPS postmark
Even if you split time between multiple addresses, by law, you must have only one legal domicile. Generally, this will be where your driver’s license was issued and where you are registered to vote. If that location is not in Blue Hill, then we cannot perform resident-only transactions for you, even if you have a secondary residence here.
