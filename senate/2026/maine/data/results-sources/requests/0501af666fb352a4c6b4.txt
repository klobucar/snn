Voter Information
Polling Location: Jonesport Town Office, 70 Snare Creek Lane, Jonesport
Municipal Clerk & Registrar: Tonia Merchant (Jonesport Town Office)
Jonesport Voting Districts
Ward: 1
Precinct: 1
Congressional District: 2
State Senate: 6
State Representative: 138
County Commissioner: 3
Voter Registration: To register, you must be a United States citizen, at least 17 years of age, and have established a fixed principal home in Maine. To vote in a Referendum or General Election, you must be registered in the community where you reside, and be at least 18 years of age. Maine Voter Registration Applications are available at the Jonesport Town Office.
For more information visit the Maine Government resources below.
