Town Clerk / Treasurer's Office



 
 

The Town Clerk's Office is the official repository of town records and provides the following services:



• Registrar of Voters
• Vital Records
     o Birth
     o Death
     o Marriage
• Board of Selectmen Minutes
• Planning Board Minutes
• Appeals Board Minutes
• Cemetery Deeds, Burials
• Town Codes and Ordinances
• Hunting and Fishing License
• Dog License
• Genealogy Research
• Notary Public
