Overview
The City Clerk's Office serves as the records keeper for the municipality. This office is typically the first point of contact for residents seeking information or municipal services. The clerk's office is currently able to provide the following services: 
Business Licenses 
Dog Licenses
Elections
Event Permits
Notary
Records Access & Requests (Freedom of Access Act)
Records Management 
Vital Records (birth, marriage, death, & burial records)
Voter Registration
Please contact the Finance Department for the following services:
Hunting & Fishing Licenses
Motor Vehicles  
Tax payments
