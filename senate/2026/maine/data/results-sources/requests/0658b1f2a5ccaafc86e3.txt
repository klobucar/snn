The next election will be November 3, 2026. This will include the State General Election, Town Council and Referendum Questions, and RSU Board of Directors. There will be  more information as we get closer and it will posted on the Elections page when available.
If you have to licensed your dog for 2026, please do so. A $25.00 late fee per dog began on February 1st.
 The town clerk handles the following: 
Dog Licensing
Hunting / Fishing Licensing
Business Licenses & Permits
Elections
Notary and Dedimus Service
Record the Minutes at all Council Meetings
Vital Records
Assist Tax Collection/Auto Registration in Collecting Property Taxes
Assist Tax Collection/Auto Registration in Registering Autos, Boats, and ATVs
Marriage Licenses:  Marriage Licenses are issued between the hours of 8:00 - 3:00.  Please allow 30 minutes for processing. Printing off the Intention form and filling it out ahead will save time.
Welcome Packets are available in the Clerk's Office
Mission Statement
The clerk is considered the keeper of the town's records. The Municipal Clerk's Office provides excellent customer service and serves the public in a courteous and friendly manner. We give support to the Town Council and the citizens of the Town of Windham.
We take pride in the way we supervise all our elections. Voter registration is handled in accordance with Maine Law and the Town Charter. If we do not know an answer to a question, we will find the answer or find someone who can help.
