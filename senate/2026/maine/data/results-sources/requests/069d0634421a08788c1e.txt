Voter Registration
Who can register and vote in Maine?
To register, you must be a United States citizen, at least 17 years of age, and have established a fixed principal home in Maine. 
How do I register to vote?  
You fill out a voter registration card. See the sample card and instructions at:  https://www.maine.gov/sos/cec/elec/voter-info/voterguide.html
Where do I go to register?  
You can register to vote at the Plantation office, online at https://registertovote.sos.maine.gov/ , through any Motor Vehicle branch office, in most state & federal social service agencies, or at voter registration drives.
