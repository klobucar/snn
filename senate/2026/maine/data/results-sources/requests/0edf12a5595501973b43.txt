The deadline to order absentee ballots is Thursday, October 29, 2026, by 4:00 pm.
Order an absentee ballot online: Absentee Ballot Request Service
Print an absentee ballot application and mail it, deliver it to City Hall, fax it to 207-777-4621, or email it to cityclerkoffice@lewistonmaine.gov

Accessible Absentee Ballot request
Voters wishing to cast an absentee ballot may do so in person at the City Clerk's Office, 2nd floor, starting October 5, 2026, through Thursday, October 29, 2026.
Early Voting Polling Place in the City Council Chambers, 1st floor, starting Tuesday, October 13, 2026, through Thursday, October 29, 2026, Monday - Thursday from 9 am to 4 pm.  Extended hours on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, until 7 pm.

The Election Cycle
The municipal election is held on the Tuesday following the first Monday in the month of November. The Mayor and City Council, and school board members are elected for two-year terms. 
Implementation Schedule to Transition to Staggered Terms for Elected Officials
The State of Maine general and referendum elections are held annually on the Tuesday following the first Monday in November. The primary election is held on the second Tuesday of June of each general election year.
Enrolling in a Political Party
Currently, there are four official political parties within the State of Maine: Democratic, Green-Independent, Libertarian, and Republican. Registered voters may enroll in one of these political parties. However, voters who wish to register to vote do not have to select a political party. Voters can be registered to vote, but do not have to be enrolled in a political party.
The employees in the Lewiston City Clerk's Office cannot answer questions for citizens regarding the purposes and objectives of the various political parties in Maine. Lewiston voters with questions regarding the activities of the local political parties are encouraged to contact their local party chairperson or explore the following links for additional information about each political party:

DISCLAIMERS
Web Content Accessibility: The City of Lewiston is committed to providing access for all individuals of differing abilities who seek information on its site. Should a visitor have difficulty accessing information on the Lewiston site, particularly if using assistive technology, please contact mis@lewistonmaine.gov.  
Translations:  The City of Lewiston uses Google to translate its City web content. Accuracy is not guaranteed.
Municipal Invoice Scams: The City of Lewiston has been made aware of fraudulent invoices that closely mimic official City of Lewiston correspondence. These fraudulent emails often include invoices with the City seal and staff names and ask recipients to pay for the invoice. Residents and businesses are advised that legitimate City correspondence will only originate from an official “.gov” email address. If you receive a suspicious invoice or communication, do not respond or remit payment and contact the City staffer or mis@lewistonmaine.gov directly to verify its authenticity.
Legal Notices: To search a statewide database of public notices and legal advertisements published in Maine newspapers, please visit Maine Public Notices or by clicking this link. On this site, you can search through a database of statewide public notices and legal ads at no cost.
