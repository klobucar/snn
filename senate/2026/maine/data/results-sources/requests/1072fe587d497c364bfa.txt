Special Town Meeting Secret Ballot Referendum and State Candidate and Referendum Election 




 

 




When: November 3, 2026
Time: 6 AM–8 PM
Where: Edward C. Winston Town Hall Auditorium; 1 Summer Street, Kennebunk
Election Results:  Election Results will be posted here (PDF) when polls have closed and results have been tabulated.
Voter Guide: The Voter Guide is now available here. You can also pick one up in our office during early voting. 
Absentee Ballots
We always encourage absentee/early voting. Absentee ballots will be available Monday, October 5, 2026 for the November 3, 2026 election. You can request a ballot to be mailed to you, pick-up your absentee ballot, or vote early in one of the following ways:
Online: Complete a request form here. 
Call: Request ballots over the phone by calling 207-604-1326.
In-Person Pick-Up: Visit the Town Clerk’s Office and take ballots home.
In-Person Voting: Visit the Town Clerk’s Office once absentee ballots are available.
For more information, see our Absentee Voting page.
What ballots will I receive? 
State Candidate Referendum
Local Referendum Ballot
Additional Hours for Registering to Vote and Voting Absentee
In addition to regular office hours Monday through Friday 8 AM–4 PM, the Town Clerk’s Office will also be open for Early Absentee Voting on:
Saturday, October 24th: 10 AM–4 PM
Sunday, October 25th: 10 AM–4 PM
FINAL DAY TO VOTE ABSENTEE: Thursday, October 29th: 8AM–7 PM
Absentee Ballot Drop Box
The Absentee Ballot Drop Box will be available starting on Monday, October 5, 2026. This is a secure 24/7 opportunity to submit your ballot and requires no postage.
