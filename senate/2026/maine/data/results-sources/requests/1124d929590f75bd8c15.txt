Ranked-Choice Voting Resources 




 

 




The Department of the Secretary of State has unveiled a new section on their website offering several informational resources for voters who are seeking to educate themselves about ranked-choice voting (RCV). On June 12, 2018, this method of voting will be used for the first time ever in a statewide election as voters cast their ballots in the Primary Election.
 Available on the RCV Resources page are: 
 Sample ballots for all ranked-choice voting races  (Only races with three or more candidates will employ the ranked-choice voting process, which requires that one candidate receive a majority of the vote)
 Frequently Asked Questions 
RCV  Fact Sheet (PDF)  for voters to review and share with others in preparation for marking their ballot
 Rules for RCV (PDF) 
For more information about ranked-choice voting, please visit the Department of the Secretary of State website.
