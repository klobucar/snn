This page is updated with new information before and following the election. Once available, election results are posted below. You can sign up to receive an email alert when updates are made and election results are posted.

Polling Information
There will be a State and Municipal General and Referendum Election on November 5, 2024. Polls will be open 7 AM - 8 PM. Voters can also vote absentee (learn more about absentee voting below). Find your polling location.
Absentee Voting
You can vote in person or pick up a ballot at City Hall, or request that one be sent to your home online or by calling the Clerk's office at (207) 767-3201 . Voters may vote absentee in person at City Hall during regular office hours Monday-Thursday starting October 1, 2024. The last day to vote absentee without cause at City Hall, or request or pick up your absentee ballot, is October 31, 2024 at 5 PM. All absentee ballots must be returned by November 5, 2024 at 8 PM (election day). Get more absentee voting information.
Extended Hours for Election Services
The City Clerk’s Office will offer extended hours for election services only on:
Saturday, October 19th 8AM-12PM
Saturday, October 26th 8AM-12PM
Tuesday, October 29th open until 7PM
Wednesday, October 30th open until 7PM
Voter Registration
Register to vote online (through October 15, 2024), at the City Clerk's office, or at your voting place on election day. Get more voter registration information.

School Board – District Five (3 year term) and the Portland Water District Trustee (5 year term) for Cape Elizabeth/South Portland are OPEN.  Since there is no candidate named on the ballot, write-in candidates shall be named by voters. Pursuant to  M.R.S.A Title 30-A, Chapter 3 - City Election § 2554(2)  “a voter may write in the name and municipality of residence of any person for whom the voter desires to vote in the blank space provided.”
