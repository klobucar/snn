Find the answers you need!
Can I renew my vehicle registration online? 
Yes, please select the online services app on our home page and select vehicle registration renewal. Please be aware that you will be charged a 2.5% fee to pay by card. 
Can I pay my property taxes online? 
Yes, you can! Please be aware that you will be charged a 2.5% fee to pay by card. Follow this link to pay your property taxes online. 
When do dog licenses become available? 
Dog licenses become available October 15th and you must present a current rabies certificate. You have until December 31st to license your dog before a late fee goes in to affect. January 1st a late fee of $25.00 will be applied.
