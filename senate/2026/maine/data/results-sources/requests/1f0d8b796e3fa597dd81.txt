Elections
State Elections
First Tuesday of every November.
Primary Elections
Even years on the 2nd Tuesday of June.
 
Referendum Elections
held with General/Primary Elections
 
 
West Bath Municipal Elections are held with November's election. 
The Town may call a Special Town Meeting for any date.
 
Active Voter Party Affiliation as of November 7, 2023 (Total Registered Voters: 1836)
Democrat: 607 • Republican: 517 • Green Independent: 90 • Libertarian: 2 • No Labels: 23
Voters NOT enrolled in a party: 597

 
Who can register and vote in Maine? To register, you must be a United States citizen, at least 17 years of age, and have established a fixed principal home in Maine.
 
How do I register to vote? Contact the Town Clerk/Registrar of Voters at (207) 443-4342 or townclerk@westbathmaine.gov for a Voter Registration Card, or stop by Town Hall with identification and proof of residency. You may register to vote at the polls on election day - please bring identification and proof of residency.
To vote in a Referendum or General Election, you must be registered in the community where you reside, and be at least 18 years of age. A 17 year old may vote in a Primary Election, if that person will be 18 by the General Election. You may register to vote at the polls on election day with identification and proof of residency.

 

Is there a deadline for registering? Maine offers election day registration, which means you can register to vote at the polls. To register to vote by mail or through a voter registration drive, registration must be received by close of business on the 21st day before the election.To change your political party you must enroll at least 15 days prior to the primary election to participate.
