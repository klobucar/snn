Town Clerk
The Town Clerk's Office serves as the first line of contact for Milford residents and business. 
Here are some of the functions of Clerk's office: Town Clerk, Tax Collector/Treasurer, Sewer Billing/Collections, Milford Cemetery, Records Management, FOAA, and General Assistance. The Town Clerk and Deputies are certified as a Notary Public. 
The Town Clerk serves as the Bureau of Motor Vehicles and Department of Inland Fisheries & Wildlife Agent by registering vehicles, issuing plates and collecting the sales tax owed to the state for newly purchased vehicles and recreational units.
