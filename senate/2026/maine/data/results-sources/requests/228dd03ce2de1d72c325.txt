Register to Vote
You may register to vote now through October 13 @ 5pm prior to the November 3 Election. You may also register on Election Day. Click the following link for more information:
https://voteclock.org/maine
Absentee Ballots Applications Available
Early voting begins October 5, 2026. Absentee ballots may be requested online @ https://absenteeballotrequest.sos.maine.gov/AbsenteeBallotMaine/Request or in person at the Town Office. The last day to vote early or to request and absentee ballot will be Thursday, October 29. Your absentee ballot can be returned via mail or at the Town Office through Election Day.
