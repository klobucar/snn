Registrar of Voters
Upcoming Election
When: Tuesday, November 3rd, 2026
            8:00 am - 8:00 pm
Where: Phippsburg Town Hall
             1042 Main Road
 Absentee Voting:
Voters may request an absentee ballot online or in person at Town Hall. 
Deadline to request Absentee Ballot: For the November 3, 2026 General Election, the deadline for a voter to request an absentee ballot, including a ballot voted in the presence of the clerk, is Thursday, October 29th unless the voter completes a special circumstances application. The Secretary of State will provide the special circumstances application and instructions to municipal election officials in a future mailing.
 
For more information regarding elections, please visit The Department of the Secretary of State 's website 
 
Registering to Vote:
 
Residents may register to vote online, in person, or by mail.
 
Registering in person: Close of the polls on Election Day.
Registering by mail or 3rd person: Received by the voter’s town or city by close of business 21 days before Election Day. (For June Primary: May 19, 2026; for November General Election: October 13, 2026) 
Registering online: 5 PM on the 21st day before Election Day. (For June Primary: May 19, 2026; for November General Election: October 13, 2026)
Registration through the BMV: On the 7th day before Election Day. (For June Primary: June 2, 2026; for November General Election: October 27, 2026)
UOCAVA voters in Title 21-A section 777-A by mail or electronically: By Noon the day before Election Day (For June Primary: June 8, 2026; for November General Election: November 2, 2026)
 


 

Bureau of Corporations, Elections & Commissions (maine.gov)
