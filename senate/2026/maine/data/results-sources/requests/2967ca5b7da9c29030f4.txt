City Clerk's Office
Lisa J Gilliam, City Clerk
Office located on the main level at City Hall
1 Winthrop Street
Phone: (207) 623-4021 x 200
Email: cityclerk@hallowellmaine.org 

Daniel Kelley, Deputy City Clerk
Phone: (207) 623-4021 ext 204
Email: deputyclerk@hallowellmaine.org   

 

The City Clerk's Office is the official keeper of records for the City, coordinates Elections, and provides the following services:


Registrar of Voters 


Vital Records: Birth, Death, Marriage 


City Council Minutes 


Planning Board Minutes 


Administrative support for Code Enforcement Officer and Police Department


Cemetery Deeds, Burials & Information 


City Codes and Ordinances 


Hunting & Fishing Licenses 


Dog Licenses 


Community Gardens 


Genealogy Research 


Tax Clubs 


Notary Public 


Business Licenses: Victualer, Liquor, Innkeeper, Special Amusement, Vending Machines, Sellers Licenses at No Fixed Location, and Miscellaneous Licenses 


And many other services


The City Clerk serves as the agent for the following State of Maine Departments:


Inland Fisheries and Wildlife 


Human Services Vital Records 


Animal Welfare 


**********
DO YOU OWN A BUSINESS IN TOWN?
If you have a corporation and file with the Secretary of State, 
this does NOT apply to you.
If you are a Sole Proprietor and doing business under an assumed name, State law requires a one-time registration for $10.  You can fill it out and have it notarized at City Hall.  The purpose is to enable the public and consumers to know with whom they are dealing or the true identity of the person behind the business.

Dog Licenses
Dogs 6 months and older need to be registered each year by January 1st:
$11.00 - male/female
$6.00 neutered/spayed
After February 1 a late fee of $25 per dog is charged for license renewals.
