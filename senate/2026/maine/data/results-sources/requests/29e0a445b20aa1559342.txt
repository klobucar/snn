Town Clerk
This is the Town Clerk’s page for the Town of Hancock. The Town Clerk is responsible for transactions such as: Motor vehicles, Dog licenses, Hunting/Fishing licenses, ATV, Snowmobile, and Boat registrations, Documented boat excise taxes, tax payments, lien discharge payments, trash stickers, notary services, website maintenance, Elections and more.
You can contact the Clerk at hancocktownclerk@hancocktownoffice.com.
