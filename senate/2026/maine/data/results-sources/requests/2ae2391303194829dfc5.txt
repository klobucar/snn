Election Information
 Welcome to the Town of Harrison’s Election Information page. Below you’ll find key dates, deadlines, and important details for state and local elections. All voting is conducted at the Town Office, unless otherwise noted.

 Upcoming Elections:
November 3, 2026 - State General Election & MSAD 17 Referendum - Voting Hours: 8 am to 8 pm - Town Office (20 Front St.) | Late voting hours: 4:00 pm to 7:00 pm - Town Office (20 Front St.)
Absentee Ballots will be available Oct. 5, 2026, to pick up or vote in person.  You can request them to be mailed now by filling out an application (linked here) or calling the office 207-583-2241 option 1.
Absentee Ballots can also be requested online through the State of Maine Online Absentee Ballot Request & Tracking Service (linked here).
 




 Additional Resources

Maine Citizens Guide to the Referendum Election
State of Maine Voter Guide
Secretary of State – Bureau of Elections (State Elections)


Please Note:
On election days, the Clerk’s Office may only be open for voting services only and regular operations would resume the following business day. Please pay attention to our sign out front, website or the weekly newsletter prior to election day for status. 
If you need assistance with non-election matters, please plan accordingly. We appreciate your understanding and cooperation.
