UPDATE REGARDING ABSENTEE BALLOT VOTING - As of close of business on Thursday, June 6, 2024 only voters with special qualifying circumstances will be able to request an absentee ballot during the three day period immediately preceding election day.
The deadline to request an absentee ballot, including a ballot voted in the presence of the clerk, is three business days prior to the election (In the Standish, this is usually close of business on the Thursday preceding the election this year it is June 6, 2024 @ 4:30 pm) unless the voter completes a special circumstances application, stating one of five allowable reasons for requesting an absentee ballot after this deadline. The five reasons are:
1) Unexpected absence from the municipality during the entire time the polls are open on Election Day;
2) Physical or mental disability;
3) Inability to travel to the polls if the voter is a resident of an island ward or precinct;
4) Incapacity or illness that has resulted in the voter being unable to leave home or a treatment facility; or
5) Being a caregiver who is unable to attend the polls due to the incapacity or illness of another person.
FMI - Call the Town Clerk's Office at: 642-4678-642-8996, 642-4713-642-4681 or 642-8841 - Monday through Thursday, 7:30 am to 4:30 pm.

Beginning on Monday, October 5, 2020 through Friday, October 30, 2020 you may vote by absentee ballot in person at the Town Clerk's Office, you can request one via the State website at https://www.maine.gov/sos/cec/elec/voter-information/absent.html, an absentee ballot can be mailed to you by calling the Town Clerks Office at 642-4681-642-8841, 642-4713 or 642-4678.
Check the status of your absentee ballot on the following State website: https://apps.web.maine.gov/cgi-bin/online/AbsenteeBallot/ballot_status.pl
NEW! Beginning October 2, 2020 an accessible absentee ballot is available to voters with disabilities:
https://www.maine.gov/sos/cec/elec/voter-information/accessiblevoting.html
Additional Hours for Absentee Voting and Voter Registration
The Town Clerks Office will be open additional hours to issue and receive absentee ballots and to register voters on:
Saturday, October 17th from 9 am to 12 pm.
Tuesday, October 27th from 7:30 am to 7 pm.
Wednesday, October 28th from 7:30 am - 7 pm.
Thursday, October 29th from 7:30 am to 5 pm.
Friday, October 30th from 9 am to 5 pm.
Returning your ballot:
If mailing back your ballot, the Post Office suggests mailing it no later than October 26, 2020 to ensure it is received in time.
Ballots can also be returned by depositing in the outside, secure Town Clerk's Office Drop Box located at the Standish Municipal Center, 175 Northeast Road adjacent to the main entrance, no postage is required. The box is accessible 24-hours a day until 8 pm on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3, 2020. Ballots are removed from the drop box throughout the day.
Ballots can also be hand-delivered to the Town Clerk's Office, Standish Municipal Center, 175 Northeast Road, Monday through Thursday from 7:30 am until 4:30 pm.
Ballots must be received no later than 8 pm on Election night Tuesday, November 3, 2020.

Standish Representation
The Town of Standish is divided into four areas for Council and Planning Board representation. Both of these boards consist of seven members; one member is elected from each of the town "areas" and three "at large" members are elected. Even though these members are "area representatives" the voters of the entire Town elect them.
Town Areas - Description of areas from Charter Sec. 201 Town Code
Voters of the Town of Standish also elect Standish representatives to Maine School Board Administration District 6 Board of Directors and Town Budget Committee. Both of these boards consist entirely of "at large" membership.
As of January 1, 2014 the Town of Standish has new State Representative and Senate Districts:
The Standish Senate District is 26
The Standish House Districts are 22 and 23
