Election Information 




 

 




Elections are held at Cumberland Town Hall in the Council Chambers. The polls are open from 6 am to 8 pm.
Town Election Schedule
Cumberland holds multiple elections a year. Below is a breakdown of what to expect:
March | Presidential Primaries
When: 1st Tuesday after the 1st Monday.
Frequency: Every 4 years (2028, 2032, 2036).
Purpose: Nominating candidates for the U.S. Presidency.
June | Municipal & School Elections
When: 2nd Tuesday.
Frequency: Every year.
Purpose: Annual Town Council elections, MSAD (School District) budget votes, and local referendums. Even years also include State Primaries.
November | General Elections
When: 1st Tuesday after the 1st Monday.
Frequency: Every year.
Purpose:
Even Years: General elections for President or Governor, Congress, and State Legislature.
Odd Years: State Referendums, Bond issues, and Constitutional Amendments.
If you have any questions about an upcoming election, please email the Town Clerk, Jennifer Doten.
