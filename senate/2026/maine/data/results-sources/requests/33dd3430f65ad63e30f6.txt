Etna is a small, rural town located in Penobscot County with an area of approximately 25 square miles and a population of approximately 1,200 residents. Etna is fast growing in popularity for a place to live or start a small business. Our community is the perfect place to relax and enjoy the comforts of small town living and enjoying ‘The Way Life Should Be’ in Maine.
