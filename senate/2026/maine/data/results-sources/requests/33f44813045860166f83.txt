November 3, 2026 State & Municipal General Election
Elections
Posted on March 04, 2026 | Last Updated on October 06, 2026
Election Information
This page is updated with new information before and following the election. Once available, election results are posted below.  You can sign up to receive an email alert when updates are made and election results are posted. 
Important Deadlines
If you are holding a voter registration drive or mailing in a voter registration application card, we must receive applications 21 days before Election Day.
If you are wanting to change party enrollment or withdraw from a party before an election, you must do so 15 days before Election Day. 
Absentee Voting
You can request a ballot in person at City Hall, by mailing a request form, or request that one be sent to your home online or by calling the Clerk's office at (207) 767-3201.
Voters may vote absentee in person at the City Clerk's Office at City Hall during regular business hours Monday through Thursday starting in early October.  The last day to vote absentee without cause at City Hall, or request or pick up your absentee ballot, is October 29, 2026 at 5 PM.
Voters may return Absentee Ballots by mail, dropping it off at the City Clerk's Office during hours of operation, or utilize the drop box located outside City Hall. The drop box is monitored 24/7 by a security camera and ballots are removed by municipal clerks regularly to secure until they are counted for election day.   All absentee ballots must be returned by November 3, 2026 at 8 PM (election day). 
Extended Hours for Election Services
The City Clerk’s Office will offer extended hours for ELECTION SERVICES ONLY:

Saturday, October 24th 8AM-12PM
Monday, October 26th 7:30AM-6PM

Ballots and Polling Information
There will be a State & Municipal General Election on November 3, 2026 from 7 AM - 8 PM.  All polling places will be open.  Voters can find their polling location by visiting this page. 
All absentee ballots must be received by 8:00pm on Election Day.  Absentee ballots must be delivered to the City Clerk's office or dropbox at City Hall. 
The deadline to request an absentee ballot without special circumstances is the Thursday before Election Day. More Information on absentee voting can be found here.

Register to vote online, at the City Clerk's office, or at your voting place on election day. Get more voter registration information.
