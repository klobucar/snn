General/Municipal Election - November 3, 2026 |  8 AM - 8 PM | Marshwood High School
The General Election is held nationally on the first Tuesday following the first Monday in November.  In 2026, Maine voters will select their choice for Governor, Maine’s Representatives to Congress, members of the Maine Legislature, and certain county officers.  Nonparty candidates gain access directly to the General Election ballot by gathering a specified number of signatures on nonparty nomination petitions between January 1, 2026 and June 1, 2026 and must submit these petitions to the Division of Elections by 5:00 pm on June 1, 2026. 
To learn more about the General Election, please visit the Maine Secretary of State's website here.
Past Election Information
Election Results Update - June 18, 2026
The Town of South Berwick's June 9, 2026 State Primary Election results have been confirmed accurate following the Maine Department of the Secretary of State's standard reconciliation review, after officials identified and resolved a paperwork discrepancy in the town's tabulator reporting.
As part of the State's centralized ranked-choice tabulation process for the June 9, 2026 Primary Election, state election officials in Augusta spend days verifying municipal data from communities across the state of Maine. On Wednesday, Day 4 of this statewide data reconciliation process, state officials identified a numerical discrepancy on South Berwick’s specific reporting materials. The variance occurred between the total ballots printed on the local tabulator result tape and the totals recorded on the accompanying electronic memory drive.
For full details, please read the Town of South Berwick's latest press release here.

June 9, 2026 State Primary & MSAD 35 School Budget Referendum Election
The State Primary and School Budget Validation Referendum Election took place on Tuesday, June 9, 2026 from 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM at Marshwood High School, located at 260 Dow Hwy in South Berwick.
Primary Elections determine each qualified party’s nomination of candidates for federal offices and State and County offices.  Party candidates who are elected at the Primary qualify to appear on the November General Election ballot.
Offices included in the 2026 Primary Election are US Senator, Representative to Congress (both districts), Governor, all 35 State Senate districts, all 151 State Representative districts, and the following county offices, which vary depending on the county: Judge of Probate, Register of Probate, County Treasurer, Register of Deeds, Sheriff, District Attorney and County Commissioner.
For results from the June, 9 2026 Election, please see the links above.

Absentee Ballots
Can't make it to the polls on Election Day? No problem. Request an Absentee Ballot using the State of Maine Online Absentee Ballot Request Service website or visit us in the office. Any registered Maine voter can request an absentee ballot — no excuse or reason is required.
How to request your absentee ballot:
Online (easiest and fastest): Use the Maine Secretary of State's free online absentee ballot request service at absenteeballotrequest.sos.maine.gov. Your ballot will be mailed to you.
By Phone: Call the South Berwick Town Clerk's Office at (207) 384-3300, Option 6
By Email: Contact the Town Clerk's Office at clerksoffice1@sbmaine.us
In Person: Visit the Town Clerk's Office at 180 Main Street during office hours (Monday–Thursday 9 AM–5 PM, with extended hours until 6 PM on Thursdays; closed Fridays).
