FAQ
How Do I Register to Vote?
Come to the Town Office and fill out a voter registration card. You can also register to vote on election day at the polls. 
If you are a first time Maine voter, proof of identity will be required as well as proof of Wayne residency. 
Types of acceptable proof include a Maine Driver’s License, Maine State ID, (or out of state photo driver’s license for ID purposes) mail addressed to actual residence (no P.O. boxes) or a utility bill with service address shown. 
If you do not have proof of residence contact the Registrar of Voters at the Wayne Town Office at 207-685-4983.
How Do I Vote Absentee?
Any registered Maine voter may vote absentee. An absentee ballot without explanation can be requested until the “close-of-business” on the Thursday prior to an election. After that, one will have to have a “Special Circumstance” to request an absentee ballot.
An absentee ballot can be obtained in person, by mail, phone, immediate family member, third party or by electronic means. 
In-person: Wayne Town Office, 14 Kent Hill Road, Wayne, ME 0284.
By Mail: Wayne Town Office, Attention: Registrar of Voters, 14 Kent Hill Road, Wayne, ME 0284.
By Telephone: (207) 685-4983. (Note: You may only request your own absentee ballot by phone, each family member must request their own ballot.)
Online: Maine Absentee Ballot Request Service. For more information, please visit the Maine Secretary of State’s website at: www.maine.gov/sos, then click on Maine Absentee Ballot Request Service to request an absentee ballot.
If the ballot is to be taken out of the Town Office an Absentee Application will need to be filled out.
Where Do I Vote?
Voting on Election Day is conducted at the Ladd Recreation Center located at 26 Gott Road, Wayne, ME 04284. 
Elections are normally held on Tuesday’s and notices are posted at the Town Office and one other location at least seven days before the election. 
Normal election days in Maine are the Tuesday after the first Monday of June and the Tuesday after the first Monday of November. 
The polls open from 8:00AM to 8:00PM in the Town of Wayne. 
Who Should I Contact With Further Questions?
Please call the Registrar of Voters at the Wayne Town Office at 207-685-4983.
