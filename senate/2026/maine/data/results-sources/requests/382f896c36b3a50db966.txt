Tax Bills 2026-27
3 days ago
1 min read
Real estate and personal property tax bills were mailed on October 6, 2026. 
The tax comittment books and tax bills are available on the homepage under tax information. 
2026 Tax Information 
- Fiscal year: July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027
- Tax Rate: $12.10 per $1,000 (same as 2025)
- Due Dates: November 4, 2026 and March 31, 2027
- Interest Rate: 7.0%
- Commitment Date: October 5, 2026
- Certified Ratio: 100%
