Elections
If you need to register to vote, change your address, or update your party affiliation, you can do so at the Town Office. Visit Maine’s Election page or the town’s website for more information https://www.maine.gov/sos/cec/elec/voter-info/votreg.html
POLLING LOCATION
Town Office

WE’RE LOOKING FOR POLL WORKERS
Every town and city in Maine needs citizens like you to help administer the election. Visit the Maine Election Worker Recruitment webpage for more information about the qualifications, duties and benefits of helping your town at the polls on Election Day. In order to have fair elections, we require voters from various political parties. If you’re interested in being a poll worker, please contact the Town Office.
