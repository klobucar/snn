Elections and Voter Registration
The Municipal and School Elections are held on the 2nd Tuesday in June. The School Budget vote and any state elections and/or state referendums are held on the same day.
 
The National and/or State General Election and referendum are held on the 1st Tuesday of November.
 
Elections are held at the New Gloucester Fire Station at 611 Lewiston Road from 6:00 am to 8:00 pm unless otherwise noticed.
 
Town Meeting is the first Monday in May and is held at the Memorial School at 7:00 pm.
 
One may register to vote at the Town Hall during normal business hours and at the polls at all elections.
 
Absentee ballots are available at the Town Hall at least a month before each election or referendum vote.
 
 
Select the Election tab on the main page sidebar for more information.
