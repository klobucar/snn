Town Clerk / Elections 




 

 




The Town Clerk's Office is the record-keeping department for the Town of Wiscasset. The office issues Birth, Marriage, Death, and all other official documents. Marriage licenses are by appointment only.
Application to be an Elections Clerk: Elections Clerk Application

Absentee ballots will be available Monday, October 5, 2026.
