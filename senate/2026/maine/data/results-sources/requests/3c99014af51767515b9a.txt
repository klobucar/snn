OCTOBER CLOSURES
The Town Office will be closing at 2:30 PM on Wednesday October 7, 2026 for staff professional development.  Regular office hours will resume on Thursday, October 8th at 7:30 AM.
The Town Office will be closed on Monday, October 12th, 2026. Regular business hours will resume on Tuesday, October 13th at 8:30am.









 

 




The Town Clerk's Office provides a wide range of services to the residents of the Town of Casco, including administering municipal elections and voter registration, maintaining vital records, issuing marriage licenses, hunting and fishing licenses and processing motor vehicle registrations.
Due to often lengthy transactions processed at the Town Clerk's Office, we ask that you visit the office before 3:30 p.m. to be sure that the office staff can best serve you to process the below transactions:
Due to often lengthy transactions processed at the Town Clerk's Office, we ask that you visit the office before 3:30 pm to be sure that the office staff can best serve you to process the below transactions:
New motor vehicle transactions;
Motor vehicle transactions that have moved into Casco, from a former state;
New Leased motor vehicle transactions;
New commercial motor vehicle transactions.
