Comprehensive Plan Committee Applications Now Being Accepted
10-16-2026
The Town Council is currently accepting applications for the newly created Comprehensive Plan Committee.  This ad-hoc committee has been tasked with reviewing and updating the current Comprehensive Plan.  Interested citizens can apply by filling out and returning the committee application and background check form to the clerk's window.  Deadline for applications is Tuesday October 27th.  The Council hopes to select applicants for the committee at their November 2nd meeting.
Click Here for committee application and background check forms.




















It's Time to Register Your Dog
10-15-2026
Dog Owners Must register their dogs by January 31st in order to avoid a $25 Late Fee per dog.
Register On-Line or at the Town Office.
