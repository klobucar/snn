Town Clerk 




 

 




The Town Clerk's responsibilities are defined by Elections Title 21-A and Municipal Title 30-A. In addition, the Town Clerk is appointed by the Bureau of Motor Vehicles and the Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife to act as the Sales Agent for the Town.
The Town Clerk is responsible for maintaining and recording all Municipal documents, vital records, board agendas, meeting minutes, FOIA requests, contracts, declarations, ordinances, and oaths of office.
