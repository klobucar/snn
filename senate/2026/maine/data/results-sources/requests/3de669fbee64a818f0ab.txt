Mission Statement:
The mission of the Town Clerk is to be the official keeper of records for the Town of Farmington.  The office is to accurately maintain all town records, conduct elections, provide State and local licenses to Town Officials and the public.
The Town Clerk is committed to achieving this mission through:

Serving the public in a courteous, friendly, professional and confidential manner while instilling excellent customer service
Staying current on laws governing the department’s areas of responsibilities
Preserving the town’s history and making it available to the public through sound records
Providing the department with the necessary knowledge through equipment and adequate training
