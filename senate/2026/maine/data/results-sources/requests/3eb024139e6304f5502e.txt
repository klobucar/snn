Register to Vote in Wells
If you are a resident of Wells, you may register to vote at the Town Clerk’s Office.
Where to Register
Town Clerk’s Office
208 Sanford Road, Wells, ME 04090
Registration Hours
Monday through Friday: 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM
First and third Tuesday of each month: until 6:00 PM
You may also register to vote on Election Day at your polling location.
What to Bring
To register, you will need:
Proof of identity (such as a driver’s license or other government-issued ID)
Proof of residency (such as a utility bill, lease, or other document showing your Wells address)
Ways to Register
In Person
Register at the Town Clerk’s Office during regular business hours.
By Mail
You may request a voter registration application and return it by mail.
Online
Maine offers online voter registration through the state system: Maine Secretary of State
Voter Registration Deadlines
Maine allows ongoing voter registration, including on Election Day. However, registering ahead of time is encouraged to make the voting process quicker and easier.
Need Help?
If you have questions about registering to vote, updating your information, or your voter status, contact the Town Clerk’s Office:
207-646-2882
Resources: 
Eligibility to Register and Vote in Maine
Maine Voter Rights
Maine Voter Information
Registrar of Voters
The Registrar of Voters maintains the Town’s official voter list and ensures compliance with Maine Revised Statutes Title 21-A.
The Registrar is appointed by the Board of Selectmen for a two-year term, with appointments made in January of each odd-numbered year.
