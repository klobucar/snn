Town Office News 
SPECIAL TOWN MEETING
WARRANT FOR SPECIAL TOWN MEETING
Androscoggin ss State of Maine
To: Glenn J. Holt, Citizen of the Town of Leeds in said County and said State.
Greetings:
In the name of the State of Maine, you are hereby required to notify and warn theInhabitants of said Town of Leeds, qualified by law to vote in Town affairs, to assemble at the Leeds Town Office in said Town on Wednesday, Oct. 7, 2026 at 6:00 pm.
ARTICLE 1: To choose a Moderator to preside at said meeting.
ARTICLE 2: To see if the citizens of Leeds will vote to authorize the Selectmen to: (i) sell all of the Town’s Leeds Broadband Network assets, including equipment, licenses, and related facilities, to the Biddeford Internet Corp. d/b/a Great Works Internet (“Purchaser”) according to the terms set forth in an Asset Purchase Agreement, allowing for such minor modifications to the terms prior to the closing that do not affect the purchase price or list of assets to be conveyed; and (ii) negotiate and execute a lease or comparable agreement respecting the Purchaser’s use of the existing central office building serving the Leeds Broadband Network on such terms as the Selectmen board deems in the Town’s best interest?
________________________________Dwight G. Buckley
________________________________ Jan B. Wiegman
________________________________David Barker

Leeds Historical Society
Our Mission
The Leeds Historical Society preserves historical records, landmarks and artifacts related to Leeds and promotes interest in the town's history.

New Items Available
Exciting news! The ladies from the Historical Society have dropped off some new merchandise. Stop by and check it out. There are bags, tee shirts, tumblers (which can be customized), and sweatshirts in many sizes.
