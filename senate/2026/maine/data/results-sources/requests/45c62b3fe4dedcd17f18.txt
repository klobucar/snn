Absentee Ballot Information 




 

 




Absentee Voting
Absentee ballots are available at the Town Clerk’s Office during regular business hours beginning 30 days prior to an election. Voters may also cast an absentee ballot in person at the Clerk’s Office.
If you would like to vote by absentee ballot but are unable to come to the Town Clerk’s Office during normal business hours to vote in person, contact the staff in the Clerk’s Office at 207-646-2882 to request a ballot by mail. 
The voter must call the office, as family members cannot request a ballot for another family member by telephone. 
Immediate family members can request an absentee ballot for another family member by completing an application form and filing it with the staff in the Town Clerk’s Office.
If you need any additional information regarding absentee voting please call the office at 207-646-2882.

Extended Hours
In addition to regular Town Hall business hours, the Town Clerk's Office will be open for absentee voting on Saturday, October 24 and Saturday, October 31 from 8 am - 12 pm. 








 




Returning Your Ballot
Completed absentee ballots must be returned by the deadline in the provided envelope to:
In person at the Town Clerk's Office during regular business hours
In the secure drop box, available at the rear entrance of Town Hall: 
208 Sanford Road, Wells, ME
Please only use the designated drop slot for ballots
By Mail 
208 Sanford Road, Wells, ME
