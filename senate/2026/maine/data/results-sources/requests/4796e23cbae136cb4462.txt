Town Clerk & Tax Collector
The Town Clerk/Deputy Tax Collector maintains all town records, conducts elections in accordance with state and federal laws, issues various licenses and registrations, and collects taxes. Additional duties include:

Voter registration.
Record and maintain all vital records (birth, marriage and death certificates).
Issue dog licenses.
Issues hunting and fishing licenses.
Provides notary services.
Registers motor vehicles, boats, all-terrain vehicles, snowmobiles and trailers.
Collects property and excise taxes.
Compiles the annual town report.


Disperses Transfer Station stickers

 

For tax information please send requests to sjones@monmouthmaine.gov.
