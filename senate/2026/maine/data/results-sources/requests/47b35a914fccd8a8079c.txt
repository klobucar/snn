2026 Tax Bills have been mailed!
 
If you have recently moved and haven't reminded me to update your address, please reach out and confirm I have the correct mailing address. 😀
 
You can receive a 2% discount on your taxes if paid in full by October 14th 2026. Interest starts accruing (7%) starting November 15th. We have committed our taxes at a mil rate of 19.6 😁 (that is just a .6 mil increase from 2025 taxes)
 
We do have a tax club option available to break up the tax amount into 8 monthly payments without interest. If you were in the tax club last year, your payment booklets were mailed with the bills. If you would like to be enrolled in the tax club, please let me know ASAP. Your account can not have a balance for a prior year to be enrolled in the tax club. The first Tax Club payment is due 10/14/2026.
Also, we are currently working on building back up some of the town roads, Monument road is being worked on this week, Estabrook road (From the sand shed to the Cary line) will be worked on in the near future. We are budgeting for building up Tracy road for next year.
I have fielded a lot of questions about the Estabrook road bridge, which is state owned and maintained. I received confirmation yesterday that the bridge will be inspected by the state this month, so when I hear any new information I will relay that to you all.
