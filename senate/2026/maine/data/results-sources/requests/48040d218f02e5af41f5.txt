The Town of Charlotte is currently accepting offers and inquiries regarding the sale or lease of the former Charlotte School building located at 1006 Ayers Junction Road. The building is 8,200 square feet and sits on a 3-acre parcel.

FOAA REQUESTSExpand
Town of Charlotte FOAA Request Policy
Print a request form HERE
Photocopies of documents will be subject to a fee of $0.10 per page for a standard 8.5" by 11" black and white page.
If your request is expected to exceed two hours of staff time, a fee of $20 per hour will be billed after the first two hours of staff time per request. A deposit for the estimated time in excess of two hours will be required. Any unused funds will be refunded. If the time frame exceeds the estimate, the information will not be released until the balance of the payment is received by the Town.
After submitting a request, you will be contacted to let you know when the has been completed. If your request has been denied, you will be notified in writing of the denial and reason for the denial. Denials may be appealed to the Maine Superior Court.
