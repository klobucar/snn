Welcome to Our Hometown: Bradford, Maine
Welcome to historic Bradford, Maine, founded in 1831 and nestled in the heart of Northern Maine!
Our community is small and close-knit, with a rich history and beautiful natural surroundings that make it a perfect place to call home. We take pride in our rural charm, our friendly neighbors, and our commitment to preserving the traditions that make our town so unique.
