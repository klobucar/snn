November 3, 2026, State of Maine General Election, Special Town Meeting
Voting place for Hollis:  Municipal Building - Meeting Room, 34 Town Farm Road, Hollis, ME 04042
Polls open at 7 AM and close at 8 PM. 
Absentee Ballots must be received by 8 PM on November 3, 2026--Drop off at the Absentee Ballot Box outside the Town Hall at 34 Town Farm Road, Hollis or bring directly to the polls.
Absentee ballots may be requested by telephone, 929-8552 ext. 27.  Please note you may only request your own absentee ballot over the phone. Absentee ballot request forms are also available at the Hollis Town Hall, 34 Town Farm Road, Hollis, Maine, 04042. Click here to request your absentee ballot on-line.
Please call early for your absentee ballots and allow 7 to 10 days for the mailing of your ballot.
Our absentee ballot drop box is located outside the Hollis Town Hall at 34 Town Farm Road, Hollis, Maine, 04042.
If you need assistance with voting, please call the Town Clerk's office, 929-8552 ext.27.
Please see the State of Maine Website for specific voting information on:
How to register to vote
How to request an absentee ballot
Candidate information, if applicable.
