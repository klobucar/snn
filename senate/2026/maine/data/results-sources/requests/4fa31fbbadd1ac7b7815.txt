MUNICIPAL ADMINISTRATION
The Patten town office is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday from 9:00am to noon and from 1:00pm to 4:30pm. It is open on Wednesdays by appointment, and closed weekends and specific dates noted on the town calendar. In the winter months, please call the office to confirm our hours.
