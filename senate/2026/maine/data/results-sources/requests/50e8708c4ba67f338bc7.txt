Elections & Voting 




 

 




State of Maine General Election
Date: Tuesday, November 3, 2026
Time: Polls open 7:00 AM - 8:00 PM (for this election only)
Location: Crooked River Elementary School, 1437 Poland Spring Road
Sample Ballot
Absentee Ballots
Absentee ballots will be available on Monday, October 5, 2026, for the November 3, 2026 election.  You can request a ballot be mailed to you, or pick up your absentee ballot in one of the following ways:
Online: Complete a request form online here.
Call: Request ballots over the phone by calling 207-627-4515.
In-Person Pick-Up: Visit the Town Clerk's Office and take ballots home.
In-Person Voting: Visit the Town Clerk's office once absentee ballots are available.
For more information, please see our Absentee Voting Page.
Additional Hours for Registering to Vote and Voting Absentee
In addition to our regular office hours, the Town Clerk's Office will be open late on October 29, 2026, from 5:00–7:00 PM.  These extended hours will be for the purpose of voter registration and absentee voting only.
Absentee Ballot Drop Box
The Absentee Ballot Drop Box will be available starting on Monday, October 5, 2026.  This is a secure, 24/7 opportunity to submit your ballot and requires no postage.
