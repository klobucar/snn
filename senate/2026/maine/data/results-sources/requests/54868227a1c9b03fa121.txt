Voting Information
Elections:

All Local, State & Federal Elections are administered and supervised by the Town Clerk. 
The Annual Municipal Election is held on the Tuesday in conjunction with State and Federal Elections. 
The Annual Municipal Election is for the purpose of electing Town Select Board Members, MSAD 28/CSD 19 School Board Directors, Budget and Library Committee members.
 
Absentee ballots and in person voting will be available starting Monday October 5th, ending Thursday October 29th at 3:30pm.
Ways to Vote by Absentee Ballot:
A voter may vote by absentee ballot in person at the Rockport Town Office, located at 101 Main St., during business hours. 
A voter may also call and request a ballot be sent by mail, before the deadline. Ballots are mailed on a daily basis. On behalf of the voter, an immediate family member may request in writing that a ballot be sent by mail or delivered to the voter by that family member. 
You can order online Absentee Ballot Request Service
Additional Resources:  Absentee Voting Guide
    
If you are not currently registered to vote in Rockport, you may come in person to the Rockport Town office with proof of residency and ID or you may register online using the link below to the Secretary of State website.
