Stow In Numbers
Stow, Maine, is a small rural town in Oxford County, nestled in the foothills of the White Mountain National Forest. Known for its scenic landscapes, including the Cold River and Bickford Slides, the town has a rich history rooted in logging and Native American heritage while offering a peaceful, nature-focused environment.

Meet Your Selectmen
The Stow Select Board serves as the town’s primary governing body, responsible for overseeing municipal operations, setting policy, and representing the interests of residents. The board meets regularly to discuss town matters, review budgets, and make important decisions that help shape Stow’s future. Residents are encouraged to attend meetings and stay involved in the community.

Real Estate Tax Notices
The 2026 tax commitment was signed on August 11, 2026. Tax bills will be sent out within the next week. This gives residents an additional window of almost 2 months to pay taxes for the 2026 season. The December 1st deadline remains the same. Friendly Reminder - Tax lien notices for unpaid 2025 taxes will be mailed out at the end of August. To avoid additional fees, please send these payments in as well. Please feel free to reach out to the town with any questions. Thank you!
