Elections

 

VOTER REGISTRATION 
Click HERE to complete a Voter Registration Card & bring or mail it to the Town Office at 33 Clarks Mills Road, Dayton, ME 04005.
 

ELECTIONS
State and Municipal Elections are held at the Town Office located at 33 Clarks Mills Road.  The Town of Dayton holds its Municipal Election on the 2nd Tuesday in June.  The Annual Town Meeting is held following the June election.  Please do not hesitate to contact the Town Office with any questions or concerns.
 

UPCOMING ELECTIONS:
11/03/2026: General Election from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm.

Absentee ballot applications will be available 90 days prior to the election date. Ballots will be mailed once received from the State of Maine.
For information on State General Election, visit Maine.gov or  click here. 
Absentee Ballot requests can be made by the following methods:
Request for Absentee Ballot Application Form
Request online at Maine.gov. Click Here
At the Town Hall in person
By phone (207)-499-7526 **
By mail to 33 Clarks Mills Rd, Dayton, ME 04005
**Phone requests must be by the voter only
 

 

 



Absentee ballots

APPLICATIONS FOR ABSENTEE BALLOTS
You may request an Absentee Ballot up to 90 days prior to Election Day. Your requested ballot will be mailed to you at an address you specify on the request form. Ballots are available to be mailed approximately 30 days prior to Election Day. If you would like to request an Absentee Ballot you may:
Call the Town Office at (207) 499-7526 to request one by phone
Come into the Town Office at 33 Clarks Mills Road, during regular office hours ·
State Election, go online to the State of Maine's Absentee Ballot Request Service (available 90 days prior to the election only) · Print the fillable Application for Absentee Ballot & return it by mail, drop box, or by dropping it at the Town Office in person.
 
VOTER REGISTRATION Click HERE to complete a Voter Registration Card & bring or mail it to the Town Office at 33 Clarks Mills Road, Dayton, ME 04005.
