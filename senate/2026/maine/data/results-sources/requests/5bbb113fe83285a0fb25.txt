EMS Resolution 2026 




 

 




Pursuant to State of Maine Law, 30-A M.R.S. § 3172, municipal officers of each municipality must adopt a plan stipulating the method by which transporting emergency services will be delivered within the Municipality.
The North Berwick Select Board as adopted the Service Model B which states that the medical services transport plan is provided via a formal agreement with the North Berwick Rescue Squad, Inc.
This was Adopted by the Select Board on August 4, 2026.
