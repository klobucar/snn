Town Clerk / Administration 




 

 




The Town of Fryeburg, Maine operates under the Town Meeting - Selectmen / Town Manager form of government. The Annual Town Meeting is now held in June. The Select Board are the policy making board, and appoint and work through the Town Manager who is charged with the day to day operation of the Town.
