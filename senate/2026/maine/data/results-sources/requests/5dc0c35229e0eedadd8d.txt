What We've Achieved





2025 Accomplishments
100+ Housing Units Advanced
Housing projects moved through planning, approval, or development stages throughout the city.
Major Zoning Modernization Completed
Revisions to Chapters 16 and 19 improved land use regulations and reduced barriers to development.
New City Website Launched
Expanded access to information, online services, and digital engagement tools.
Road and Sidewalk Improvements Continued
Investments funded through the voter-approved bond improved critical infrastructure throughout the community.
Statewide Recognition for Housing Leadership
Rockland received recognition through the GrowSmart Summit for its housing initiatives and innovative policy work.






Where We're Going





2026 Council Priorities
Housing & Livable Neighborhoods
Objective: Increase housing opportunities for residents of all income levels while supporting vibrant and livable neighborhoods.
Key Initiatives:
Implement recommendations from the Housing Plan
Support workforce and middle-income housing development
Continue zoning modernization efforts
Explore housing options for older adults
Maximize the impact of voter-approved housing investments
Why It Matters: A diverse housing supply supports economic growth, workforce retention, and long-term community stability.
Economic Growth & Vitality
Objective: Support local businesses, attract investment, and strengthen workforce opportunities.
Key Initiatives:
Develop a citywide economic development strategy
Support business attraction and retention
Strengthen partnerships with educational and workforce institutions
Encourage downtown and neighborhood investment
Why It Matters: A strong local economy creates jobs, expands the tax base, and supports community services.
Financial Sustainability & Property Tax Relief
Objective: Promote long-term fiscal sustainability while reducing pressure on property taxpayers.
Key Initiatives:
Diversify municipal revenue sources
Expand the commercial and residential tax base
Improve long-range financial planning
Align capital investments with fiscal priorities
Why It Matters: Financial stability allows the City to maintain services and invest responsibly.
Transportation, Mobility & Infrastructure
Objective: Improve how residents, workers, and visitors move throughout Rockland.
Key Initiatives:
Improve parking management
Expand pedestrian and bicycle infrastructure
Support transportation alternatives
Coordinate with regional transit providers and state agencies
Why It Matters: Reliable transportation supports economic activity, accessibility, and quality of life.
Communications & Public Engagement
Objective: Strengthen communication between residents and local government.
Key Initiatives:
Expand communication tools and outreach
Increase transparency around City initiatives
Create more opportunities for community engagement
Improve access to Councilors and city leadership
Why It Matters: An informed and engaged community leads to better decision-making.
Aging & Livable Community
Objective: Ensure Rockland remains a welcoming community for residents of all ages.
Key Initiatives:
Pursue Age-Friendly Community designation
Improve transportation options for older residents
Support age-friendly housing solutions
Explore opportunities for dedicated senior gathering space
Why It Matters: Residents should be able to remain active and connected throughout their lives.
Efficient, Innovative & Responsive Government
Objective: Deliver high-quality municipal services through innovation and continuous improvement.
Key Initiatives:
Improve technology and digital services
Enhance customer service and responsiveness
Improve operational efficiency
Pursue energy efficiency opportunities
Why It Matters: Efficient government improves service delivery while making the best use of taxpayer resources.
Infrastructure & Capital Planning
Objective: Plan proactively for future infrastructure investments and community needs.
Key Initiatives:
Update long-term capital plans
Identify future infrastructure needs
Pursue grants and alternative funding
Coordinate investments across departments
Why It Matters: Thoughtful planning reduces future costs and helps the City make strategic investments.
