Property Tax Information for FY2026 




 

 




The Fiscal Year runs from July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026.
The Tax Rate is established by the Select Board each year in August, this year that date was August 19, 2025.
The Tax Rate for Fiscal Year 2026 is $9.35
The tax bills were mailed shortly after the commitment date.
We offer two installment payments to taxpayers:
Taxes are due October 3, 2025 and May 1, 2026.
Interest will accrue on October 4, 2025 and May 2, 2026.
Pursuant to State Law, the Town does not pro rate taxes.
The Town sends out one Tax Bill per year, it is addressed to the owner of the property as of April 1, 2025. It is the current owner's responsibility to transfer the tax bill to the new owners upon sale of property. It is the property's owner responsibility to forward a copy of the bill to their mortgage company if taxes are escrowed.
All tax payments made on tax accounts are credited to the oldest taxes on the account.
Please contact the Town Office by phone at 207-676-3353 Opt. 1 if you need the status of your current tax bill. Tax status, along with valuation of each tax account, can also be found on the Town's Online Assessing Database.
Tax Payments may be mailed to:
Town of North Berwick
P.O. Box 422
North Berwick, Maine 03906
Payment can also be made online by credit card at town of north berwick There is a service fee for online credit card payments.
