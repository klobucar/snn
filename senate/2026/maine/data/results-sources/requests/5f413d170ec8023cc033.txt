Voter Registration
Welcome to the State of Maine Online Voter Registration Service
If you have a current valid Maine driver's license or Maine State ID, you must provide: ** 
Your Maine issued driver's license (DL) or State ID number 
Your legal name as it appears on your Maine DL or State ID card
** If you do not have a Maine driver's license or Maine State ID, you must provide: 
The last 4 digits of your Social Security Number
An image of your handwritten signature (not a signature created with a computer font). See instructions on how to save a picture of your signature to upload with your application.
This service is not currently available for Maine Voters.
Online voter registration is not available for the 21 days before a state or federal election, but you can still register to vote in person in your municipality. In-person voter registration is available up to and including Election Day. 
Download and complete the Maine Voter Registration Application. 
Then return it in-person with your proof of identity and residence to your municipal clerk or registrar. 
Don’t have a printer? Your municipal office will have a voter registration card for you to fill out in person. 
Still have questions about registering to vote in Maine? Visit our State of Maine Voter Guide for answers. 
This service is not currently available for UOCAVA Voters.
Online voter registration is not available for the 21 days before a state or federal election, but UOCAVA voters can still register to vote by sending in a completed voter registration form until noon on the Monday before Election Day.
Download and complete the Federal Post Card Application: Federal Post Card Application.
Then email it with your proof of identity and residence to the Maine Division of Elections at UOCAVA.CEC@maine.gov.
Still have questions about registering to vote in Maine? Visit our Uniformed Service & Overseas (UOCAVA) Voters page for answers.
How to Create a Picture of Your Signature to Upload
Use your computer and a scanner:
Write your signature in a normal size using black or blue ink on a blank, unlined, white piece of paper.
Scan the page and save the image on your computer as a .jpg file.
If necessary, crop the image using editing tools to eliminate most of the blank space around your signature. Make sure the image is clear and readable.
The size of the file must be no more than 5MB.
Use your smartphone or tablet:
Write your signature in a normal size using black or blue ink on a blank, unlined, white piece of paper.
Use the camera on your smartphone or tablet to take a picture of your signature. Make sure the picture is well lighted, clear, and readable.
If necessary, crop the image using editing tools to eliminate most of the blank space around your signature.
Save the picture as a .jpg file. The size of the file must be no more than 5MB.
