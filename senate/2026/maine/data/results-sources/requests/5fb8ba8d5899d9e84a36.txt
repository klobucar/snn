City Clerk












Kimberly Finnemore, City Clerk
kfinnemore@presqueisleme.us
Phone: (207) 800-3488 Select Option: 2
Fax:  (207)764-2521
The City Clerk is responsible for:
Processing vital records, which includes birth, death and marriage.
Processing various business licenses, (i.e. liquor, special amusement for music, dancing and entertainment)
Issuing dog licenses, hunting and fishing permits
Issuing ATV, boat and snowmobile registrations
Administering elections
Staff support for City Councilors and recording minutes of City Council meetings.
Registrar of Voters
Vital Records Request Forms:
Birth Certificate
Marriage Certificate
Death Certificate
City Clerk Fees
