Bowdoinham Holiday Festival December 4–6, 2026
A weekend of art, music, and cheer!
Festivities begin Friday, December 4, with coffee, pastries, and community spirit at the Breaking Bread Café, benefiting the Bowdoinham Food Pantry. The evening continues with Santa’s arrival and tree lighting at 5:30 p.m. at the Fire Station, complete with carols and cheer, followed by the Bowdoinham Guild of Artisans’ opening celebration at 6 p.m. with live music and food. 
Throughout the weekend, stroll through our festive village to discover local art at the Merrymeeting Arts Center Holiday Art Fair, the Community School Holiday Craft Fair, the Artistic Endeavors Studio Sale, and the Bowdoinham Guild of Artisans Holiday Sale—plus hands-on fun with a wreath-making workshop, cookie-decorating party, Victorian Christmas Open House, and the beloved Pony Parade!
Enjoy more merriment at the Makers Market at Dandelion Spring Farm starting at 11 am on November 28 and the Bowdoinham Community School Annual Craft Fair from 10 am - 3 pm on December 13. 
Explore times, locations and more details below! 
Dandelion Spring Farm Holiday Market
Saturday, November 28th, 11:00 am - 3:00 pm
The Hive at Dandelion Spring Farm, 961 Ridge Rd. 
Kick off the season at the maker's market with art, food, live music, and fireside gathering.
Breaking Bread Café 
Fridays, December 4th, 11th & 18th, 8:00 am – 10:00 am
Watersong Music, 9 Main St. 
Coffee, pastries and conversation. Proceeds benefit the Bowdoinham Food Pantry.
Bowdoinham Country Store
Friday, December 4th, 5:00 pm - 6:00 pm
Saturday, December 5th, 8:00 am - 7:00 pm
Sunday, December 6th, 8:00 am - 5:00 pm 
Bowdoinham Country Store, 54 River Road
On Friday, come visit the holiday hot chocolate station then grab a spot and wave hello as Santa rolls by on the fire truck! Then step into the magic of the season! Browse the collection of locally made products, handcrafted jewelry, ornaments, candles, unique gifts, holidays treats baked by Mom and more!
Tis’ the season for giving! Ask about their buddy board and make sure to check out the Bowdoinham Giving Tree! Plus, don’t forget to drop off your letter to Santa, they’ll have a special mailbox inside the store starting December 1st, with all letters post marked for the North Pole! Oh, and new merchandise will be rolling out on Saturday. Limited amount, while supplies last.
Santa Comes to Town and Christmas Tree Lighting
Friday, December 4th, 5:30 pm
Bowdoinham Fire Station, 57 Post Rd.
After a short ride around the village, the Fire Department will be bringing Santa to the Fire Station at 6:00 pm to light the Christmas Tree and sing our favorite carols.
Bowdoinham Guild of Artisans Show & Sale (2 locations) 
Friday, December 4th, 6:00 pm - 8:00 pm
Saturday, December 5th, 10:00 am - 3:00 pm
Sunday, December 6th, 10:00 am - 3:00 pm
The Bowdoinham Guild of Artisans will be at two locations— the renovated Town Hall and the Cathance River Gallery. This group of over twenty artisans will have works that include fiber arts, pottery, jewelry, woodworking, metal work, glass work, book art, fine art paintings. mosaics and more. 
Don’t miss these offerings at the Town Hall: Mocktails served on Friday night from 6:00 pm – 8:00 pm to benefit BCDI; Cafe offerings on Saturday from 10:00 am - noon to benefit the Bowdoinham Food Pantry and on Sunday 10:00 am to noon with proceeds going to the Bowdoinham Firefighters. Come out and support your local non-profits. 
Also, live music, including Ben Hunsberger on Friday night and Hal Ahlers and friends on Sunday, will be ongoing during the show. 
Merrymeeting Arts Center Holiday Craft Fair 
Friday, December 4th, 6:00 pm - 8:00 pm
Saturday, December 5th, 10:00 am - 3:00 pm
Merrymeeting Arts Center, 9 Main St.
Join us for MAC’s Annual Holiday Craft Fair, featuring a wonderful variety of local artists
and artisans showcasing their work inside the MAC Gallery.
Nazarene Church Victorian Christmas Open House
Friday, December 4th, 6:00 pm - 8:00 pm
Saturday, December 5th, 10:00 am - 3:00 pm
Sunday, December 6th, 12:00 pm - 3:00 pm 
Nazarene Church, 7 School St.
Enjoy wassail and cookies as you experience the sights and sounds of an old-fashioned Christmas. A reminder to relax and slow down during a hectic season.
Artistic Endeavors Studio Holiday Sale
Friday, December 4th, 6:00 pm - 8:00 pm
Saturday. December 5th, 10:00 am - 3:00 pm
Sunday, December 6th, 10:00 am - 3:00 pm
Artistic Endeavors Studio, 9 Main St.
Join Kathleen Goddu, Diane Friese and Sally Onstead at the Artistic Endeavors Gallery. There will be Indigo Shibori scarves, home decor items, jewelry from nature (pins, necklaces), greeting cards, miniature easels, wall hangings and mandalas.
Merryreading Books
Saturday, December 5th, 9:00 am – 4:00 pm
Merryreading Books, 27 Main Street
Give the gift of reading. Offering books under $5, local authors, Maine stories, kid-approved holiday favorites, cookbooks for holiday entertaining, and more. Proceeds support the Bowdoinham Public Library.
 
Downing Arts
Saturday, December 5th, 9:00 am - 4:00 pm
Longbranch School, 25 Main St. (Outdoors, weather permitting)
Nicholas Downing will be doing live blacksmithing demonstrations and selling wares ranging from fine jewelry to hand forged household items to artisan tools.
Bowdoinham Public Library Wreath Workshop 
Saturday, December 5th, 9:00 am - 3:00 pm
Merrymeeting Arts Center’s Studio, 6 Main St.
Come make your own Balsam wreath, $20 for small and $25 for a large. Supplies and instruction included. Proceeds benefit the library.
Bowdoinham Community School Holiday Craft Fair
Saturday, December 5th, 10:00 am - 3:00 pm
Bowdoinham Community School, 23 Cemetery Rd.
Come support our school, as well as local crafters, and artisans at the annual craft fair!
Cookie Decorating Party
Saturday, December 5th, 11:00 am - 2:00 pm
Bowdoinham Food Pantry, 9 Main St.
Please join us for a cookie decorating fundraiser for the food pantry! We'll provide the cookies, frosting and baubles; you bring your festive spirit. Financial donations to support the food pantry's work are appreciated but not required. All are welcome!
Pony Parade
Saturday, December 5th, Noon - 1:00 pm
Come see our local equestrians and their beautifully decorated horses at the Mailly Waterfront Park at Noon. Visitors can meet the horses, and some gifts will be offered as well. They’ll make another stop at the Community School around 12:30 pm. Weather permitting.
Snow Globe Workshop 
Sunday, December 6th, 12:00 pm – 2:00 pm
Merrymeeting Arts Center Pottery Studio, 9 Main Street
Build your own snow globe! MAC brings back this popular workshop for all ages.
Suggested donation: $5
