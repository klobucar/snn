Welcome to Knox Maine 
Knox, Maine is a quiet, rural town nestled in the rolling hills of Waldo County, offering a peaceful slice of Midcoast life. With a population of just over 800 residents, it’s the kind of place where neighbors wave from porches and the rhythm of life is set by the seasons. 
Surrounded by farmland, forests, and small ponds like Mixer Pond and Dutton Pond.
Located about 13 miles from Belfast, and within 40 miles of Bangor, Rockland, and Augusta.
Home to part of the Frye Mountain Wildlife Management Area, a haven for hiking, hunting, and wildlife watching.

About Knox Maine
The Town of Knox, a small rural community set among rolling hills and farmland, is named for General Henry Knox, the first United States Secretary of War. Situated on the northeastern corner of the Eastern Mid-Coast region, the town is thirteen miles from Belfast, thirty-six miles from Bangor, forty miles from Rockland, and thirty-eight miles from Augusta, which is the state capital.
