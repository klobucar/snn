Main Street/Route 1B PPI Project (2026) 




 

 




A Planning Partnership Initiative, or PPI, is a MaineDOT program designed to help fund time-sensitive, community-informed infrastructure projects. Damariscotta has embarked on a PPI project spanning the Main Street/Route 1B corridor from Bristol Road up to Route 1 near N.C. Hunt (seen in blue in the image to the right). 
This is an exciting opportunity to reimagine what an improved corridor could look like in 3-4 years – how Main Street could be more safe, accessible, and easy to travel for those living, working, and playing in Damariscotta! The Town is working with Sebago Technics engineering firm to create preliminary concept and design plans. 
This is not construction work - this is the visionary phase where the community can voice their ideas for Main Street.

Public Engagement





Between March and May 2026, the Town of Damariscotta hosted six public engagement meetings about the Main Street PPI process. 
In March, three meetings were held with business representatives, property owners, and abutters to the corridor. The meetings were designed so that the voices of our business community along Route 1B could be given a platform, and to ensure that the eventual concept design incorporates their unique needs without sacrificing levels of service or physical access to businesses.
Two public meetings were held in April, open to all members of the Damariscotta community. The project was introduced, and attendees were given a chance to voice concerns and share ideas about what improvements they wanted to see along Main Street. Discussion with community members and Town staff was substantial, and all feedback heard will be shared with Sebago Technics as they draft the design.
Finally, Sebago Technics hosted a public meeting in May. This was a great chance for their engineers to hear directly from community members, and for attendees to give ideas about specific traffic solutions, priorities, and budget concerns. Great conversations continued, and important feedback about the Main Street corridor was compiled by the Sebago team. You can read their meeting summary and early findings here.
Stay tuned for another public meeting in September, when the public can review the concept designs and provide feedback. This page will be updated accordingly.
