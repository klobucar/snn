Town Office
The office is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, & Friday, 8:30 am – 4:30 pm; Wednesdays 10:00 am - 4:30 pm.  The office is closed for lunch from 12:30 – 1:30 pm, and is closed on weekends and all major holidays.  
The primary administrative building for the town of Islesboro is located at 150 Main Road.  It houses the municipal positions associated with the town government, including the Town Manager, Town Clerk, Financial Director, Assessor, and the Code Enforcement Officer. The building also houses the Public Safety Offices.
Some facilities are available for your usage.  
The Building Facility Use Permit application may be used to reserve facilities for your organization.
The Recreational Facilities Use Permit application may be used to reserve recreational facilities for your organization.
Please contact us with any further inquiries.
