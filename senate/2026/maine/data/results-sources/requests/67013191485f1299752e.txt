                                Deepvale Update
The Central Avenue Extension is progressing.  Water connections are planned for the East side of
Central Avenue in the target area for the week of 10/12/2026.  Sidewalk installation to follow.  Project on schedule for completion by Nov. 1.
This extension will replace over 100 year old water main infrastructure for the first 10 houses on Central Avenue located just after the Sokokis Lake bridge and before Dunkin Donuts.  Please use caution in the area and follow directions from flaggers.  Please plan ahead and use caution when traveling through the work area.
Thank you.
