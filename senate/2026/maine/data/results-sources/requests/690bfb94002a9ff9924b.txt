Absentee & Ongoing Absentee Voting 




 

 




Absentee Ballots can be requested 2 months before a primary election and 90 days before an any other election. The ballots are available at Town Hall 30 days before an Election.
Easy Ways to Obtain Your Absentee Ballot
Online: The online request service is through the State of Maine.
Over the Phone: Call the Clerk's Office at 207-829-5559 to request your ballot be mailed to you!
In Person: Stop by Town Hall to pick up ballots for yourself/immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sister/brother) or vote your own ballot in person.
After you receive and complete your ballot, you can drop it in the drop box located outside the front entrance of Town Hall, hand deliver to the clerks office or mail them back.  Ballots must be received no later than 8:00 pm on election night.
In-Person Absentee Voting Hours
Absentee Voting hours are Monday through Wednesday, 8 am to 5 pm, and Thursday from 8 am to 6 pm.
Absentee ballots become available 30 days prior to Election Day.
Ongoing Absentee Voting
As of December 31, 2025, any registered voter, except for a uniformed service or overseas voter (i.e., UOCAVA voter), may apply for Ongoing Absentee Voter status. The previous law, which was in effect since early 2024, limited this status only to non-UOCAVA voters who were aged 65 or older or who self-certified that they had a disability. These restrictions are no longer in effect.
A voter who qualifies will automatically receive an absentee ballot for each statewide election, municipal election and any other election for which the voter is eligible to vote and need not submit a request for each election.
Learn more on the State of Maine website. (Find the application in the second paragraph!)
Accessible Absentee Ballots
Accessible absentee ballots are available to voters with disabilities.
Uniformed Service & Overseas Voters (UOCAVA)
Absentee ballots are available to Uniformed Service & Overseas Voters (UOCAVA).
