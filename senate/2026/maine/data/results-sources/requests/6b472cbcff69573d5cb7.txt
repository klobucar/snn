Town Clerk
The Town Clerk in Lincoln, Maine plays a vital role in keeping the town running smoothly and transparently. The Clerk’s office handles everything from maintaining official town records and vital statistics—like birth, death, and marriage certificates—to managing elections and voter registration. They also issue licenses and permits, record Town Council and Town Meeting proceedings, and ensure public documents are accessible to residents.
