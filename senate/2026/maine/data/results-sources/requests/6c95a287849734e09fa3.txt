Welcome to Cambridge Maine
New to town or just our website?  Take a look around to find out the local government, schedules of office
and meeting hours.  Check out our
Cambridge Community Board and News & Updates

We try our best to keep our website up to date with the
lastest information to keep you informed.  Have an event
you would like to add to the Cambridge Community Board?
Opening a new business in town?  Benefit supper or bake sale?
We would love to share your news with the community!
