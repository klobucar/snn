About the Office of The Town Clerk
The Office of Clerk began in England around 1272. The Clerk, or "Remembrancer" was called to meetings to remind the Councilors of the actions they had taken at the prior meetings.
Clerk's Office Recent Updates & Info:
September 1, 2026 Update - Stickers Have Arrived & The Town Clerk's Office Can Process All ATV Transactions
Good news! 2027 ATV registration stickers have arrived at Town Hall, and the Town Clerk’s Office can now process all ATV registration transactions as usual, including new registrations and renewals.
Thank you for your patience while the State worked through the sticker supply challenges. We appreciate everyone’s understanding during the delay and are happy to have registrations back to normal.
For more information, please visit our homepage newslist post here.
