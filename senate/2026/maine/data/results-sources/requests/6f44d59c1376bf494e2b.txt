Waterboro, ME
A Business & Family Friendly Community
Welcome to the Official Website of the Town of Waterboro, Maine. We hope this site will serve to better acquaint you with your local government and that it will provide you with an accurate history of the town through various means, including articles, photographs, and archives of meeting minutes from boards and committees. This site serves the Town of Waterboro as an updated, informative source of information regarding upcoming meetings and events as well.
The Town welcomes the input of its residents at meetings and through involvement with various boards and committees. Questions, comments and/or concerns may be presented through any of the contact means contained herein.

WELCOME TO THE TOWN OF WATERBORO
"Welcome to the Town of Waterboro. Our community is a hardworking town that is family and business friendly. It's a great place to visit and a great place to live. Waterboro is home to the regional schools RSU#57 including Massabesic High School and Middle School with an active high-school sports community. The Waterboro Town Hall is located at 24 Townhouse Road in East Waterboro and the phone number is: 207-247-6166.
