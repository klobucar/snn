Search Tips



Search Terms
What you'll find: 


Vehicle
Documents contaning the term vehicle, in any combination of upper and lowercase letters


motor vehicle 
Documents containing both words in any place or order in the document


motor NOT vehicle

Documents that have the word motor but not vehicle


motor not vehicle 
Documents that have all three words - motor, not, vehicle - in any order


motor OR vehicle 
Documents that have the word motor or vehicle or both


motor or vehicle 
Documents that have all three words - motor, or, vehicle - in any order


"motor vehicle" 
Documents containing the exact phrase motor vehicle


motor AND vehicle 
Documents with both terms motor and vehicle in any order


Wildcards
Wildcards let you search for words with multiple spellings and endings.


mot*
Documents with words beginning with mot, including words like motor, Motors, motion 


t?b
Documents with 3 letter words with any middle letter, like tab or tub




Note: Boolean keywords AND, OR, NOT must be in uppercase, and only one keyword can be used per query.
