Elections
 
The Next Town Meeting is: June 8, 2026 7PM Main St School Auditorium
The Election is: November 3, 2026
Polls open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.
Old Point Avenue School 108 Old Point Ave 
Parking is available at 23 Locust Street 
*Absentee voting is held at the Madison Town Office for all elections except for Presidential Elections. Please see the town clerk. Voting hours are the town office hours. Last day to absentee vote without special circumstances for the upcoming election is October 29th. Extended hours will be offered at the town office from 5PM to 7PM on October 29th. 
Need to register to vote? Please go to the Madison Town Office to Register to Vote. If you have questions please contact the town clerk at 696 5622 or 696 3971.
Looking to run for locally elected office?  Papers must be taken out and the required number of signatures returned up to 6 weeks prior to local elections.  Local elections are held the second Tuesday in June.  Contact the Town Clerk for details.  *Nomination Papers are currently available at the Town Clerk's Office as of March 2nd. Deadline to Return is 4PM April 3rd.
