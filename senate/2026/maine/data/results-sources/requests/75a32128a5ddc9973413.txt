Welcome to Houlton, Maine
Click on the image above for an informative video
Welcome to the official Town of Houlton website! Houlton is a living example of what life is like in the state of Maine.
A service center community, Houlton offers full time police, fire and ambulance service, an excellent community hospital, municipal water and sewer, and one of the lowest electric rates in New England. Yet, we are surrounded by the beauty of Northern Maine, and year round recreational and sporting activities.
Our beautiful downtown Market Square, excellent dining choices, shopping, and year-long list of events and activities make Houlton a great place to visit… or better yet live! Whether you are interested in visiting us, moving here, or locating a business here, browse our site to find what you are looking for. If you don’t see what you are looking for please feel free to contact us.
