Welcome to the Town of Turner, Maine!
The Town of Turner is a vibrant community, rich in history and deep-rooted cultural ties.  Nestled in the foothills of western Maine, bordering the cities of Lewiston and Auburn, our community has a uniquely distinct sense of place that embraces both the peacefulness of Turner’s natural assets with the hustle and bustle of our neighboring communities to the south.
With a population of just under 6,000, the Town of Turner is one of the more sought after communities for families looking to enjoy a high quality of life, while still having nearby access to the amenities that a large city provides.
​We encourage you to come and see for yourself all that Turner has to offer, and hope that you find our website convenient and informative!
