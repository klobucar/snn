Serving our Community
The Town Clerk’s Office offers many services for the residents of our Town. Our staff is trained to assist the residents of Lebanon with all of their registration and licensing needs. This includes all motor vehicle and recreational vehicle registrations and the issuance of dog and Inland Fisheries & Wildlife licenses.
The Clerk’s Office also handles all vital records for the Town which includes the issuance of marriage licenses and the recording of all births and deaths. When coming to the Town Office to do marriage intentions for your license, you must arrive 1  hour prior to closing time.  
Cash, Check and Debit/Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express & Discover) are accepted for payment. Any transaction that is processed with the Town of Lebanon by the use of credit and debit cards will incur a 2.95% fee.
