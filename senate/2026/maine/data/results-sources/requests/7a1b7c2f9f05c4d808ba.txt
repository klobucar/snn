Town Clerk
Duties & Responsibilities:
The Town Clerk’s Office is the keeper of the ordinances and minutes of meetings, as well as all vital records. We sell hunting/fishing licenses, dog licenses, parking permits, and horse permits. Trash bags are now sold in the Tax Office. The Town Clerk’s Office is also the supervisor of all elections.
