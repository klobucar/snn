Elections - State











STATE OF MAINE
GENERAL ELECTION
November 3, 2026
Polls will be open 8:00am - 8:00pm
Town Hall
23 South Effingham Road, West Newfield, Maine
In-person absentee voting available starting on October 5, 2026 at the 
Town Office
637 Water St., Newfield 
during regular office hours
Monday - Thursday 8:00am - 6:00pm
ending at 6:00pm Thursday, October 29, 2026
 
Absentee Ballot request's can be made by telephone 207-793-4348, written request by mail, in person
or electronic request
 
Please click on the left Absentee Ballot Request & Upcoming Election Information button to be taken to the State of Maine Elections site for more information. 
There is an external ballot drop box located at 637 Water St, Newfield for completed absentee ballots 24 hours a day, seven days a week.  This drop box is locked at 8:00pm on Election Day (November 3, 2026).
Voters can register to vote, update their voter registration or change their party status online up to 15 days before Election Day.  You must be in a party for three months before changing to another party.  
****Please call the Town Office at 207-793-4348 to verify your voter registration status****
**CLICK ON THE BALLOT TO ENLARGE**
