UPCOMING ELECTION, ​November 3, 2026:
State General & Local Referendum Election
Important Election Information and Dates:
October 5 - Absentee Ballots become available/In person voting at Town Hall
October 20 - Closed Period Begins: Only in person registrations will be accepted until Election Day
October 29 – Last day to Absentee vote without a special circumstance
November 3 -  Voting - Yarmouth High School Gym 286 West Elm Street 
Polls are open from 6:00AM to 8:00PM
Absentee Voting: 
Absentee Ballots can be requested NOW. The ballots are available at Town Office 30 days before an Election - ballots will be available October 5th for this election.
 
3 Easy Ways to Obtain Your Absentee Ballot:
Online: Request your ballot online. 
Over the Phone: Call the Clerk's Office at 207-846-9036 to request your ballot be mailed to you! Telephone requests must be made by the voter only.   
In Person: Stop by Town office starting October 6th to pick up ballots for yourself/immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sister/brother) or vote your own ballot in person.
After you receive and complete your ballot, you can drop them in the drop box located in the front parking lot (close to cleaves street).

In-Person Absentee Voting Hours:
Absentee Voting hours are Monday - Thursday: 8am-4pm, Friday 8am-12:00pm

Reminders:
• Update changes to your voter record ahead of time.  Name Changes, Address Updates or New Voters may do so during regular business hours to save time on Election Day.  
• Voter Registration Requirements:  New voters are required to show proof of ID and Residency, while those updating their status will need to show proof of their new name/address. 
• If you have questions, please call the Town Clerk at 207-846-9036, or e-mail the Town Clerk.
 
Questions?
Email Lisa Grant, Town Clerk, or call the Town office at 207-846-9036 or contact the State of Maine Bureau of Corporations, Elections and Commissions.
