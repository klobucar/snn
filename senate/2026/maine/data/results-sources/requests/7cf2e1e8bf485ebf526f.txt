*Election - November 3, 2026 General Election* 




 

 




November 3, 2026 is a General Election. 
VIEW THE SAMPLE BALLOT (PDF).
Polls will be open from 6:00am-8:00pm at Cumberland Town Hall. (Please note the earlier opening time!)
If you have any questions please contact Jennifer Doten, Town Clerk at 829-5559 x 1011 or [email protected]
Registering to Vote
In person: Residents can stop by during Town Hall office hours
Through the mail: complete a voter registration card through the mail/drop box. (Please note that after October 14th 2026, all registrations must occur in person at Cumberland Town Hall.*)
Register to Vote Online
After completing and signing the voter registration application, voters are encouraged to mail it or deliver it directly to the Municipal Clerk or Registrar of Voters in the municipality where they live, to avoid delays in processing. (In Cumberland, that's the Town Clerk at Town Hall.)
Residents wishing to register to vote must provide the following:
Proof of Identity (Included, but not limited to, a valid drivers license, state ID, passport, social security number, or any other government issued identification.)
Proof of Residency if your Cumberland address is not listed on your form of ID. (Included, but not limited to, a vehicle registration, hunting/fishing license, utility bill, bank statement, etc.)
Return documents to Cumberland Town Hall. Place them in the drop box at Town Hall or mail them to ATTN: Town Clerk, Cumberland Town Hall, 290 Tuttle Road, Cumberland, ME 04021.
*During the Closed Period, a resident needs to register in person at Town Hall. The Closed Period for this upcoming election will begin at the close of business October 14, 2026.  
Absentee Voting
Absentee Ballots can be requested 2 months before a primary election and 90 days before an any other election. Absentee ballots are available at Town Hall 30 days before an Election.
View the State of Maine site on Absentee Voting.
As of 12/31/25, any voter (other than a uniformed service or overseas voter) may apply for ongoing absentee voter status: (PDF). A voter who qualifies will automatically receive an absentee ballot for each statewide election, municipal election and any other election for which the voter is eligible to vote and need not submit a request for each election.
3 Easy Ways to Obtain Your Absentee Ballot:
Over the Phone: Call the Clerk's Office at 829-5559 to request your ballot be mailed to you! Telephone requests must be made by the voter only.  
In Person: Stop by Town Hall to pick up ballots for yourself/immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sister/brother) or vote your own ballot in person.
Online: Request your ballot online. 
After you receive and complete your ballot, you can drop them in the drop box located outside the front entrance of Town Hall, hand deliver to the clerks office or you can mail them back.
In-Person Absentee Voting Hours
Absentee Voting hours are Monday-Wednesday: 8am-5pm and Thursday: 8am-6pm. 
Important Information and Dates
October 5 - Absentee Ballots become available.
October 14 - Closed Period Begins: Only in person registrations will be accepted until Election Day
October 29 – Last day to Absentee vote without a special circumstance 
REMINDERS:
Update changes to your voter record ahead of time.  Name Changes, Address Updates or New Voters may do so during regular business hours to save time on Election Day.  
Voter Registration Requirements:  New voters are required to show proof of ID and Residency, while those updating their status will need to show proof of their new name/address. 
If you have questions, please call the Town Clerk at 829-5559 x1011, or e-mail the Town Clerk.
