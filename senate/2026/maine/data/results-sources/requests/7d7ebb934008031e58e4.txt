Absentee Voting:
Absentee request period starts 90 days prior to any election. We normally receive the ballots for an election 30 days prior. When we receive the ballots, we send them out to any and all voters who have submitted an application for that election. You must submit a request for each election to receive an absentee ballot. Within the 30 days prior to each Election, voters may come in to the Clerk's Office and Vote in Person as well, no application needed.
1. Electronic Request
You can apply for an absentee ballot through the State of Maine portal. Follow the prompts and fill in your info, and the City Clerk will receive an email stating that there are pending requests the following day. We then check the portal for our voters and process each request that comes through. Using this method, you will receive confirmation that we have either received or denied your request. This portal is also available for Over Seas voters.
2. Voting at City Hall (Absentee Voting Only!)
A voter may cast their absentee ballot at City Hall during normal working hours, Monday through Thursday from 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. starting 30 days prior to the election. The Thursday prior to the Election, our office is open until 7:30pm to allow for anyone to come in for No Reason Absentee voting because Friday and Monday before election day-Special Circumstances absentee balloting applies.
City Hall Clerk’s Office
131 Church Street, Lobby Floor
Belfast, ME 04915 
3.  Telephone Request
A voter may request a ballot by telephone. The city clerk will verify the voter’s identity by confirming the voter’s residence and date of birth. The city clerk will mail the ballot to the voter. To make a telephone request, please contact the city clerk at 207-338-3370 ext. 114.
4. Written Requests
You can also print the application below, complete, and send into the Clerk’s Office. When we receive it, we will process it and send you a ballot when we receive them. Again, we do not receive ballots until 30 days prior to the election. 
Absentee Ballot Application
5. Ongoing Absentee Voting
As of 12/31/25, any registered voter (other than a uniformed service or overseas voter) may apply for Ongoing Absentee Voter status: (Fillable PDF). A voter who qualifies will automatically receive an absentee ballot for each statewide election, municipal election and any other election for which the voter is eligible to vote and need not submit a request for each election. Voters must submit the completed form to their municipal clerk.
A voter applying for this status must do so in writing using the form designed by the Secretary of State and must originally sign the application and submit it to the Municipal Clerk in the town that they live in. A copy of the new application revised in 2026 is provided below, available at the City Clerk Office, available online and is posted on the Secretary of State’s Upcoming Elections web page. The voter must provide a telephone number and/or email address so that the voter can be notified if the voter’s status is terminated. However, voters who previously received Ongoing Absentee Voter status prior to law change will not have to reapply, unless their status was terminated as described below.
The revised law still provides that the voter’s Ongoing Absentee Voter status may only be terminated by the Clerk or the Secretary of State based on one of the following reasons:
The voter requests in writing that the Ongoing Absentee Voter status be terminated;
The voter dies or becomes disqualified;
The voter's registration record is cancelled in the central voter registration (CVR) system;
The voter’s absentee ballot is returned as undeliverable; or
The voter's registration status is changed to inactive in the CVR (because the voter failed to respond to a change of address mailing). Likewise, if the voter moves to a new municipality in Maine, the voter’s Ongoing Absentee Voter status is terminated, and the voter must submit a new application to the voter’s new residence municipality.
