Important Voting Information for the Upcoming November 3, 2026 Election
Standish residents, get ready for Election Day! Whether you plan to vote early, request an absentee ballot, or head to the polls on November 3rd, here are some key highlights you need to know:
Election Day Polls: Tuesday, November 3, 2026, from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM at the Standish Municipal Center (Fire Department Vehicle Bays, 175 Northeast Road).
In Person Absentee Voting Starts: October 5, 2026.
Absentee Ballot Request and Tracking: Available now! Click HERE
Election Information: Visit this link to see election information such as early processing times and extended hours, as well as warrants, full ordinance texts and more!
