Election Information
The Town of Orland will hold voting for the State of Maine General Election on Tuesday, November 3rd, 2026, from 8:00 AM-8:00 PM. The voting will be held at the Orland Community Center, 21 School House Rd, Orland, ME 04472.
For the most up-to-date information about State Elections visit the Maine Secretary of State Website
