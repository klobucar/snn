Click for details on other upcoming elections and voting
Online Voter Registration
You can register to vote using the State of Maine online voter registration service. Online voter registration is not available for the 21 days before a state or federal election, but you can still register to vote in person at the Sanford City Hall Clerk's Office. In-person voter registration is available up to and including Election Day.
In Person Voter Registration
You can register to vote in person by visit the Sanford City Clerk’s Office (919 Main Street - 1st Floor) or any State Bureau of Motor Vehicles office. Be sure to complete the full application and answer all required questions, including Party Affiliation. 
Important Notice: Green Independent is a political party. If you wish to have no party affiliation, select the last box: UNENROLLED
Absentee Ballot Request
Absentee ballot online requests are available 3 months before State Elections. *THIS WILL NOT BE AVAILABLE FOR THE SCHOOL BUDGET ELECTIONS
Use the State website to:
Request a ballot.
Print an application to mail or drop off.
Check the status of your request.
By Phone: Call the City Clerk’s Office at (207) 324-9125
After Hours: Print and complete the application to mail or drop off at the Clerk's Office.
For further assistance, please email the City Clerk/Voter Registrar
