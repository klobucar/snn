2027 Dog Licenses Now Available


2027 DOG LICENSES NOW AVAILABLE 

This is a reminder that dog licenses are now available. The rabies vaccination must be current at the time of licensing. The license renewal fee is $6.00 for spay/neutered; $11.00 for able to produce. Please renew on or before January 31st to avoid a $25.00 late fee. Town office hours are Monday, Wednesday & Friday 8am-5pm. Mailing address is 39 Cross Rd, Cushing and can be reached at (207)354-2375 Licenses can be purchased online at www.doglicensing.com
