Department Responsibilities
The Office of the Town Clerk and Treasurer is responsible for:
Collection of taxes and town fees
Issuing certified copies of birth, marriage, and death certificates
Registration of vehicles, dogs, boats, snowmobiles, and ATVs
Issuing marriage licenses, hunting and fishing licenses
Cemetery information
The election process (including voter registration)
2024 Tax Mill Rate = 17.72
Sign up for the Tax Club - break your tax bill up into eight monthly payments.
