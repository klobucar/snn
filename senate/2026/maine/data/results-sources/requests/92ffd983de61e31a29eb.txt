FREEPORT TOWN COUNCIL DISTRICT MEETINGS 
Town Council is once again hosting District Resident Meetings! These meetings are opportunities for the Freeport community to connect with councilors and discuss items and issues important to them. The remaining events are listed below. 
Unsure of which meeting to attend? Use this PDF map to find out which district you live in
Districts 2 & 3 - Hosted by Councilors Eric Smith & Matt Pillsbury Watch the councilors' welcome video here
Both events have passed
Districts 1 & 4 - Hosted by Councilors Dan Piltch & Chip Lawrence Watch the councilors' welcome video here
Thursday, October 29th at Maine Coast Waldorf School (57 Desert Rd)
All events will begin at 6:00pm
FREEPORT’S LOCAL GOVERNMENT STARTS WITH YOU! Taking the time to learn about issues facing the Town Council and sharing your feedback now helps to shape decisions made in the future! 
For more information or to request a reasonable accommodation, please contact (207) 865-4743 (#9) or swilson@freeportmaine.com
