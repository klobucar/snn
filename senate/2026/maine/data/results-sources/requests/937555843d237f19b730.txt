Help Keep Our Waste Workers Safe – Share This Important Reminder
Municipal WasteHub communities are sending more material than ever to the Municipal Waste Solutions (MWS) facility in Hampden, and most of it is exactly what belongs in the solid waste stream. Thank you for helping make that happen.  However, we’ve recently seen a rise in a few dangerous and damaging items that don’t belong in household trash. These include tires, syringes, liquids, and construction debris. These items can harm our equipment and, more importantly, the people who operate it.  We’re asking for your help in reminding residents what can’t go into their regular household trash. Please consider sharing a public notice or posting to your town’s website or social media. A quick reminder to your hauler would also be helpful, as they often catch these issues first.  We’ve included a graphic below that clearly outlines the list of unacceptable items.
