Elections2





The Town Clerk oversees all local, state, and federal elections held within the Town. The office manages voter registration, absentee ballot requests, and ensures polls are open and accessible to residents. Elections are held in the Town Hall Auditorium from 8am to 8pm, unless otherwise noted.

Elections
Berwick Election Results Are In After Record June Turnout
Berwick’s June election turned into a two-day count this week after more than 1,800 voters cast ballots in what town officials believe may be a record turnout for a June election in Berwick. The municipal results were signed by the Town Clerk on June 11, following a long Election Day marked by steady lines, hot weather, a nine-page ballot, and the use of an auxiliary ballot box to keep voters moving.
