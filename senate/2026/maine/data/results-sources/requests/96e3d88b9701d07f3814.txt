Millinocket Election Notice — November 3, 2026
 The State General Election and Millinocket Municipal Election will take place on Tuesday, November 3.
 Absentee ballots will be available Monday, October 5, through 4:00 p.m. Thursday, October 29.
📍 Returning your absentee ballot? The ballot drop box is located at the front entrance of the municipal building and will accept ballot returns until 8:00 p.m. on Election Night, November 3.
