Registering to Vote

Eligibility & Where to Register

In order to vote, you must be a United States citizen, live in Maine, be at least 18 years old, and be registered to vote in the municipality where you live.

You may register to vote at:

The Hampden Town Office during regular business hours
Any Motor Vehicle Branch Office
Voter registration drives
At the polls on Election Day




Identification Requirements

Any of the following forms of documentation may be offered by an applicant and considered by a registrar in verifying your identity. Please note: the registrar need not request or consider all of these forms of documentation to verify your identity.


Government-issued photograph identification or credential: Includes, but is not limited to, a current and valid United States passport, military identification, driver's license, or state identification.
Other government-issued identification: A document without a photograph, including a certified birth certificate or a signed social security card.
Other official documents: Documents confirming eligibility for public benefits, a utility bill, a bank statement, a government check, a paycheck, or other government document that shows the name and address of the voter.
Verified unique identifier (for new voters): A Maine driver's license number, Maine identification number, or the last 4 digits of the voter's social security number that are successfully verified through the central voter registration system verification.



Requesting a Voting List



Maine law governs the use and distribution of information from the Central Voter Registration (CVR) system. A specific form must be used for all requests for information contained in the CVR system.


Usage Restrictions: CVR material cannot be used for solicitations of any kind that are not directly related to the activities of a political party, so-called "get out the vote" efforts, or activities directly related to a campaign.


If you have questions about the CVR material or restrictions, please contact the Secretary of State's Office at 207-624-7650.




Please scroll down to the attachments section to download the Maine Voter Registration Application and the CVR Request Form.
