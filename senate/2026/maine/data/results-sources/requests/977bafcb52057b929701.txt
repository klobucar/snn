November 3, 2026 Municipal & State Election
Annual Town Meeting 
The Annual Town Meeting will take place on Thursday, June 10 2027 at the Hermon High School Auditorium. Time will be determined on the Warrant. 
When Can I Vote?
Election Day: 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM at Hermon High School Gym.
Absentee/Early Voting:
Applications available 3 months prior to the election.
Absentee ballots available and mailed 30 days prior to Election Day.
Can I Review the Ballots Ahead of Time?
Yes! Sample ballots will be available 30 days prior to the election.
Municipal Election Sample Ballot – Notice of Election 
State of Maine Referendum Sample Ballot – Notice of Election 
Voter Registration
Residents can register to vote or update their name/address:
On Election Day at Hermon High School Gym
At the Town Office during regular business hours
Online via the Maine Voter Registration Service
Maine Voter Guide: Describes who can register, how to register and how to vote
Voter Eligibility in Maine: Details the eligibility requirements to register and vote in Maine
Voter Rights: Provides an overview of voter rights in Maine
Requesting an Absentee Ballot
Call the Town Office: (207) 848-1010 – Option 3
Visit the Town Office in person during business hours
Use the Maine.gov Online Absentee Ballot 
Available only to current registered voters
Online option is active only during State & Federal Elections
Accessible ballots available for voters with disabilities
To expedite your application, consider using the online absentee ballot application administered by the Secretary of State. This service is only available during State and Federal Elections.
Returning an Absentee Ballot
Drop Box: Outside the Municipal Office, 333 Billings Rd
By Mail: Town of Hermon, 333 Billings Rd, Hermon, ME 04401
Must be received by November 3, 2026 at 8:00 PM
In Person:
At Town Office until November 3, 2026 at 8:00 PM
At Hermon High School Gym on Election Day, 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM
