Town Clerk 




 

 




Functions
The town clerk's office has four major functions: Elections, Vital Records, Licensing, and Keeper of Town Council and Board/Committee minutes. 
Dog Licenses
Dog Licenses due by December 31st each year. Please provide proof of rabies and spay/neuter. License at the Clerk's Office or online at Maine.gov.
Notary Services
Notary Public Services are offered through the Clerk's Office.
Freeport residents: $6 per notarization
Non-residents: $9 per notarization
If you are in need of Notarial Services, please bring proper I.D. and do not sign your document(s) ahead of time.
Dedimus Justice Services are also offered free of charge.
Record Keeping
The Clerk's office maintains and preserves legal and historical documents:
Town Council minutes
Boards and Committees information and minutes
Ordinance Book
Oaths of office
Road Book - discontinuance and acceptance of roads
Town Reports
For historical information, you may also contact the Freeport Historical Society at 207-865-3170. It is located at 45 Main Street.
