Comprehensive Planning Committee












The Comprehensive Planning Committee (CPC) is charged with developing, implementing, and monitoring strategic, long-term land use policies as outlined in the 2024 Comprehensive Plan. The CPC will facilitate implementation by coordinating with the Town Council, Planning Board, Town departments, School Department officials, and resident advisory boards, committees and commissions. With support from the Department of Planning & Development, the CPC shall: 
Regularly review the Comprehensive Plan and issue annual reports to the Planning Board and Town Council; and
Recommend setting or resetting priorities to the Planning Board, Town Council or other Boards and Committees; and
Identify the most efficient strategies for implementation in alignment with Town departments and Town Boards and Committees; and
Track and convene the identified responsible parties for the implementation strategies and actions to ensure that the plan goals are being addressed. In some cases, the Committee, or a working group of Committee members, may be called upon to be the responsible party to implement any of the strategies and actions.
The CPC released its 2025 Annual Report that identifies progress made on implementation of the Comprehensive Plan.
Interested in reviewing the current implementation table? Check out the March 16, 2026 update. Provide any updates or ask questions of the CPC and Town staff by emailing Erin Zwirko at [email protected].
The CPC meets on the fourth Tuesday of the month in the Town Hall Community Room beginning at 7 PM. Remote access is available: https://meet.goto.com/451872165, or join by phone 1 (872) 240-3412, access code 451-872-165.
