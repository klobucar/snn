Uniformed Service and Overseas Voters
The voter registration and absentee voter services on this page are for UOCAVA voters, who are:
Uniformed Service Voter:
Active duty military and their dependents (U.S. Army, Navy, Air Force and Marine Corps)
Coast Guard
Commissioned corps of the U.S. public Health Service and National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration
The Merchant Marine
Overseas Voter:
U.S. citizen living outside of the United States who was qualified or would be qualified to vote in their place of residence in Maine prior to leaving the U.S.Register to Vote
Register to vote in Maine online, or
Fill out this Federal Post Card Application (PDF) to register to vote and to request an absentee ballot for all elections within 18 months of submission. You can type your information onto the form, print, and sign your name.
There are 3 ways to submit your completed and signed application:
Email it as a scanned attachment to UOCAVA.CEC@maine.gov; or
Fax it, toll-free (within the U.S.) to 1-877-443-1302; or
Mail it to:
Bureau of Corporations, Elections, and Commissions
Elections Division
184 State House Station
Augusta, ME 04333-0184Request a Ballot
Fill out the Federal Post Card Application (PDF) or use this online Ballot Request to get a ballot:
Remember to choose how you want your ballot to be sent to you-either by mail or electronically
If you choose to get your ballot electronically, you will receive additional instructions after you submit your request
If you are a UOCAVA voter and you have requested an absentee ballot but not yet received it, please contact the Elections Division at 1-888-VOTESME (1-888-868-3763) or UOCAVA.CEC@maine.gov.
NOTE: The State Elections Division issues only the ballot for the state and federal elections. If  your municipality has a local election, your municipal clerk will issue that ballot. Ballots for local elections are sent via regular mail and should be requested as soon as feasible to increase the likelihood of timely receipt and return.  Receive the Ballot
Depending on the method you chose for ballot delivery, your ballot will be:
Mailed to the address you provided when you requested the ballot; or
Accessed by you through the State's online service as a printable PDF fileReturn a Voted Ballot
When you receive your absentee ballot and other election materials, please follow the included instructions to ensure that your ballot is returned on or before 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. If you have questions, please contact the Elections Division.
UOCAVA voters must return their voted ballot (by mail or electronically) by 8:00 p.m. on Election Day.Track Your Ballot
If you have requested a federal or state ballot from the Maine Elections Division, you may check the status of your request or ballot.Dates and Deadlines
The deadline to submit your voter registration application (FPCA) or request an absentee ballot is 12 p.m. (noon) on the day before Election Day.Request a Ballot for a Local Election
The State Elections Division issues only the ballot for the state and federal elections. If  your municipality has a local election, your municipal clerk will issue that ballot. 
NOTE: Ballots for local elections are sent via regular mail and should be requested as soon as feasible to increase the likelihood of timely receipt and return.  Additional Links
