Absentee Voting























General Information
State law permits absentee ballots to be cast at any election by any voter who properly requests a ballot. If a voter is interested in voting in a particular election but is unable to go to the polling place on election day, then the voter should obtain an absentee ballot. Absentee ballots become available at the Town Office (29 Elm St.) 30 days before the election.
Ways to Vote by Absentee Ballot
Camden voters may request an absentee ballot at any time between five and 90 days before any election. The statewide deadline for requesting an absentee ballot is at the close of business on the Thursday before an election day. Voters needing to obtain an absentee ballot after this deadline may do so if there are extenuating circumstances, such as medical emergencies.
A voter may vote by absentee ballot in person at the Town Office, located at 29 Elm St., during business hours. 
A voter may also call and request a ballot be sent by mail, before the deadline. Ballots are mailed on a daily basis. On behalf of the voter, an immediate family member may request in writing that a ballot be sent by mail or delivered to the voter by that family member. An application for an absentee ballot may be downloaded from this webpage as an election draws near. 
You can order online Absentee Ballot Request Service
You can complete an Absentee Ballot Application and submit it by:
Mailing it to: Town Clerk, Camden Town Office, P.O. Box 1207, Camden, ME 04843
Fax it to: 207-236-7956
Email it to: jmerrill@camdenmaine.gov
Note: If the ballot is returned by an immediate family member, the immediate family member must also sign the application or written request indicating they returned the ballot. The family member must state their relationship to the voter.
