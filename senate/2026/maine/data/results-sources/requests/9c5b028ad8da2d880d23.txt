About AARP Maine
AARP Maine is dedicated to empowering adults 50 and older to choose how they live as they age. We advocate for the health, financial security, and overall well-being of Maine residents through community programs, expert resources, and member benefits. With local volunteers and staff across the state, we work to strengthen communities and support family caregivers while fighting for issues that matter most to Mainers. Whether you're navigating Medicare, planning for retirement, or looking to stay active and engaged, AARP Maine is here to help you thrive.
