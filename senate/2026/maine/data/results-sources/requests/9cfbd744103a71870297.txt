Upcoming Election Information
















Election dates for the City of Sanford are ALWAYS as follows: 
 
Second Tuesday in June: School Budget Validation Election. 
First Tuesday after the first day of November: Municipal Election.
Voting Location
All Sanford & Springvale Residents Vote at the following location:
Sanford High School (100 Alumni Blvd, Sanford, ME, Door #41)
Polls are open: 7AM-8PM
 
2026 ELECTIONS IN SANFORD/SPRINGVALE: 
Sample Ballots for This Election now available:
TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2026, 
POLLS OPEN 7AM - 8PM

     Thursday, October 8: School Board & State Seats

MAINE ABSENTEE BALLOT REQUEST (State Elections Only) NOT AVAILABLE UNTIL 90 DAYS PRIOR TO ELECTION (AUG 3)
Maine citizens who are registered to vote may use this free service to request an absentee ballot for the: November 3, 2026, State Gubernatorial & Sanford Municipal Election (Local Ballot will also be issued automatically)
Ballots must be received no later than 8:00 p.m. EDT on Election Day.

You can also call the City Clerk's Office at (207) 324-9125 to request an application by mail.
