Marriage License Information
Couples will be required to complete the Marriage Intentions in BLACK ink.  
DO NOT sign the intentions until you are in the presence of the Clerk and/or Notary Public. 
Please call our office at 207-547-3340 to schedule an appointment for Marriage Intentions or click the link to direct you to the fillable forms online
