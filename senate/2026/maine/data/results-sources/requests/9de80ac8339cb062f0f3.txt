Voter Registration Appeals Board 




 

 




Members
The Voter Registration Appeals Board is composed of three members, one nominated by the City Clerk and one nominee of each of the two major political parties, serving 3- to 4-year terms.
Kandi-Lee Hoy
Republican Party Nominee
Term Expires May 5, 2028

Grace Roemer

Susan Mooney

Term Expires May 5, 2028
Overview
The Voter Registration Appeals Board is tasked with the purpose of hearing appeals of the Registrar's decision to remove a name from the voting list or refusing to place a name on the voting list and other duties as prescribed by law.
