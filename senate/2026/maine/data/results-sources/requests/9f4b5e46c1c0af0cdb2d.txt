Town of Merrill 
The town of Merrill, an excellent place to live, work and play, is located in Southern Aroostook County on Route 2. Merrill is just a couple of miles from I95. The human population of our six-mile square town is 250, moose – probably double that. We are just one hundred miles north of Bangor, and we have the wild untamed North Woods in our backyard – providing us with the best of both worlds. We have a Selectmen/Town Manager/ Town Meeting form of Government


































Attractions




All of the local residents know that some of the most beautiful sites in the world are located in Merrill, Maine.










Education




Merrill is a member of the Southern Aroostook Community School District. Six towns make up CSD#9 or SACS.
