September 21, 2026
Columbia Falls Select Board Statement Regarding Proposed Special Town Meeting
The Columbia Falls Select Board would like to address some of the questions and concerns
surrounding the petition brought forward by Richard Poirier and the proposed Special Town
Meeting scheduled for September 24, 2026.
At the July 14, 2026 Select Board meeting, the Board decided to place the articles contained in
Mr. Poirier's second petition before the voters at the regular Annual Town Meeting rather than
call a Special Town Meeting. That decision has not changed.
The Select Board believes that decisions involving the development and future use of Town-
owned property deserve proper planning and careful consideration before moving forward. We
also believe these articles should be heard at a meeting where as many Columbia Falls voters as
possible can participate. Our Town has historically had much lower voter turnout at special town meetings held at other times of the year, even though decisions made at those meetings can have
a lasting impact on the entire community.
The articles in the petition have not been denied. They will be brought before the voters at the
Annual Town Meeting. The Board's decision has been about when that vote should take place
and making sure a decision involving Town property has the planning, consideration, and
community participation it deserves.
The Select Board remains committed to following Maine law and conducting Town business
through the proper public process.
On September 18, 2026, the Select Board held a publicly noticed Special Select Board meeting
to consult with Town legal counsel. Following that consultation, the Board voted to declare the
“Special Town Warrant” dated September 16, 2026 and signed by a notary public invalid. The
Board also directed the Town Clerk, Treasurer, and all other municipal officials and employees
not to expend municipal funds or resources in support of the meeting called for in that warrant.
As a result, the Select Board does not recognize the proposed September 24 meeting as an
authorized Special Town Meeting of the Town of Columbia Falls and does not recognize any
vote resulting from that meeting as official Town action.
There have also been questions about the September 18 executive session. The Select Board
meeting itself was publicly noticed and open to the public. The Board voted publicly to enter
executive session to consult with Town legal counsel, as allowed under Maine law. The Board
returned to public session before taking any official action.
We understand that people have different opinions about this project and about the decisions the Board has made. We respect that. Our responsibility is to consider the Town as a whole, follow the law and the proper municipal process, and make the decisions we believe are in the best interest of Columbia Falls.
We encourage residents to stay involved, attend meetings, ask questions, review Town meeting
minutes and records, and participate when these matters come before the voters.
The Select Board will continue to keep residents informed as this moves forward.
Columbia Falls Select Board
Town of Columbia Falls, Maine

Quick Registration
Anyone can do a re-registration with rapid renewal provided your current registration has not expired.  You'll need your proof of insurance and current mileage.  Your previous registration is helpful but is not necessary.  
Vehicle Registration
Register your ATV or Snowmobile for the season.  Registrations are valid from July 1st - June 30th each year.  You will need your VIN number and a printer for your temporary registration.
