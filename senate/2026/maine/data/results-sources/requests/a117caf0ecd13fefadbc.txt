 Office Closures 2026
Town Office will be closed on the following holidays:
New Year's Day: Thursday, January 1st, 2026
Martin Luther King Jr. Day: Monday, January 19th, 2026
President's Day: Monday, February 16th, 2026 
Memorial Day: Monday, May 25th, 2026
Juneteenth: Friday, June 19, 2026 - Observed Thursday, June 18, 2026
Independence Day: Saturday, July 4th, 2026 - Observed Thursday July 2, 2026
Labor Day: Monday, September 7th, 2026
Columbus Day/Indigenous Peoples' Day: Monday, October 12th, 2026
Veterans Day: Wednesday, November 11th, 2026
Thanksgiving Day and Day After: Thursday, November 26th and Friday, November 27th, 2026
Christmas Day: Friday, December 25th, 2026
Please note: The Town Office may be closed for necessary training or other events. Due to the possibility that the website may not be updated in time, please call ahead.
