Voter Registration 




 

 




Voter registration hours for new registrations, enrollments, and any address or name changes are Monday through Thursday 7:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m. at the Town Clerk's Office. Please contact the Town Clerk's office at (207) 725-6658 for additional hours prior to each election.
