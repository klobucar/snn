REQUEST YOUR ABSENTEE BALLOT ONLINE  
In person absentee voting will begin October 5, 2026 at Auburn Hall, 60 Court Street during normal business hours.  The deadline to request an absentee ballot without cause is October 29, 2026.
POLLING PLACE INFORMATION
All polls will be open 7:00am-8:00pm on Election Day.  All polling places are ADA accessible. 
CityLink will provide free transportation on Election Day.  Please contact CityLink for more information.
Wards 1 and 2 vote at the Auburn Senior Community Center, 48 Pettengill Park Road
Wards 3 and 4 vote at Auburn Hall, 60 Court Street
Ward 5 votes at the Boys and Girls Club, 43 Second Street
If you are uncertain about where you vote, either contact the Office of the City Clerk by calling 333-6600 or click on the link below.
