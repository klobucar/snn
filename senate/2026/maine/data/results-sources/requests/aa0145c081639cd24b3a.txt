Welcome to Albion, Maine
Albion is a rural community located on the Northeast side of Kennebec County in Central Maine. With a total area of 39.46 square miles (38.83 sq miles land, .63 sq miles water) Albion is home to just over 2,000 people in approximately 916 households.
Land area – 24,192 Acres
Inland Water Area – 408 Acres
Previously called Freetown, Fairfax and Lagonia
Incorporated March 9, 1804, the 151st Town
Part set off to China, June 5, 1818
Name changed to Lagonia, March 10, 1821
Name changed to Albion, February 25, 1824
Part of Unity Plantation annexed March 30, 1852
Part sent off to Benton, March 14, 1853
Part of Benton annexed May 26, 1989
Population 2020 census - 2006
