Election and Voter Registration Information 




 

 




Polls are located at the municipal building in the gym located at 48 Morton Avenue, Dover-Foxcroft
Polling hours are from 8 am to 8 pm. Voter Registration opens at 7 am.
To link to the State of Maine Bureau of Corporations, Elections, and Commissions for information about this Election, visit the Maine.gov website.
Special Circumstances Ballot Applications are now available in the town office.
There are many convenient and secure ways to request an absentee ballot.
If you would like to make an electronic request for an absentee ballot, visit the Maine.gov Absentee Ballot Request webpage.
How to request an Absentee Ballot from the Town Office:
You may cast an absentee ballot in the town office through the Thursday prior to election day. No application is necessary if you are voting at the municipal office.
You may complete an application to take a ballot out of the office for yourself and your immediate family members.
An immediate family member can request an absentee ballot on your behalf in person at the town office or in writing.
You may request an absentee ballot over the telephone, but we must speak to each voter directly. Call the town office at 207-564-3318.
If you would like to request an absentee ballot in writing through the mail. Please call to have an application mailed to you, or view the Application for Absentee Ballot (PDF)
Mailing Address:
Julie Trotter, Town Clerk
Town of Dover-Foxcroft
48 Morton Avenue, Suite A
Dover-Foxcroft, ME 04426
Phone:207-564-3318
Returning Your Absentee Ballot
We must receive your absentee ballot by 8 pm on Election Day in order to count your vote! If you are delivering your ballot on Election Day, please bring your ballot to the polling place or use the secure absentee ballot drop box located at the entrance to the town office.
You may return it in person, by an immediate family member, by mail (postage paid), or by using our secure ballot drop box located at the front entrance of the municipal building at any time.
