Elections Information
Next Election : Tuesday, November 3rd, 2026 
State Election (Governor, State Reps, etc.)
All Elections Local and State are held from 8AM to 8PM at the Livermore Community Building located at 35 Church Street in Livermore
Absentee Ballots will be available starting October 3rd!
You do NOT need to call and request one if you have filled out an ongoing absentee ballot request. We already have those filled out and ready to go whenever we get the ballots in. 
If you want to request an Ongoing Absentee Ballot, please contact Amanda and we can get one sent out to you or you can come in and fill one out. You need to be 65 or older - OR - have a condition that prevents you from being able to go out to the polls to vote.
