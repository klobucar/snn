Town Clerk 


















Audra Fleury
Town Clerk/Registrar of Voters 
The Winslow Town Clerk serves as the Administrative Assistant to the Town Manager & Town Council.  Duties also include Registrar of Voters where the responsibility is to administer all aspects of State and Local Elections, as well as voter registration for the community.  
The Town Clerk's Office also provides Notary Public for the community at no fee, there is a $10 fee for non-residents.  The Town Clerk's Office is open Monday - Thursday from 8 am to 6 pm.  
General Assistance
The General Assistance Administrator works to assist the community with its welfare needs.  Those who wish to apply for General Assistance may do so Monday through Thursday from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.  *In the case of an Emergency, applicants may contact the Winslow Police Department at (207)872-5215*
If you need to contact me, I can be reached at 872-2776 extension 5209 or by email at AFleury@winslow-me.gov.
**The Town of Winslow does not have a Dedimus Justice available. 
If you need one, you can search for a Dedimus Justice in the area on the State of Maine website**
