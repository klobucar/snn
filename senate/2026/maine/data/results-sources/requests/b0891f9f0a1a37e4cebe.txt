Celebrate Bowdoinham 2026: A Weekend of Family Fun! 
Join us Friday, September 11 and Saturday, September 12, 2026 as we celebrate Bowdoinham’s 264th birthday with two days of all-ages fun along the Cathance River! Rain date: Sunday, September 13. From parades and fireworks to lobster crate races, live music, artisan booths, and the 26th Anniversary of the KenDUCKY Derby, Celebrate Bowdoinham 2026 has something for everyone.
New for 2026: We’ll be erecting a 40x80’ temporary pavilion at the new Bowdoinham Riverfront Park. Visit the Community Development Advisory Committee there to learn more about the park’s future plans and their work.
SeaSpray Kayaking will be offering kayak, paddleboard, and canoe rentals and an Introduction to Paddling Course at the hand carry launch. Advanced registration is required for the course only. More information below.
Step into the hidden world of the Cathance Meadow Trail system during a one-hour guided wildlife walk with Maine Inland Fisheries & Wildlife  staff (1–2 PM) —where every turn offers a chance to spot birds, uncover tracks, and learn the stories behind the landscape. Get to know our local flora and fauna and deepen your connection to the vibrant ecosystems that make Bowdoinham such a remarkable place.  Muster at their booth in Mailly.
Echo Ridge K9 demonstrations from 2-3 PM at the Bowdoinham Riverfront Park.
Festivities begin Friday evening with the return of the Chicken Run and the outdoor cinema featuring Open Season at the Recreation Field.  
On Saturday, the fun kicks off with the parade, themed “Bowdoinham Wildlife” and continues all day along the waterfront with local vendors, food trucks, carnival games, face painting, balloon art, inflatables, Jay the Magician, a dunk tank, live music, dancing, and more—all leading up to the fireworks finale over the river.  
 We can’t wait to Celebrate Bowdoinham with you! 
Friday Sept 11 - Evening Kickoff
Merrymeeting Arts Center: 5 – 8 PM
MAC presents a special exhibition and auction of works by longtime Bowdoinham artist Howard Solomon on September 11-12. Proceeds will benefit the Bowdoinham Public Library and Merrymeeting Arts. The opening reception is on Friday from 5 to 8 PM and continues from 11 AM – 4 PM on Saturday.
Annual Chicken Run: 5:30 PM
Children’s 1-Mile Fun Run kicks off at 5:30 PM in front of the school, followed by the 5K race. Hosted by the Bowdoinham Community School’s Parent Partnership, a fun way to support the school and start the weekend running! Info & registration: Runinarace.com | Facebook: @bowdoinhamchickenrun
Outdoor Cinema: 7 - 9 PM
New this year: outdoor family movie night featuring Open Season at the Rec Field. Free popcorn & open snack shack! Rain location: Bowdoinham Community School cafeteria.
Saturday, Sept 12: Celebrate All Day! 
Bowdoinham's Best Pie Contest
Please drop off your pie with your recipe at the Pie Sales Booth in Mailly before the parade. Cash prize awards will be posted at the booth after the parade. Pie slices will be sold with proceeds will go to Bowdoinham Food Pantry. If you have questions you may contact Shelley Hooper at 207-578-2686 or [email protected].
Coffee & Art Stop: 9 AM – Cathance River Gallery (18 Main St.)
Pop into the Cathance River Gallery to view their new exhibit and grab fresh coffee and donuts before heading to the waterfront. 
Blacksmithing & Local Shops: 9 AM – 4 PM - Longbranch School (25 Main St.)
Watch Nicholas Downing of Downing Arts give live blacksmithing demonstrations and shop his handcrafted jewelry, tools, and household items. While you’re there, browse the shelves at Merryreading Books from 9AM – 4 PM. 
Electa Chapter #2 Yard Sale: 9 AM – 4 PM - Village Lodge Masonic Hall 
Yard Sale proceeds will be donated to this year's charity, the Demsey Center. 
Historic Exhibit: 9:30 AM - Merrymeeting Hall
The Bowdoinham Historical Society will be open to the public, offering exhibits and displays that highlight the community’s story in celebration of the United States’ 250th anniversary.
Celebrate Bowdoinham Parade: 10 AM
This year’s parade theme is “Bowdoinham Wildlife!” Festivities kick off at 10 AM and follows this route:  From the Community School (23 Cemetery Rd); up Cemetery Rd.; Right onto Center St.; Left onto Post Rd; Left on Main St; Left on River Rd;  Left on Ridge Rd; Left on Cemetery Rd. (~1.8 mi)
Family Activities, Local Booths & Lunch: 10 AM - 4 PM  - Mailly Park
Stroll the artisan booths, visit with community groups, enjoy family fun with inflatables, and a dunk tank! Grab some lunch at Sylvester’s Fish & Chips, Kabayan Philippine Foods, or the Recreation Department benefit BBQ. Didn’t find what you were looking for? Support our other local businesses like Three Robbers Pub or the Country Store for fresh local to-go meals and provisions. Mocktails and deserts to benefit the Bowdoinham Food Pantry will also be availale.
Town Hall 50/50 Raffle: 10 AM – 4 PM – Town Tent
Enter the Town Hall Committee's 50/50 Raffle at Celebrate Bowdoinham and help support the final preservation projects for our Historic Town Hall. Proceeds will fund improvements including steeple painting, stone wall restoration, landscaping, and sound-dampening enhancements to make the space more welcoming for all.
$10/hr kayak, paddleboard and canoe rentals: 10 AM – 4 PM - Bowdoinham Riverview Park - Hand Carry Launch
Select from single and tandem kayaks, single and 6-person paddle boards or canoes. No advance reservation required. Gear and instructions provided. Appropriate for all ages.
Introduction to Paddling Class (Pre-Registration Required): Noon - 2 PM -Bowdoinham Riverview Park - Hand Carry Launch
Come learn how to paddle either a kayak or a paddleboard. $20/per person. Ages 10 and up. Register by calling: (207) 404-1100.
Zucchini Races: 11:30 AM - Matthew Townsend Parker Memorial Skatepark 
What do you do with a bumper crop of zucchini? Fit them with wheels and fun decorations and race them through the skatepark! Prizes for the fastest and best-decorated creations.
Afternoon Entertainment at the Gazebo: 11 AM – 4 PM
Visit the Gazebo at 11 AM to catch the announcement of Citizen of the Year, followed by a dance party with Dance Alinement at the Gazebo at 11:30 AM. From 12:30- 2:30 PM enjoy local music by Waxwing. Dr. Drew & His Animals Too will take the stage from 2:45 – 4:00 PM. Then there will be an intermission for the KenDucky Derby at 4 PM followed by more local music from Cilantro and the Bath Municipal Swing Band from 4:30 – 8:00 PM.
Afternoon Kids’ Activities 
Jay the Strolling Magician: 12:30 - 2:30 PM (Mailly Park)
One-hour Maine Inland Fisheries & Wildlife guided wildlife walk: 1–2 PM (Muster at IFW booth in Mailly Park)
Coin Scramble: Near the Carnival Games: 1 PM (Mailly Park)
Party Palooza Face Painting & Balloon Art: 4-6 PM (Mailly Park)
Lobster Crate Races: 2 PM (Mailly Park)
Echo Ridge K9 Demonstrations: 2 - 3 PM (Bowdoinham Riverfront Park)
KenDUCKY Derby: 4 PM (Mailly Park)
Three quacks for the 26th anniversary of the KenDUCKY Derby! Stop by the Town Tent by 3:45 PM to buy your lucky ducks. CASH ONLY and while supplies last! Check out the new town-branded merchandise while you’re there. All prizes for the day including the parade float grand prize and pie contest winner will be announced just before the ducks run the river.
Music at the Gazebo: 4:30 PM - 8 PM
Live Music with Cilantro: 4:30-6:30 PM 
Featuring classics and originals and a unique style of Blues, Jump Swing, Jazz, R&B.
Bath Municipal Swing Band: 7-8 PM
This 17-piece group primarily performs old standards from The ’40’s – Big Band – era with some additional numbers from the ’50’s and ’60’s.
Fire Dancing with Liza May: 7-8 PM 
Liza May returns for this captivating show!
Fireworks: 8 PM - Over the beautiful Cathance River 
Before the night ends, visit the new story walk along the Cathance Meadow Trails at the new park.
