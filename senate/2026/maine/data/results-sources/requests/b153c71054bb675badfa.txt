Welcome to Jackman, Maine!
Welcome!! We invite you to discover a place where natural beauty, outdoor adventure, and peaceful surroundings come together. Our community is built around recreation, tourism, and vibrant local events. Whether you’re chasing excitement in the great outdoors or seeking a quiet escape, you’ll find it here. Breathe in the pine-scented air, listen to the sounds of the wild, and savor the purest spring water in Maine.
