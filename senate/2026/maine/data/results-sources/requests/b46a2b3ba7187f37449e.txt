Welcome toLincoln, Maine
With nearly 70 miles of shore frontage, 19 miles of river frontage, over 4,300 acres of water, and 22,000 acres of woodland, Lincoln is one of Maine’s best-kept secrets. Lincoln is a small New England town, full of charm. Its central location, regional airport, and 13 lakes provide convenience, accessibility, and recreation for visitors as well as those who call Lincoln home.
Come for the lakes, stay for the lifestyle.

Departments
From essential services to community programs, our departments work every day to support the people, places, and progress that make Lincoln home.
All Departments
Police Dept
The Lincoln, Maine Police Department is a dedicated law enforcement agency focused on community engagement and public safety. Led by Chief Lee Miller, the department emphasizes fair, professional service and strong partnerships with residents and local organizations.

Fire Dept
The Lincoln Fire Department has been proudly serving the Lincoln region for over 100 years, including the towns of Chester and Winn, covering a 97-square-mile area.

Parks & Recreation
Lincoln Parks and Recreation provides spaces and programs that encourage play, connection, and community. From youth sports to parks and walking trails trails, there's something for everyone.
Read More
Library
The Lincoln Memorial Library in Lincoln, Maine, established in 1924, offers a collection of over 30,000 titles, free Wi-Fi, public computers, and children's programs, serving as a vibrant community hub for residents and visitors alike.
Read More
Town Council
The Lincoln Town Council serves as the legislative body of Lincoln, Maine, comprising seven elected members who meet monthly to enact policies and oversee municipal governance under the Council-Manager system.

Airport
Lincoln Regional Airport (FAA code: LRG) is a public-use facility located approximately two miles southwest of Lincoln, Maine. There are no landing fees for the Lincoln Regional Airport.
