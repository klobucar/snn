Welcome to Searsport
Welcome to Searsport, Maine – a charming coastal community of about 2,600 residents, rich in history, natural beauty, and small-town warmth. Whether you’re planning to make Searsport your new home or start a business here, you’ll find a supportive and welcoming environment. Our vibrant community offers a mix of historic charm, scenic views of Penobscot Bay, and a growing economy fueled by great food, culture, recreation, and maritime heritage. From friendly neighbors and excellent schools to recreational opportunities and a thriving arts scene, Searsport is a wonderful place to live, work, and grow. We’re excited to have you join us and look forward to supporting your journey in our town!
