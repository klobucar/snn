Free Assistance with Tax Filing
AARP Foundation Tax-Aide provides in-person and remote tax assistance free of charge to anyone, with a special focus on taxpayers who are 50 or older or who have low to moderate income.
Tax-Aide volunteers are located nationwide, and are trained and IRS-certified every year to make sure they know about and understand the latest changes and additions to the tax code. 
Free tax preparation and free electronic filing will be available at the Westbrook Community Center. Appointments are required and will be scheduled on Saturdays. To schedule an appointment, call 207-518-8579 beginning January 16, 2026.
Visit https://www.aarp.org/money/taxes/aarp_taxaide/ or call 207-518-8579 for more information.
CA$H Maine is a statewide collaboration of ten coalitions, comprised of 50 non- and for-profit partners, working together to help empower Maine individuals and families to achieve long-term financial stability. If you, or your household, made $69,000 or less, CA$H Maine volunteers will prepare and file your taxes for free. CA$H Maine volunteers receive rigorous IRS standards of conduct training and utilize secure and encrypted online software.
Visit https://www.cashmaine.org/free-tax-prep/ or call 207-347-2335 to contact the CA$H Greater Portland Coalition.
Federal Tax Forms
Walker Memorial Library has received a limited number of 1040/1040-SR federal tax forms and instruction booklets. Forms can be picked up in the lobby during the library's scheduled business hours.
Patrons can order additional print forms and instruction booklets at 1-800-829-3676 or www.irs.gov/uac/Forms-and-Publications-by-U.S.-Mail.
The South Portland Taxpayer Assistance Center (TAC) is open by appointment. To schedule an appointment, call 844-545-5640. Call 207-879-4683 or visit www.irs.gov/help/contact-my-local-office-in-Maine for additional information.
State Tax Forms
Maine state tax forms and instruction booklets can be requested at 207-624-7894.
Additional forms can be printed at https://www.maine.gov/revenue/tax-return-forms.
