Voting Location:

Elks Lodge, 37 Fourth Street, Old Town, Maine

Poll Hours: 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Absentee Ballots
Absentee ballots for the State General and Municipal
Election are expected to be available in early October 2026.
Requests may be made by:

- Online: the link is on the City’s website, under departments, Elections. 

- Phone: 207-827-3965, ext. 122

- Mail request

- In person at:
Old Town City Hall

265 Main Street

Monday – Thursday 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM
