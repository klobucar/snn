About Us








Winthrop is a 38–square–mile town in Kennebec County, Maine, with more than six square miles of sparkling lakes and ponds. Its 11 waterbodies, including the iconic Cobbossee Lake, along with scenic Mount Pisgah, make it a destination for outdoor recreation. Founded in 1771, Winthrop blends historic character with an engaged, forward-thinking community. Located along U.S. Route 202, just west of the capital city of Augusta, the town offers a vibrant quality of life, strong public services, and convenient access to the region’s major hubs.
