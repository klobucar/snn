Election Workers 




 

 




Overview
The Town of Gray is always looking for registered voters to help each election cycle. If you are interested in helping, please review the below information and then submit an application via this online form.
What Does an Election Worker Do?
Election workers play a critical role in the electoral process, ensuring that elections are conducted smoothly and fairly. Their responsibilities can vary depending on the specific role and the stage of the election process, but generally include:
Setting up polling stations
Checking voter registration
Assisting voters
Cleaning up
Is This a Paid Position?
Our election workers are volunteers eligible to receive nominal fees for the work they perform for the Town of Gray. Before starting, the Town requires completion of payroll forms, including the I-9, W-4 (state), W-4 (federal), and a background check consent form.
What Experience is Needed?
None! Everything you need to know about being an election worker will be explained before Election Day.
What If I Have More Questions?
Contact the Gray Town Clerk at 207-657-3339 or email townclerk@graymaine.org.
