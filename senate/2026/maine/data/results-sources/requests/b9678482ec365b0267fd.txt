General Information
                
                          ** NOTICE TO VOTERS**
                    Town of Falmouth Legislative and Senatorial District Changes
Please be advised that the State Representative District numbers have changed in accordance with the adoption of the 130th Legislature's apportionment laws.
                              District 43 is now District 112                 District 44 is now District 111
It is that your State Representative has changed as a part of that process as well. Please see the following Secretary of State's Web Lookup for your new district numbers and your representatives.
Falmouth Voting District Map
                       
                                      THIS CHANGE DOES NOT AFFECT THE POLLING LOCATION.

Absentee Voting
       Absentee Voting begins Monday, October 5, 2026. (30 days prior to the election)
                  A.  REQUESTING AN ABSENTEE BALLOT
1.Phone:        Contacting the Clerks Department at 699-5320   
2.Written:       a. Visiting the Town Hall during regular business hours
                           (Monday 7-4:30 & Tues-Thurs 8:00am-4:30pm)
                       b. Printing the Absentee Ballot Request Form (PDF) and mailing to Clerks Department at Town Hall, 271 Falmouth Road, Falmouth Maine 04105   
  3. Electronic:     Requesting it online through the Maine Absentee Ballot Request Service        
Check the status of your  Absentee Ballot Request   
_____________________________________________________________________________
                       B.  RETURNING AN ABSENTEE BALLOT
All Ballots must be received by 8:00pm on Election Day, there are no exceptions. 
Ballot may be returned as follows;
1. Official Ballot Drop Box:     There is an official ballot drop off box that you can deposit your ballot into at any time; it is located at the front of Town Hall to the left of the entrance 
2. Drop off In Person:             Stop by the Clerk's Department at Town Hall during business hours:
                                               Monday 7:00am-4:30pm, Tuesday-Thursday, 8:00am-4:30pm
3.  Mail:                                    Return ballot in the mail. Please allow enough time for us to receive the
                                                ballot by deadline of 8pm on Election Day.
                                       Please note that ballots are NOT accepted by postmark dates
                           *** All ballots received after 8 o'clock on election day must be REJECTED***
_____________________________________________________________________________
 ELECTION DAY VOTING
Polls are open on Election Day, at the Falmouth High School Gym, 74 Woodville Road, at 7:00am and close at 8:00pm.
No voting will take place at Town Hall on Election Day. The Clerks' Office at Town Hall will be closed so staff can facilitate the elections at the polling place. All other Town Offices and Departments will remain open.
