How to Request an Absentee Ballot Application:
By Phone: Call 207-475-1313
Online: Visit https://absenteeballotrequest.sos.maine.gov/
By Email: For an absentee ballot application, click here. Applications can be emailed to Jrichards@kitteryme.org.
In Person: Stop by the Customer Service Center:
Monday - Wednesday: 8:30 AM - 4:00 PM
Thursday: 8:30 AM - 6:00 PM
Friday: 8:30 AM - 1:30 PM
If you have already submitted an ongoing absentee ballot application, you do not need to apply again.
Absentee Ballots
We mailed ballots to everyone who requested one over the weekend; please allow a few weeks for them to arrive.
Please note that you cannot vote early at Town Hall until October 5, which is 30 days before the election, in accordance with state election regulations. You may return your completed absentee ballot to Town Hall; however, you must complete ballots outside of Town Hall. You may place completed absentee ballots in the drop box outside Town Hall.
If you have questions about absentee ballots or the voting process, please contact Jillian Richards at 207-475-1313 or jrichards@kitteryme.org

The Town Clerk's office would like to remind voters that it is necessary to update their voter registration card if they
Move or change your address.
Have an updated address due to the Town changing your street name or street number.
Change your name.
When updating a voter registration card due to a name change, you must include a certified copy citing the change through Marriage, Death, or Legal Name change through the court system.
Wish to change your party.
To make changes to your voter registration card, you can
Visit Town Hall, located at 200 Rogers Road, Kittery, ME, and make the changes in person.
Update your registration information online with Maine's Secretary of State Office.
