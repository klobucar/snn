Windham's Next Election
November 3, 2026 State General and Municipal Election, to be held in in the Windham High School, 406 Gray Road, in the Main Gym from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM
Warrant for November 3, 2026 Election
View the Sample ballots  
To participate in elections you must be registered to vote. 
Voters may cast an absentee ballot instead of voting in person. Casting an absentee ballot is convenient and secure. 
Absentee ballots may be requested in writing, by phone, online, or in person up to  the Thursday before Election Day, without specifying a reason.  However, if you are asking to receive your ballot by mail, please request it early enough to allow for postal mail delivery time – both to receive it and return it (2-5 days each way).
To request a ballot please call 892-1900.
You can request an absentee ballot online by or by mail from the Secretary of State's website (click link in side-bar menu above)  
Ballots must be returned to the municipal clerk no later than 8:00 pm on Election Day.
You may also request a ballot for a relative by filling out an application for absentee ballot.  Please print and present at the Clerk's Office.
Please reference the Absentee Voting Guide on the Maine.gov website.
