2026 Annual Town Cleanup Day
September 18, 2026Town Notices
The Town of Bowdoin will host an annual Cleanup Day ever year in the Fall. The exact date will be updated and is determined by the weather and availability of staff and volunteers.
Details
Date: Saturday, October 24, 2026
Hours: 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM
Location: 320-328 West Road, Bowdoin, ME 04287
Restrictions
You must be a current Town of Bowdoin resident with a valid address.
Proof of residency may be requested
One load per household
Acceptable Items
Appliances (Except those listed in the prohibited items section below)
Bicycles
Couches
Lawnmowers
Must be drained of all fluids
Mattresses
Metal
Swing Sets
Tires, off rims (Limit of 2)
Wood, Brush, Rocks, and Bricks (Separated)
Prohibited Items
Asphalt Shingles
Batteries
Glass
Hazardous materials
Helium Canisters (Unless valve has been removed)
Hazardous Liquids (Gasoline, Oil, Chemicals, etc.)
Household trash
Loose Pieces of insulation
Loose Pieces of Styrofoam
Propane Canisters (Unless valve has been removed)
Refrigerators, Freezers, Air Conditioners, and/or Humidifiers **
Windows
** Items are accepted at the Town Recycling Center. Please see below for details
Special Collection at Bowdoin Recycling Center
Date: Saturday, October 24, 2026
Hours: 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM
Location: Town Recycling Center Only
Acceptable Items ($50.00 fee per item)
Air Conditioners
Dehumidifiers
Freezers
Refrigerators
Acceptable Items ($20.00 fee per item)
Computer Monitors
Digital Photo Frames
E-Waste (Batteries Must Be Removed)
Game Consoles
Printers
Televisions
