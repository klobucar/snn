Election Redistricting Maps 




 

 




Every 10 years in Maine, redistricting occurs immediately following a nationwide census effort.  In 2020 / 2021, Orono was examined and then reassigned from House of Representatives District 123 / 124 to House of Representatives District  24 / 25. Many Orono streets and roads voter districts were impacted, thus state official representation changed.  In order to stay informed, please view and utilize our tools below.
View the Town of Orono street list.
Use the interactive tool for looking up your district ward/precinct, and candidates information.
