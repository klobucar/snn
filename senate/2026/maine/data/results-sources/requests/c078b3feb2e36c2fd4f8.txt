In Lubec, the sun rises before anywhere else in the U.S., and every morning feels like a quiet promise. It’s the kind of place where you grab your coffee, watch the light stretch across the ocean, and remember how good stillness can feel.

AS A REMINDER…
Taxes 
The first half payment of 2026 Real Estate and Personal Property Taxes are due December 31, 2026. The second half of taxes are due May 28, 2027.
Credit/Debit Card payments are made through a third-party payment processor, Maine Payport, and each payment made through the service will incur a Payport Fee. The fees are: $0-$40= $1.00 minimum charge; $40.01 and Up= Flat 2.5% fee calculated based on the transaction total.
