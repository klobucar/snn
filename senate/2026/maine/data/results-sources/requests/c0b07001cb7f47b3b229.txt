About Us
The Town Clerk provides the communication link between Citizens, Council, Town Manager, Administrative Departments, and agencies of government at other levels.  The Town Clerk serves as the information center by making available the records of local government (ordinance's, meeting minutes, legal documents, etc.), explaining procedures, distribution of materials, and answering questions pertaining to municipal government activities, and administrating State and Local Elections for the Town of Madawaska.

Moving a Mobile Home
Maine State law requires that the property taxes be paid on a mobile home before it can be moved from one town to another.  The mover is required to attain a form from the Tax Collector, MV-47, that certifies that the property taxes on the mobile home have been paid and submit this form to the BMV in order to obtain an overlimit permit.
