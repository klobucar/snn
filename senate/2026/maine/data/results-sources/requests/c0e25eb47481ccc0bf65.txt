East Millinocket means East of "many islands" in the language Maine's Abenaki people. The town lies on the northern side of the West Branch 
of the Penobscot River, seventy miles north of Bangor. The town was incorporated on February 21,1907.


This rural Northern Maine community has a population of approximately 1860 people, rich in many different ethnic backgrounds. With an 
interesting history of being started around the business of papermaking to moving into the 21st century with many ideas of economic 
development, we welcome you.


Please sit back and tour our town to see what a great area we have to visit, vacation, relax and enjoy a day or a lifetime.










































East Mill Works - A New Beginning


East Mill Works is a redevelopment initiative focused on transforming a historic former paper mill
in East Millinocket, Maine into a vibrant mixed-use hub for businesses, jobs, and community spaces.
Backed by federal EPA Brownfields grants totaling $4.5 million, the project is actively cleaning
and preparing the site to attract new investment and partners. Current tenants — including Convalt
Energy, Home Grown Fuels, and Lynch Logistics — are already putting the mill back to work as the
community shapes its economic future.
