Election FAQs
How do I register to vote? 
Any resident of the Town of Hebron can register to vote by coming to the Town Office during its open hours. A driver’s license and proof of residency is required. Residents can also register on election day.
How do I vote absentee?
Residents already registered to vote in Hebron can obtain an absentee ballot in a number of ways. They can make a telephonic request for an absentee ballot by calling the Town Office, can pick one up at the Town Office, and can also request one from the State of Maine’s web site. Absentee ballots must be returned according to the instructions provided on the ballot.
Where do I vote?
All voting (other than that done at the Annual Town Meeting) is done at the Hebron Town Office.
Who should I contact with further questions?
For any other questions, please call the Town Office during its regular hours.
