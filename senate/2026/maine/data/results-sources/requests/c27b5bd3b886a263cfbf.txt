June 9, 2026 Update - It's Election Day!
Today is Election Day in South Berwick!
Residents are encouraged to take part in the June 9, 2026 State Primary and School Budget Validation Referendum Election by casting their ballot and making their voice heard.
June 9, 2026 State Primary and School Budget Validation Referendum Election
Location: Marshwood High School, 260 Dow Highway, South Berwick
Polling Hours: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM
Whether you're a first-time voter or a longtime participant in the democratic process, we encourage all eligible residents to make a plan to vote today.
Review Your Sample Ballot Before You Go
Want to feel more prepared before arriving at the polls?
Sample ballots are available on the Town's Elections webpage and provide a helpful preview of what will appear on your official ballot. Taking a few minutes to review your ballot ahead of time can help make your voting experience quicker and easier.
To view Sample Ballots for the June 9, 2026 Election, please click here.
Absentee Ballot Reminder
If you are returning an absentee ballot, please remember that all absentee ballots must be received by the Town Clerk's Office by 8:00 PM today in order to be counted.
Absentee ballots may be returned using the secure ballot drop box located outside Town Hall or delivered directly to election officials before the deadline at the polling location (Marshwood High School, 260 Dow Hwy).
A Reminder to Be Kind
Election Day is the result of many hours of preparation and coordination by the Town Clerk's Office, Election Workers, Town staff, and volunteers.
From preparing ballots and testing equipment to assisting voters and processing absentee ballots, these dedicated individuals work tirelessly to help ensure a safe, secure, accurate, and accessible election for our community.
As you visit the polls today, we kindly ask everyone to extend patience, kindness, and respect to the staff and volunteers who are working hard to assist voters throughout the day.
Clerk's Office Closure Reminder
Please note that the Town Clerk's Office is closed today, June 9, for Election operations and will remain closed on Wednesday, June 10, for post-election processing.  We appreciate your understanding as staff focus on conducting and administering today's election.
Thank you for participating in our community and helping shape the future of South Berwick. We look forward to seeing you at the polls!
State Primary & MSAD 35 School Budget Validation Referendum Election Reminders & Info
Elections are one of the most important ways we come together as a community to share our voices and help shape the future of our town. Whether you are a first-time voter or have participated in many elections before, the Town of South Berwick encourages all eligible residents to take part in the upcoming June 9, 2026 State Primary Election. A little preparation ahead of time can make Election Day smoother, easier, and more meaningful.
Below you’ll find key information to help you plan ahead, including where and when to vote, sample ballots, absentee voting details, and important upcoming dates and office closures.
Election Day Information - June 9, 2026
The State Primary and School Budget Validation Referendum Election will take place on Tuesday, June 9, 2026 from 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM at Marshwood High School, located at 260 Dow Hwy in South Berwick.
Primary Elections determine each qualified party’s nomination of candidates for federal offices and State and County offices.  Party candidates who are elected at the Primary qualify to appear on the November General Election ballot.
Offices included in the 2026 Primary Election are US Senator, Representative to Congress (both districts), Governor, all 35 State Senate districts, all 151 State Representative districts, and the following county offices, which vary depending on the county: Judge of Probate, Register of Probate, County Treasurer, Register of Deeds, Sheriff, District Attorney and County Commissioner.
Sample Ballots Now Available
Sample ballots for the June 9, 2026 Election are available online and provide a helpful preview of what will appear on your official ballot. Taking a few minutes to review your sample ballot in advance can help you feel more confident and prepared when you arrive to vote:
To view sample ballots for the June 9, 2026 Election, please visit our Elections page here.
Absentee Voting Information
If you are unable to vote in person on Election Day, absentee voting is available as a convenient option.
Residents may request an absentee ballot through the Town Clerk’s Office in person, by phone or online until June 4th, 2026 at 7 PM. We strongly encourage voters to request ballots early to allow enough time for processing, mailing, and return.
Online (Fastest & Easiest): Use the Maine Secretary of State’s secure online absentee ballot request service:
Click here to visit the Maine Online Absentee Ballot Request Service
By Phone: Call the South Berwick Town Clerk’s Office at (207) 384-3300, Option 6.
In Person: Visit the Town Clerk’s Office during regular business hours at the South Berwick Town Hall (180 Main Street).
Monday - Wednesday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Thursdays: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
Closed Fridays
Additional absentee voting forms and information are also available through the Maine Secretary of State website here: Maine Elections & Voting Information
Absentee Ballot Reminders: Tracking & Deadlines
Maine voters can track the status of their absentee ballot online, including when it was issued and when it has been received by visiting the Maine Secretary of State’s website below:
Track your ballot using the Maine Online Absentee Ballot Request & Tracking Service here.
Important Absentee Ballot Reminders:
Under Maine State law, the deadline to request an absentee ballot from the Town Clerk’s Office is Thursday, June 4, 2026 at 7:00 PM, unless special circumstances exist.
Completed ballots must be received by the Town Clerk’s Office by 8:00 PM on Election Day in order to be counted.
Absentee ballots can be returned using the secure drop box located outside Town Hall (180 Main Street in South Berwick) or returned via mail.
If returning absentee ballots by mail, please allow extra time for mail delivery when planning your return.
If you have questions about eligibility or the process, the Clerk’s Office is always available to help guide you through it.
Voter Registration Night – June 4, 2026
The Town Clerk’s Office is pleased to offer a Voter Registration Night ahead of the election to make it as easy as possible for residents to get ready to vote.
Date: Thursday, June 4, 2026
Time: 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM
Location: South Berwick Town Hall – Clerk’s Office (180 Main Street)
During this event, residents can:
Register to vote
Update voter registration information (address, name changes, etc.)
Ask questions about voting or Election Day procedures
This is a great opportunity to take care of registration needs in a quick and convenient way before Election Day arrives.
Clerk’s Office Closures for Election Preparation
Due to the Election, please note the following schedule changes at the Town Clerk’s Office:
Monday, June 8, 2026 – Closing at 12:00 PM for Election setup
Tuesday, June 9, 2026 – Closed for Election operations
Wednesday, June 10, 2026 – Closed for post-election processing
We appreciate your understanding as staff work diligently behind the scenes to prepare for and carry out a smooth and secure election.
A Few Final Reminders for Election Day & Election Worker Opportunities
Behind every election is a tremendous amount of preparation, coordination, and dedication from the Town Clerk’s Office and Election workers who help ensure voting is conducted fairly, accurately, and securely for our community.
In the days leading up to Election Day, and throughout the election itself, staff work long hours preparing ballots, testing equipment, coordinating logistics, assisting voters, processing absentee ballots, and managing countless behind-the-scenes details that help make the process run smoothly.
We ask residents to please remember that our staff and Election workers are here to help. Patience, kindness, and respect go a long way during what can be a very busy and demanding time.
The Town of South Berwick sincerely appreciates the hard work and commitment of the Clerk’s Office Department, Election workers, Town Staff and volunteers who help make our local elections possible. Their dedication plays an important role in supporting a safe, secure, and accessible voting experience for all.
As Election Day approaches, we encourage residents to take a few simple steps that can make a big difference:
Confirm your voter registration is up to date
Review your sample ballot ahead of time
Plan ahead if you intend to vote absentee
Reach out early with any questions or concerns
Interested in Becoming an Election Worker?
We are always looking for community members who are interested in supporting the democratic process by serving as Election Workers. Election workers play an essential role in ensuring elections run smoothly, securely, and fairly, and it is a meaningful way to contribute directly to your community.
If you are interested in becoming an Election Worker for the Town of South Berwick, we encourage you to apply using the following link: South Berwick Election Worker Form
For full election details, sample ballots, and voter information, please visit the Town of South Berwick Elections page at www.southberwickmaine.org/elections.
