Absentee Ballot & Election Information Available
State General Election & Special Town Meeting  
November 3, 2026
Absentee ballots are available now for the State General Election & Special Town Meeting scheduled for Tuesday, November 3, 2026. 
The Municipal Clerk must receive your absentee ballot application by close of business on Thursday, October 29, 2026, unless you qualify for special circumstances under Maine law. 
 
Return completed absentee ballots to the Municipal Clerk by 8:00 p.m. on Election Day, November 3, 2026.
You may download the paper absentee ballot application using the link below:
Click here to download an Absentee Ballot Application
Maine voters may also request an absentee ballot online and track the status of their ballot through the State of Maine’s secure portal:
Click here to go to the State of Maine Online Absentee Ballot Request & Tracking Service
Polls will be open on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM at the Wiscasset Community Center.
