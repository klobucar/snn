Election Results 




 

 




Included below are the results from recent elections held in the City of Westbrook: 
June 9. 2026 - Official Results, State Primary & Municipal Referendum Election. 
November 4, 2025 - Official Results, State Referendum and Municipal General Election. A recount for the City Councilor, At-Large position was held on November 14, 2025, the final results for that recount can be found here.
June 10, 2025 - Official Results, Municipal School Budget Validation Referendum
November 5, 2024 - Official Results, State General & Referendum Election and Municipal Election. Includes official Ranked Choice Voting tabulation for the Mayoral race. Click here for information on the City of Westbrook's RCV Policies. Click here to access the recording of Westbrook's first-ever Municipal RCV tabulation. A recount for the Ward 4 City Council seat took place on November 15, 2024. Click here to see the final results from this recount. 
July 16, 2024 - Official Results, Municipal School Budget Validation Referendum
June 11, 2024 - Official Results, State Primary & Municipal School Budget Validation Referendum
March 5, 2024 - Official Results, Presidential Primary
November 7, 2023 - Official Results, State Referendum
June 13, 2023 - Official Results, School Budget Validation Referendum
November 8, 2022 - Official Results, State General & Municipal Election
June 14, 2022 - Official Results, State Primary & Municipal School Budget Validation Referendum
November 2, 2021 - Official Results, State Referendum and Municipal General & Referendum Election
June 8, 2021 - Official Results, School Budget Validation Referendum
November 3, 2020 - Official Results, State General & Municipal Referendum
July 14, 2020 - Official Results, State Primary & Referendum; Municipal School Budget Validation Referendum
March 3, 2020 - Official Results, Presidential Primary & Special Referendum 
November 5, 2019 - Official Results, State Referendum & Municipal General
June 11, 2019 - Official Results, Municipal School Budget Referendum
November 6, 2018 - Official Results, State & Municipal General & Referendum Elections. A recount for the Ward 5 School Committee seat took place on November 19, 2018. Click here to see the final results from this recount.
June 12, 2018 - Official Results, State Primary & Referendum Election and Municipal School Budget Validation Referendum
November 7, 2017 - Official Results, State Referendum Election
June 13, 2017 - Official Results, State Referendum & Municipal School Budget Validation Referendum
November 8, 2016 - Official Results, State & Municipal General & Referendum Elections
June 14, 2016 - Official Results, State Primary & Municipal School Budget Validation Referendum
November 3, 2015 - Official Results, State Referendum & Municipal General Election
June 9, 2015 - Official Results, Municipal School Budget Validation Referendum
June 10, 2014 - Official Results, State Primary & Municipal Referendum
