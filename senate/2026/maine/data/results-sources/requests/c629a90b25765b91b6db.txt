News Flash








November 3, 2026 Election Information 

Applications for absentee ballots for the November 3, 2026 Election are now available. 




Community Center Leases 

At the October 20, 2026 City Council meeting, the City Council will consider a recommendation about the future of lease agreements at the J. Richard Martin Community Center. Read on...





Biddeford and Saco Selected for National Homeless Response Peer Network 

The Cities of Biddeford and Saco have been selected for the 2026 Homeless Response Peer Network, a national program of the Housing Solutions Lab at New York University’s Furman Center that will strengthen their regional response to homelessness. 




City of Biddeford Announces 2026 Employee and Volunteer of the Year Honorees 

The City of Biddeford recognized outstanding contributions from staff and community members at its annual Employee and Volunteer Appreciation Banquet at the J. Richard Martin Community Center. 




Sewer Customers: Learn More About Your Next Bill 

Find information about past billing errors, our bill credit process, and upcoming changes to sewer billing rates. Contact us at 207-286-9387 or sewerbilling@biddefordmaine.org with questions not answered here. Sewer Billing Information





Jessica Muise Nominated as Biddeford City Councilor for Ward 4 

Mayor Liam LaFountain has nominated Jessica Muise as the City of Biddeford’s next City Councilor for Ward 4 for the remainder of the current term, which expires in December 2027. Her appointment is subject to confirmation by the City Council on Sept. 1. 




Mayor LaFountain Update on the Shooting of Johan Sebastián Durán Guerrero and Ongoing Developments 

Read the latest statement from Mayor Liam LaFountain. 




Alfred Street Sewer Separation Project Information 

Sewer separation work will take place this year on Alfred St (between Summer St and Porter St), as well as Mt. Vernon St, Birch St, and Porter St. Construction is anticipated to begin the week of April 13 and continue through November in six phases.
