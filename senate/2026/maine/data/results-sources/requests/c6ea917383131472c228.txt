Vital Records


PLEASE NOTE:  NEW MARRIAGE LICENSES REQUIRE AN IN PERSON APPOINTMENT.  CALL 696-5622
Certified copies of Birth, Marriage, and Death records are available at our office. The cost is $15.00 for the first copy and $6.00 for each additional one of the same certificate purchased at the same time. You may come into our office or order copies by mail.

Boat, Snowmobile and ATV Registrations



The Town of Madison serves as an agent for the Maine Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife (IF&W) for the registration and re-registration of boats, snowmobiles, and all terrain vehicles (ATVs). This service is only available to Madison residents. You can find out more information on how to register your boat, snowmobile or ATV at the Maine Department of Inland Fisheries & Wildlife website.

Dog Licensing & Animal Complaints


Dog Licenses expire December 31st of every year. A late fee of $25 goes into effect February 1st of every year. To license your dog you will need to bring in proof of current rabies certificate and neuturing or spayed certificate if applicable.  Fees for licensing a dog is $11.00 unaltered dog and $6.00 for an altered dog.

*Absentee voting is held at the Madison Town Office for all elections except for Presidential Elections. Please see the town clerk. Voting hours are the town office hours. Last day to absentee vote without special circumstances for the upcoming election is October 29th. Extended hours will be offered at the town office from 5PM to 7PM on October 29th. 
Need to register to vote? Please go to the Madison Town Office to Register to Vote. If you have questions please contact the town clerk at 696 5622 or 696 3971.
Looking to run for locally elected office?  Papers must be taken out and the required number of signatures returned up to 6 weeks prior to local elections.  Local elections are held the second Tuesday in June.  Contact the Town Clerk for details.  *Nomination Papers are currently available at the Town Clerk's Office as of March 2nd.
