Moscow, Maine
Population: 464
Settled: 1783 
Incorporated: January 30, 1816
The Town of Moscow's western boundaries are beautiful Wyman Lake and the famed Kennebec River. The scenic Arnold Trail winds northward through Moscow on its way to Quebec.
Moscow is home to Baker Mountain Ski Tow, one of the oldest ski areas in the state. 
Wyman Dam, one of the largest hydroelectric facilities, is located in Moscow.
