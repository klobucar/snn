Press Release - Admin Assistant / Deputy Clerk
10-09-2026
PRESS RELEASE  
Tremont, Maine – The Town of Tremont is pleased to announce the hiring of Renee Hodgdon as Administrative Assistant/Deputy Clerk.
Hodgdon previously served as an administrative assistant and secretary at both Tremont Consolidated School and Pemetic School. She also owns Bittersweet Shop in Bernard and serves on the Tremont Planning Board.
A longtime member of the community, Hodgdon resides in Tremont with her husband, Doug. Her friendly demeanor, familiarity with the community, and strong work ethic make her an excellent addition to the Town Office team.
The Town of Tremont looks forward to welcoming Renee to her new role and working with her as she serves the residents of Tremont.
Questions regarding this appointment may be directed to the Town Office at (207) 244-7204.

Now Hiring - Public Works Positions
09-24-2026
The Town of Tremont is seeking applicants for the following positions;  a fulltime, year-round Public Works Foreman, a fulltime, year-round Equipment Operator/Maintenance position, and part time snow plow drivers. Minimum requirements include possession of a Maine Class 2 CDL driver’s license, 5 years’ related experience, and a team player attitude.  Application forms available on line at www.tremont.maine.gov or at the Town Office, 20 Harbor Drive, Bass Harbor.  Applications must be submitted by October 15, 2026. Applications may be submitted by mail to PO Box 159, Bernard, Me 04612, in person at the Town Office, or by email to manager@tremont.maine.gov.~ The Town Of Tremont Is An Equal Opportunity Employer ~
