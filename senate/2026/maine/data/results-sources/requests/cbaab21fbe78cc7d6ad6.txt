Town of Jackson, Maine
Welcome to the town of Jackson, Maine, USA. Settled in 1798 and incorporated on June 12, 1812, Jackson is a small town of about 600 people in Waldo County. If you would like to be added to the resident-run email list for town news, send an email to JacksonBulletinBoard@gmail.com.

This is sponsored by the Waldo County Humane Society, and is FREE for all Waldo County residents. It is $10 per pet for non-residents. Cats must be in secure carriers. The Transfer Station is open Sundays, 8 AM - 5 PM. Jackson transfer station stickers are free for residents, and available at the town office. Please bring your vehicle registration to get one. Stickers must be displayed on your vehicle in order to use the transfer station.
