 NOVEMBER 3, 2026 STATE OF MAINE GENERAL ELECTION
Polling location: 105 Camden Rd., Warren (Masonic Hall)
Polls open at 8 am and close at 8 pm. 
Offices on ballot: US
Senate, Rep. to Congress, Dist.1, Governor,
Maine State Senate Dist. 12, Rep. to Legislature Dist. 44, Judge of Probate Knox County, Sherriff Knox County, District Attorney Knox County





ABSENTEE BALLOT REQUESTS may be submitted to your town clerk starting 8/3/2026. The request can be made in person, via telephone, online or via mail.  Please see links for those forms and/or sites. 
ABSENTEE BALLOTS will be available by October 5, 2026.
THE LAST DAY TO REQUEST AN ABSENTEE BALLOT: THURSDAY, OCTOBER 29, 2026 
The Town Clerk/Registrar of Voters office is open during
regular business hours of the Town Office for inquiries. You may register to vote in person at the town office or on election day at the polls, online, at voter registration drives, or by mail in application. Proof of ID and residency is required.
