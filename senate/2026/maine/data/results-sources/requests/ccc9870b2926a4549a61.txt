§755. Deadline
In order to be valid, an absentee ballot must be delivered to the municipal clerk at any time before the polls are closed.
 
[PL 1991, c. 622, Pt. X, §9 (AMD).]

SECTION HISTORY
PL 1985, c. 161, §6 (NEW). PL 1987, c. 624 (AMD). PL 1991, c. 622, §X9 (AMD).
