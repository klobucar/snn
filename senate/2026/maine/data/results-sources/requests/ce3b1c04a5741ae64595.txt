Absentee Ballots 




 

 




Absentee Ballots are available at the Town Office 30 days prior to the election.
Below are the options you have to vote by Absentee Ballot:
Vote in person at the Town Office (10 Village Square Road) during office hours.
Contact the Town Clerk's Office at 207-829-3705 to request a ballot by mail. Telephone requests must be made by the voter only.
Complete a request form (PDF) and bring it or mail it to the Town Office.
Online request service .
