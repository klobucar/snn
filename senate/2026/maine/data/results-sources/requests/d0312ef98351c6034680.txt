ABSENTEE BALLOTS for November 3rd Election
Absentee Ballots are now available. 
If you want to vote Absentee for the upcoming November 3rd Election, you may do one of the following:
Go to the Secretary of State website and request your ballot through the online portalhttps://absenteeballotrequest.sos.maine.gov/.  The Town receives the application from the State and will mail out your Absentee Ballots.
Print and fill out the Absentee Ballot Form (click here) and mail or bring to Town Office. Once received, we will issue the ballots.
Call the Town Office (207) 926-4126 x 1 and request over the phone (we must talk to the voter who is requesting a ballot).  The Town will mail out your absentee ballot.
Automatic Absentee Ballot Request
The State now has a way to Absentee Vote each election and not make a request each time to do so.  You may now fill out a form once at Town Hall (must be done in person) and Absentee Ballots will automatically be mailed to you for each Election.  No phone calls.  No filling out forms each time.
