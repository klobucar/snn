Election Overview
State and federal elections, including the General Election in November, are held in accordance with Maine election schedules, typically on the first Tuesday following the first Monday in November.  Town of Wells holds its Annual Town Meeting on the second Tuesday in June, which includes the Annual Budget Referendum and the election of municipal officials.  Special Town Meetings may also be scheduled as needed.

Absentee Voting
Absentee ballots are available for voters starting Monday, October 5, 2026. 
Voters can request an absentee ballot in several ways:
Submit an Absentee Ballot Application
Request an Absentee Ballot Online
Call the Town Clerk's Office at (207) 646-2882
Visit the Town Clerk's Office at Town Hall

Thursday, October 29, 2026 during regular business hours, without special circumstance.
Return Deadline
All voted absentee ballots must be received by 8:00 PM on Election Day.

Voter Registration
To vote in Wells elections, you must be registered with the Town. Voters may register: 
In person at the Wells Town Clerk’s Office, located at the Wells Town Hall, 208 Sanford Rd, Wells, ME 04090. 
By mail using the Maine Secretary of State voter registration application
On Election Day at the polls.  
You must be a U.S. citizen, a resident of Wells, and at least 18 years old. Maine offers same-day voter registration, allowing eligible residents to register and vote on Election Day.
For questions or assistance, please contact the Town Clerk’s Office at 207-646-2882 or visit the State of Maine Voter Guide. 









 

 




Wells Voting Districts 145 and 146
Wells has two districts in the Maine House of Representatives: District 145 and District 146. 
Most of Wells is covered by District 145. 
A small portion of the town is in District 146: the southern/coastal portion of Wells, areas adjacent to Ogunquit and bordering on the Atlantic Ocean. 
The candidates on your ballot will only be the ones representing your district.










 

 




Election Worker Information
The Town of Wells is always seeking residents interested in serving as election workers. Election workers play an important role in ensuring fair, efficient, and secure elections for the community.
If you are interested in serving as an election worker, please contact the Town Clerk’s Office at 207-646-2882 for more information.
