Town Clerk/Registrar of Voters























The mission of the Town Clerk's/Registrar of Voter's office is to accurately maintain all town records, conduct elections with professionalism in accordance with State Law, issue State licenses and provide information to both Town officials and the general public in a friendly and confidential manner.
The Department, as required per Maine State requirements, is responsible for the following:
Coordinate election administrations for the Town
Record and maintain all vital statistics records (birth/marriage/death) for the Town
Serving as custodian of all official Town records
Administer the town-wide dog licensing program
