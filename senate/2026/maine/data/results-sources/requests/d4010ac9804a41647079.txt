The way life used to should be.
Hike, ATV, Snowmobile, hunt, fish, boat, kayak, swim, farm, or cool off in the Pleasant and Narraguagus rivers. 
Incorporated, 1833. Population, 60. Beddington lies 35 miles N.W. from Machias and about 40 E. from Bangor.
Town Offices Hours
The Town Office will be open every Thursday from 8am-3pm. Office may close due to State or Federal Emergencies or inclement weather. 
If you wish to register your vehicle, boat or pay your taxes, please call the office at 207-638-2514 to verify we are open.
You can also register your boat at: https://www5.informe.org/online/boat
