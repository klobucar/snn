Elections:
All Local, State & Federal Elections are administered and supervised by the Town Clerk.
The Annual Municipal Election is held on the Tuesday in conjunction with State and Federal Elections. The Annual Municipal Election is for the purpose of electing Town Select Board Members, RSU20 School Board Directors, Budget and Library Committee members. Click HERE to read a Citizen's Guide to Town Meetings in Maine.


Absentee Ballots:
Citizens who are unable to vote at the polling place on Election Day may vote by absentee ballot. 
Use Maine's Absentee Ballot Request Service to order an absentee ballot online.
Or you can request an absentee ballot from the Town Clerk's office by:

Telephone request for your own ballot application.  A ballot will be mailed to the address you provide the Town Clerk.
Written request by completing an absentee ballot application.  This application can be mailed, faxed or delivered in person to the Town Clerk.
You can also obtain a ballot for an immediate family member by written request.   A ballot will be mailed to the voter directly or given to the immediate family member making the request.  Immediate family member includes:  spouse, parent, grandparent, child, grandchild, sister, brother, step-parent, step-grandparent, step-child, step-grandchild, step-sister, step-brother, mother-in-law, father-in-law, brother-in-law, sister-in-law, son-in-law, daughter-in-law, or guardian.

UPCOMING ELECTIONS


A General (midterm) Election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026 at the Searsport Community Building located at 42 Prospect Street. The polls will be open for secret ballot voting from 8:00AM until 8:00PM.
Click HERE to see the State and County races on the Sample Ballot.
Click HERE to see the Federal races on the Sample Ballot.
Early & Absentee Voting is available at the Town Office from Monday, October 5th through Thursday, October 29th, 8:30AM - 4:00PM.
Early & Absentee ballots will be processed on Friday, October 30, 2026, at the Town Office located at 1 Union Street, Searsport. A 1-hour inspection period is available from 8:30AM to 9:30AM. Processing of early and absentee ballots will begin at 9:30AM.
Two races on the ballot use Ranked Choice Voting (RCV). 
Click HERE to view a poster published by the State of Maine explaining the do's and don'ts of RCV.
Click HERE to navigate to Maine.gov and read more about Ranked Choice Voting.
