Police
This form is for general questions, requests for information, comments, suggestions or messages to specific individuals.
This form should not be used to report a crime or request police services.
We appreciate your constructive comments and are continually striving to find ways to enhance the level and quality of service to the community. The Message Center mail is checked on a daily basis, Monday through Friday. Every effort will be made for a timely response to those messages requiring one. Your message will be forwarded to the appropriate person within the department for consideration.
This email form is not intended for the reporting of roadway defects and SHALL NOT CONSTITUTE NOTICE as it cannot be assured that the email will be promptly received by the City’s Employees.  If you have a concern about a roadway defect (such as a pothole) or an immediate infrastructure issue that needs to be addressed in Sanford or Springvale, please call the Department of Public Works at (207) 324-9135 x 1 from Monday – Friday between the hours of 8:00 am to 4:30 pm.  If the issue is urgent and occurring outside of these hours, please contact the Sanford Regional Communications Center at (207) 324-3644 x 1 or dial 911.
