Welcome to the State of Maine Online Absentee Ballot Request & Tracking Service
November 03, 2026 General Election 
Registered Maine voters may use this free service to request an absentee ballot for the November 03, 2026 General Election.
Ballots must be received no later than 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time on Election Day.
For more information, please visit our Upcoming Elections page.
Track My Ballot
If you have already requested a ballot, check the status here.
Maine Voters (currently living in Maine or elsewhere in the U.S.)
If you are a Maine voter currently living at a Maine address or temporarily staying at an address located within the United States or a U.S. territory, you can request an absentee ballot by clicking the button for this section.
Please review the Maine Voting Residence Fact Sheet if you are unsure whether you meet Maine voter requirements. 
Maine Voters Request an Absentee Ballot
Uniformed Service or Overseas Voters (UOCAVA)
If you are a Uniformed Service voter living away from your Maine residence or a U.S. citizen living outside the United States and otherwise meet the requirements to be a Maine voter, you can request an absentee ballot by clicking the button for this section.
Please review the UOCAVA Voter page if you are unsure whether you meet UOCAVA voter requirements. 
UOCAVA Voters Request an Absentee Ballot
Accessible Ballots for Maine Voters
If you are a voter with a print disability that makes it difficult for you to use a paper ballot, you can request an electronic accessible ballot by clicking the button for this section.
Please review the Maine Voting Residence Fact Sheet if you are unsure whether you meet Maine voter requirements.
