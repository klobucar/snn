ABSENTEE VOTING
There will be a State and Municipal Election on November 3, 2026.  We are currently accepting absentee ballot applications.
In-person absentee voting will start on Monday, October 5, 2026, and take place in the City Hall Auditorium on the 2nd floor.    Monday - Thursday 7:30 a.m. - 4:00 p.m. and Friday 7:30 a.m. - 1:00 p.m.
Extended Hours - On Thursday, October 29, 2026, our office will be open until 6:00 p.m. to accept absentee ballot requests and voter registration applications.
The last day to request or vote an in-person absentee ballot is Thursday, October 29, 2026, unless you have a special circumstance. 
There will be no absentee voting on Friday, October 30, 2026, or Monday, November 2, 2026.
SEMI-OPEN PRIMARY
Effective January 1, 2024, Maine will be transitioning from a closed primary system to a semi-open primary.  Unenrolled voters will now be able to participate in primaries.
          Unenrolled Voters - Voters who are not enrolled in a party may choose to vote in one party's primary.
          Voters enrolled in a party - May only vote in that party's primary.
                            
ongoing absentee ballot status
Beginning January 1, 2026, any registered voter, except for a uniformed service or overseas voter, may apply for Ongoing Absentee Voter status.   A voter applying for this status must do so in writing using the form designed by the Secretary of State and must originally sign the application and submit it to the Municipal Clerk.   This means that after submitting the application, the voter does not need to submit an application for each election; instead, the voter will automatically receive an absentee ballot for each ensuing statewide election, municipal election, and any other election for which the voter is entitled to vote.  
Note:  The voter MUST complete the ongoing absentee ballot application.  An immediate family member MAY NOT complete the ongoing absentee ballot application for the voter.  The ongoing application is located below under "Mail-In Requests".

This service is for:

Once you receive your ballot, you can return it using the absentee ballot drop box (please note: the drop box will not be set up outside City Hall until ballots are ready to be issued). Alternatively, you can hand deliver the ballot to the City Clerk's Office or mail it to the City Clerk's Office, 300 Main Street, Saco, ME. 04072.
