Voter Registration
Maine residents may register to vote at any time, including on Election Day. 
If you live in Warren, you may register:


At the Town Office: 167 Western Road, Warren


At the polls on Election Day

online: Voting Registration Forms | SOS

To register to vote, you must provide:


Proof of identity (e.g., driver’s license)


Proof of residency (must show a physical street address in Warren — not a P.O. Box)


There is no deadline for IN-PERSON registration at your town office or city hall.
However, if registering by mail or through a voter registration drive, or online, your application must be received by the close of business 21 days before the election.
Updating Your Voter Registration
Already registered but need to update your name, address, or party affiliation? Visit the Town Clerk’s Office.
Maine recognizes four qualified political parties:


Democrat


Republican


Green Independent


Libertarian

Other (voters may currently choose "No Labels" or "Constitutional Party" as other)

Voters may also choose to remain unenrolled (independent of any party).
Changing Political Party Enrollment
State law requires certain waiting periods for party enrollment changes:


If switching from one party to another, you must wait 15 days before voting in that party’s primary, caucus, or convention


Unenrolled voters may join a party at any time, including on Election Day.  


Currently UNENROLLED VOTERS may choose one ballot from any party at primary elections.


Once enrolled in a party, you must stay enrolled for at least 3 months before changing or withdrawing


If you withdraw from a party, you must wait 15 days before enrolling in a new one


Closed Period Registration
During the 20 business days before any election, voter registration is only available:


In person at the Town Office


At the polls on Election Day at the Registrar’s Table


New residents must present proof of identity and Warren residency to be added to the voter list.
Registrar Hours
In accordance with Title 21-A MRSA §101(6), the Registrar of Voters is available during the following hours (as approved by the Board of Selectmen on December 26, 2024):


Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday: 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.


Thursday: 9:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.


For questions or assistance, please contact the Warren Town Clerk’s Office 273-2421.
