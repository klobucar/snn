Public Hearing Being Held on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, for November 3rd, 2026, Special Town Meeting
Home
Posted on October 14, 2026 
The Select Board will hold a Public Hearing during its October 20th, 2026, meeting to take comments and answer questions about the November 3rd Town Meeting ballot. No changes can be made to the ballot. NOTE: This meeting will be held at the Wiscasset Middle/High School gym. 
For more information:
