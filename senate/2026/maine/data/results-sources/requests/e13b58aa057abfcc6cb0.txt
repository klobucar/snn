Arrowsic Elections
Our Next Election: November 11/3/2026, General Election.
Polling Location: Town Hall, 340 Arrowsic Rd.
Polling Hours: 10AM – 8PM.
Ballot Information: Ballot information will be posted when available.
Absentee Ballots: Absentee Ballots may be requested 90 days prior to an election. Absentee Ballots are available 30 days prior to the election. The deadline to request an Absentee Ballot without a Special Circumstance is  5:00PM the Thursday before the election.
All absentee ballots must be returned by 8PM on election day (the close of polls).
November 3, 2026, Election:
Absentee Ballot Request Date: Beginning, Monday, August 3, 2026
Absentee Ballots Available: Beginning,  Monday, October 5, 2026
Absentee Ballot Request Deadline (without a Special Circumstance): Thursday, October 29, 2026
 You may request an absentee ballot in one of the following ways:
Complete an online request form HERE. Your ballots will be mailed to you once they become available.
Call 207-443-4609  to request ballots be sent to your mailing address.
Make an appointment with the Town Clerk, Barbara Boyce, once absentee ballots are available, and either vote in person or take your ballots home and return once voted.
Absentee Ballot Drop Box: The Absentee Ballot Drop Box will be available starting the week of October 5, 2026. This is a secure, 24/7 way to submit your ballot and requires no postage.
Accessible Absentee Ballots. Voters that have a disability that prevents them from completing a paper ballot, may fill out an application for an Accessible Absentee Ballot.For information about accessible absentee voting go HERE.
Ongoing Absentee Ballot. State election law allows all registered voters to apply for ongoing absentee voter status. A voter who qualifies will automatically receive an absentee ballot for each statewide election, municipal election, and any other election for which the voter is eligible to vote and need not submit a request for each election. You may request an application from the Town Clerk or print and fill in the application  HERE. Submit your completed application to the Town Clerk.
Questions about the upcoming election? Contact Town Clerk, Barbara Boyce, at barbara.boyce@ arrowsic.org or call 443-4609.
For information about voter registration, go HERE or contact Registrar, Vicky Stoneman, at vicky.stoneman@arrowsic.org.
