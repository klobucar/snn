Election Worker Signup
Looking to volunteer your time or earn a little money? We would love to add you to our list of awesome community members whom we reach out to each election to make our town's elections possible.
QUALIFICATIONS
   - Must be a U.S. Citizen
   - Must be at least 18 years old
   - Must be a registered voter in Waterboro, Maine
   - Must not be a candidate or a member of a candidate's immediate family
   - Must pass a background check
DUTIES AND TASKS
The following duties are standard at many polling places:
   - Check voters in using our voter list
   - Distribute ballots
   - Monitor the voting machine or ballot box
   -  Tally results at the end of the night
   - Process absentee ballots
BENEFITS
   - Workers earn the federal minimum hourly wage, or you may volunteer your time.
   - Opportunity to help the community 
   - Learn more about the election process
