City Clerk 




 

 




The City Clerk's Bureau provides the following services:
Enrollment and Registration of Voters, Conducts Elections, Vital Records (birth, death, and marriage), Dog Licenses, and IFW's Hunting and Fishing Licenses, and is the custodian of City Council Agendas and Minutes.
The City Clerk's Office operates under the Department of Finance and Administration, overseen by Director Ingrid Nivison. The Department of Finance and Administration is organized into smaller, specialized bureaus to enhance our effectiveness. This structure allows us to focus on different areas of community service with the required expertise.

Vital Records





Vital Records: Birth, Death, & Marriage Certificates
'How to' Document (PDF)
Access to birth, death, and marriage records will now be limited to the person on the record, the person's spouse, registered domestic partner, parent, guardian, descendant (child, grandchild), attorney or agent, certain other family members*, genealogists/agent, officiant, informant, or funeral establishment.
These identification requirements apply to both certified and non-certified copies of vital records.
Individuals requesting records will need to provide proof of their eligibility to obtain the record (proof of lineage, certificate of domestic partnership, marriage license, guardianship papers, notarized statement from family, etc.).
Vital Records (me.accessgov.com)
Persons requesting to purchase copies of vital records must complete a request form that declares their name and connection with the person who is listed on the record. Anyone purchasing a certified copy must show proof of identification: driver's license, passport, or other government-issued picture identification card.
If one of these is not available, the requestor will need to show two items from the following list: utility bills, bank statements car registration, copy of income tax return, personal check with address, a previously issued vital record, letter from a government agency requesting the vital record, Department of Corrections ID card, Social Security card, DD214, hospital birth worksheet, license/rental agreement, pay stub or W-2, voter registration card, Medicare/Mainecare insurance card, private or public school photo ID, college photo ID, employee photo ID, or a disability card from SSA.
*Other Family Members include grandparent, sibling, step-parent, step-child, aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, mother-in-law, and father-in-law.





Dog Licensing Information





About Licensing
Dog licensing ensures rabies vaccination, which protects the health of your pet. Licensing your dog directly helps fight animal cruelty and abuse; 90% of dog license fees go directly to the Maine Animal Welfare Program. Dog license fees account for 95% of the State Animal Welfare Program's entire funding, and without these fees, the state would be unable to protect the animals of this state.
All dogs six months and older must have a license. If you become the owner of a dog six months or older, the dog must be licensed by January of each year. To obtain a license for your dog, the owner must present a current State of Maine Rabies Certificate obtained from a veterinarian. This certificate will verify that your dog has received its rabies shot within the past two years.
Renewing the License
All dog licenses expire on December 31 of each year. Renewal of licenses for the following year can be done at the City Clerk's /Tax Collectors Office beginning in the middle of October of each calendar year. If your dog has been "fixed" since it was last licensed, bring proof of spaying/neutering from your veterinarian. If you have already shown this proof to the department, additional proof is not necessary.
Dog Licensing Fees
$6 per year for spayed/neutered dogs (altered)
$11 per year for dogs not spayed/neutered (unaltered)
$30 per year for a nuisance dog
$100 per year for a dangerous dog
$25 late fee per dog starting February 1 of each year
How to Obtain a License
In person: Visit the City Clerk’s/Tax Collector’s office during our regular business hours, which are Monday through Friday from 7:45 am to 4:30 pm. Please bring the current State of Maine rabies certificate and (if the dog is “fixed”) proof of spaying/neutering.
By mail: Send a current State of Maine rabies certificate and (if the dog is "fixed") proof of spaying/neutering, a self-addressed stamped envelope, along with a check to: City Clerk's Office, 16 Cony Street, Augusta, ME 04330
Online: You may also register your dog online at State Online Dog Licensing. This service does cost an additional $1 fee, and your license will be mailed to you. (The online option is only available October 15 - January 31 each year)
Dog licenses are available starting October 15 each year.





Registrations





Vehicle Registration
For renewing an existing vehicle registration, please bring:
Previous registration
Current insurance card (required)
Current vehicle mileage (required)
For your convenience, you may renew your registration online through Maine BMV Rapid Renewal.
Vehicle Purchased From a Dealership
For registering a vehicle purchased from a dealership, please bring:
Current insurance card
Current vehicle mileage
CTA (title application) provided by the dealership
Bill of sale
Window sticker / Monroney label, if the vehicle is brand new
Previous vehicle registration, if you are transferring plates
Vehicle Purchased in a Private Sale
For registering a vehicle purchased in a private sale, please bring:
Current insurance card
Current vehicle mileage
Original title from the previous owner
Bill of sale
Previous vehicle registration, if you are transferring plates
New License Plate Process
Maine's license plate process changed May 1, 2025. New License Plate Process (PDF)
Trailer Registration
For registering a newly purchased trailer or renewing an existing trailer registration, please bring:
Bill of sale
Title, if over 3,000 pounds
For your convenience, you may renew your registration online through Maine BMV Rapid Renewal.
Boat Registration
For renewing an existing boat registration, please bring:
Previous registration or a photo registration
For your convenience, you may renew your registration online through Maine Boat Registration Renewal Online Service.
Newly Purchased Boat
For registering a newly purchased boat, please bring:
Bill of sale
ATV & Snowmobile Registration
For renewing an existing ATV or snowmobile registration, please bring:
Previous registration or a photo registration
For your convenience, you may renew your registration online through the IF&W ATV & Snowmobile Registration Online Service.
Newly Purchased ATV or Snowmobile
For registering a newly purchased ATV or snowmobile, please bring:
Bill of sale
Hunting & Fishing Licenses
To purchase a hunting or fishing license, you will need:
Personal information, including name, date of birth, and contact information
Hunter safety information or a previous hunting license number, if purchasing a hunting license
Access to a printer if purchasing your license online
Purchase online through the Maine Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife (broken link).
You can also view current Maine outdoor laws through the Maine Department of Inland Fisheries & Wildlife Outdoor Laws.
