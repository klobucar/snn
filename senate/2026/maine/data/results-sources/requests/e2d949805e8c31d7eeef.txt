There will be two Public Hearings regarding the Moratoriums on new Subdivisions,  on Data Centers and the language on an Elected Officials Recall Ordinance.  Public Hearing dates will be during Select Board meetings on Monday November 2nd and Monday November 9th, both beginning at 5pm.  You may always email the Select Board if you are unable to attend any of the Public Hearings.
 
There will be a Special Town Meeting on Thursday November 12th at 6pm to vote on the above Moratoriums.  This meeting will be held at the Town House.
