DISCLAIMERS
Web Content Accessibility: The City of Lewiston is committed to providing access for all individuals of differing abilities who seek information on its site. Should a visitor have difficulty accessing information on the Lewiston site, particularly if using assistive technology, please contact mis@lewistonmaine.gov.  
Translations:  The City of Lewiston uses Google to translate its City web content. Accuracy is not guaranteed.
Municipal Invoice Scams: The City of Lewiston has been made aware of fraudulent invoices that closely mimic official City of Lewiston correspondence. These fraudulent emails often include invoices with the City seal and staff names and ask recipients to pay for the invoice. Residents and businesses are advised that legitimate City correspondence will only originate from an official “.gov” email address. If you receive a suspicious invoice or communication, do not respond or remit payment and contact the City staffer or mis@lewistonmaine.gov directly to verify its authenticity.
Legal Notices: To search a statewide database of public notices and legal advertisements published in Maine newspapers, please visit Maine Public Notices or by clicking this link. On this site, you can search through a database of statewide public notices and legal ads at no cost.
