Elections and Voting
How do I register to vote?
To register to vote in Kennebunkport, please come to the Town Clerk's office during regular business hours to fill out a voter registration application. Title 21-A Sec. 122 & 152 requires that you show a State of Maine-issued photo ID or Social Security Card and proof of residency in Kennebunkport. You may also register to vote at the polls on election day with the documents referenced above.  The deadline for receipt of voter registration applications submitted by mail or by a 3rd person is the close of business on the 21st day before election day. The deadline for in-person registration is the close of the polls on election day (Title 21-A Sec.121).
Click the Voter Registration Application Instructions link for guidance on completing the application.
Please note that it may save you from waiting in long lines on election day if you register at Town Hall beforehand.
Where/when do I vote?
All Kennebunkport residents vote at the Village Fire Station, 32 North Street. The polls are open at 8:00 AM and close at 8:00 PM. If you choose to vote absentee, you may pick up your ballots at Town Hall up to 30 days before any election. The last day to pick up absentee ballots is always close of business on the 5th day before the election (per State law).
Absentee Voting
Absentee ballots are available approximately 30 days before each election (Title 21-A Sec. 753). There are three ways to request ballots:
Stop by the Town Clerk's Office during regular business hours to pick up your ballots; 
Call the Town Clerk (967-1610) to request that ballots be mailed to you; or
Use the following link to electronically request that ballots be mailed to you (only if there is a State of Maine election)
 https://apps.web.maine.gov/cgi-bin/online/AbsenteeBallot/index.pl
See below for frequently asked questions regarding absentee voting. 
Per State Statute (Tile 21-A Sec. 753-B.2), the last day to request absentee ballots is 5 days before any election. Absentee ballots must be received by 8 PM on election day in order to be counted (Title 21-A Sec. 755). Please plan accordingly!

Town Meeting Information
Town meeting is the basic legislative assembly of local government used in Maine and throughout New England. At the heart of this type of government is the ideal of direct participation in local government. Kennebunkport uses a bifurcated (two-part) meeting style for the annual town meeting in June. There is a secret ballot election, often coupled with State elections, on the 2nd Tuesday of June. This ballot contains races for open town offices as well as ordinance revision questions. The Wednesday evening that follows is an open town meeting to consider all other business. A Special Town Meeting may be held in November to coincide with State and Federal elections. Ballot questions for Special Town Meetings are usually referendum questions.
How do I run for Select Board or School Board?
Select Board and School Board members are elected in June as part of the Annual Town Meeting secret ballot vote on the second Tuesday. In order to appear as a candidate on the ballot, an individual must circulate nomination papers and collect at least 25 signatures from Kennebunkport registered voters. Nomination papers are available 100 days before the election. They must be returned to the Town Clerk's Office with the minimum number of signatures at least 60 days before the election. The process and dates are set by State Statute 30-A MRSA, Section 2528(4). For more information, please contact the Clerk's Office at 967-1610.
Absentee Voting FAQs
Can I vote now?
Absentee ballots become available 30 days before every election.  At that time, you may pick-up ballots from the Town Clerk’s office for yourself and your immediate family members. 
I already requested my ballot. When will I get it?
If you requested your ballots online, by mail, or through the Town Clerk’s Office, your ballots will be mailed about 30 days before the election. 
I don’t want to wait. Can I pick up a ballot if I’ve already requested one?
If you would like to pick-up your ballots once we receive them, please let us know. We will make a note on your absentee ballot request form and call you when they are available.
Why do I continue to receive absentee ballot applications in the mail if I have already requested ballots?
The political parties as well as some special interest groups are mailing absentee ballot applications to voters. If you have already requested your ballots or if you wish to vote at the polls, just disregard the mailings. We have no control over these mailings. If you aren’t sure if you have already requested absentee ballots, please call the Town Clerk’s office to confirm (207-967-1610).
Will you have an outside drop box for ballots?
Yes!  It will be clearly marked for absentee ballots only and will be located by the sidewalk in front of Town Hall. Do not place absentee ballots in the black drop by the front door of Town Hall. This is not a State or Federally approved drop-box for ballots.
Is there a difference between absentee voting and mail in voting?
All voting in Maine prior to Election Day is considered absentee voting. Five states (Colorado, Hawaii, Oregon, Utah and Washington) use a mail-in only system for voting. Maine allows voters to vote via absentee ballot OR in-person at the polls on election day.
Don’t want to use the mail?
You can come to Town Hall after October 7th to pick-up ballots for yourself and your immediate family members.  You can then return the ballots to the Town Clerk’s Office during regular business hours or use our secure drop box that will be installed by the sidewalk in front of Town Hall.
How do I request an absentee ballot?
There are three ways to request an absentee ballot:
Stop by town hall during regular business hours to pick up your ballot.
You may call us at (207) 967-1610 and request that we mail one to you. If you leave a voicemail message, please include a return phone number. We must actually speak to each voter personally when requesting over the phone. 
Use this link to electronically request that your ballot be mailed to you.
Who can request an absentee ballot?
Any registered voter may request an absentee ballot.
How are absentee ballots processed?
Absentee ballots are processed and counted in the same manner as Election Day ballots.  The difference is that the voter is putting their ballots in an envelope, which they sign and seal.  When it is time to process the absentee ballots, we open the envelopes, separate the ballots and process them by using the same voter tabulation machines that are used on Election Day. This is done using several teams of election workers for accuracy as well as checks and balances. 
How can I see what is on the ballot before I get to the polls on election day?
Sample ballots will be available to view at Town Hall or on the Elections and Voting page on our website as soon as they are available. Information regarding registering to vote as well as ranked choice voting can also be found on the website.
