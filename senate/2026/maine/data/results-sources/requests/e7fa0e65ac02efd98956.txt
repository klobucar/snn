We would like to share important updates with our residents! 
🕘 Town Office Hours
 Monday: 9:00 AM – 2:30 PM     and    Thursday: 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM 
👋 Please welcome our new Town Clerk, Anne Drawdy - Kucipak, who started August 17! As we get settled, we anticipate expanding our office hours in late August and into September, with the goal of being open four days a week.
🚗 Motor Vehicle Registrations  -We will not have new license plates available in the Town Office until late fall. If you are registering a new vehicle or need new Tree plates, you will need to:
1. Pay your excise tax at the New Vineyard Town Office, then
2. Visit either Rumford or Augusta to obtain your new plates.
We can process renewal registrations for all vehicles, as we have registration stickers available in the office.
🎣 Other Services Available at the Town Office:
* ATV registrations
* Snowmobile registrations
* Hunting & fishing licenses
* Property tax payments
🗑️ Transfer Station Hours
* Wednesday: 12:30 PM – 3:30 PM
* Saturday: 7:30 AM – 3:30 PM
🔵 The current transfer station sticker color is Blue. If you have not purchased your new sticker, please stop by the Town Office.
Rapid Renewal is also available on line. You can now reregister your vehicles on line. You can do this by clicking on the "on-line services" icon at the top of this page or you can go directly the BMV Site and use the drop down menu to select New Vineyard.
