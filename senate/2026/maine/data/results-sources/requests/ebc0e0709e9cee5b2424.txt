HOMEPAGE FOR TOWN OF CHELSEA MAINE 
PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT: The Chelsea Selectboard Meeting scheduled for Wednesday, September 30 2026, has been CANCELLED due to there being no agenda items requiring action or discussion. The next meeting is October 14, 2026. Thank you for your understanding.
Please be aware that roadside mowing will be starting August 5, 2026 in Chelsea. Your patience and extra care around the contractor is greatly appreciated. 
Do you have time to give to your town? The Selectboard is asking for volunteers to serve on the Board of Appeals and the Board of Assessment Review. Boards and Committees Application.
The Town of Chelsea Annual Town Meeting was held on June 16, 2026. All warrant articles passed as written. Thank you to the 40 residents and 6 non-residents who attended and participated in the meeting.
