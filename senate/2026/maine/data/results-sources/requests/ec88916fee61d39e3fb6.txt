VOTER REGISTRATION & PARTY CHANGES
You can now register to vote or update your existing registration (including party changes) through the State of Maine Online Voter Registration Service.
What You Need to Register Online
If you have a valid Maine Driver’s License or State ID:
Your Maine Driver’s License (DL) or State ID number.
Your legal name exactly as it appears on your ID.
If you do NOT have a Maine Driver’s License or State ID:
The last four digits of your Social Security Number.
An image of your handwritten signature (digital/computerized signatures are not accepted).
Register to Vote Online Here
WAYS TO REGISTER
Online: Use the Online Voter Registration Service.
In-Person: Visit Town Hall during regular business hours or register at your polling location on Election Day.
By Mail: Complete the Voter Registration Application (PDF). Paper Applications are available at Town Hall.
Required Documentation: To register, you must be a U.S. Citizen, provide proof of identity, and proof of residency. A Maine driver’s license or State ID with your current Cape Elizabeth address satisfies both requirements. If registering by mail, please include a photocopy of your ID. Additional Information can be found here: https://www.maine.gov/sos/elections-voting/registering-to-vote

GET INVOLVED:  ELECTION WORKERS
If you are interested in working at the polls on Election Day or assisting with in-person absentee voting during the month leading up to the election, please fill out this short questionnaire:  https://forms.gle/2zkNRRotQe8PfPXK6 
Please keep the following State Law Requirements in mind before committing:
Eligibility
State law sets the requirements for election workers:
Must be at least 18 years old (16 and 17-year-olds who are conditionally registered to vote are allowed to serve)
Must be a registered Maine voter
Must be a resident of the municipality or county where they will be serving
Must not be a candidate or a member of a candidate’s immediate family
Duties & Tasks
Although the work may vary slightly, the following duties may be included:
Check in voters at the Incoming Voting List check-in table
Distribute Ballots
Monitor the voting machine or ballot box
Register New Voters
Process Absentee Ballots
** Training will be provided**

Elections & Voting FAQ
How do I register to vote?
The State of Maine allows residents to register to vote at any time, including the day of the election. Residents of Cape Elizabeth may register to vote at the Town Clerk/Tax Office at Town Hall, or at the Cape Elizabeth High School gymnasium the day of the election. Proof of residency and proof of identity is required.
For more voter information: https://www.maine.gov/sos/cec/elec/voter-info/index.html
Where do I vote?
All residents of Cape Elizabeth vote at the Cape Elizabeth High School gymnasium. The polls are open at 7:00 AM and close at 8:00 PM.
When are elections scheduled?
The municipal election is held on the Tuesday following the first Monday in November. Town Council and School Board members are elected for three-year terms. The terms are staggered so that 2 or 3 seats become available each year. The State of Maine general and referendum elections are held on Tuesday following the first Monday in November. The State Primary Election is held on the 2nd Tuesday of June of each general election year.
