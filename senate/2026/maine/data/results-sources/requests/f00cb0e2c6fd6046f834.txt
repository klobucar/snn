Notice to Voters
Town of Falmouth Legislative and Senatorial District Changes
Please be advised that the State Representative District numbers have changed in accordance with the adoption of the 130th Legislature's apportionment laws.
District 43 is now District 112
District 44 is now District 111
It is possible that your State Representative has changed as a part of that process as well. Please see the Secretary of State's web lookup for your new district numbers and your representatives.
This change does not affect the polling location.
