Elections
"Election workers are essential to ensuring that elections are a success. With each election, millions of Americans dedicate themselves to sustaining the backbone of democracy - our election process. The Town of Thorndike encourages those interested in becoming election workers at the polls on election day to learn more about what is required and how to sign up to work with your local election official." u.s. Election Assistance Commission 
 

If you are interested in volunteering before, on, or after election day Please contact Rose Hill at the Town Office. Thank you!

How do I register to vote?






 How do I register to vote?
You can come into the Town office. Please have with you updated idea and proof of residency, they must match. You can also register at Dept. Motor vehicles. Be sure to choose your party affiliation on the card.
