Elections
Who can Register to vote? 
You must be 18 years to vote in any election. Residents 17 years of age, who will be turning 18, prior to the election can register to vote.
How do I register to vote?
The State of Maine allows residents to register to vote anytime, including the day of the election. Residents of Anson may register to vote at the Town office at any time during regular business hours. Proof of residency and proof of identity is required — such as a driver’s license indicating a physical street address in Anson (not a post office box mailing address). If your driver’s license shows a previous address, please bring other documents (checkbook, utility bills, etc.) that show your Acton Street Address.
Residents may also register to vote on Election Day at the polls. To assist us in making the voting process as easy as possible, we ask that you register in advance if at all possible.
When are Elections held?
Municipal Elections are usually held in March of every year.
School Elections are usually held in June of every year.
Absentee Voting
You no longer have to wait and vote on Election Day. The Town offers Absentee ballots which can be picked up at the Town Clerks Office or mailed to you at your request. This allows you vote ahead of time if you are going to be out of town or cannot make it to the polls.
