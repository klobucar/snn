REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
Structural Engineer Review 468
Limerick Road
 

The Town of Arundel, Maine is
seeking proposals from State of Maine approved Structural Engineers or Firms to
provide professional services to assist the town in the structural review of
the existing Arundel Fire Station built in 1998.
 

 The proposals shall be submitted either by
envelope clearly identified on the outside as “Structural Engineer Review to
the following address or it can be e-mailed to the Town Manager at the e-mail
below.
 

                   Keith M. Trefethen, Town Manager
                   Town of Arundel
                   257 Limerick Road
                   Arundel, Maine 04046
                     e-mail: townmanager@arundelmaine.org
 

All proposals shall be
returned to the Town Manager by November 1, 2026, and include a flat price
and/or hourly price for your engineering services.
 

Inquiries regarding this RFP
can be made and submitted via, fax (207-985-7589) or e-mail to: townmanager@arundelmaine.org
 

 

Background
The Town of Arundel is in need
of a professional engineering evaluation of the structural integrity of its
existing fire station as it relates to a possible expansion. The station
presently consists of an apparatus area that is 3-bays and 4,080 square feet in
size, and a living area of 1,240 square feet that includes, kitchen/day room
and 4 bedrooms. The Town is considering the expansion of the apparatus bay to
the rear of 900 square feet (60x15), to include 3 additional bay openings to be
in line with the openings at the front of the structure. The town is seeking
opinions and advice as to what code requirements this proposed expansion might
involve and whether there are code impacts beyond the expansion itself. It is
in need of professional engineering evaluation of the viability of the proposed
project. Further the Town would seek opinions relative to the repositioning of
the Decontamination Room and whether the proposed expansion can be conducted
without interruption to the station’s normal conduct of its business.
