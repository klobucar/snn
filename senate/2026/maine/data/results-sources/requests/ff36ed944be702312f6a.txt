November 3, 2026 Election: 
GENERAL & MUNICIPAL ELECTION 
NOVEMBER 3, 2026 
ON ELECTION DAY, ALL WATERVILLE VOTERS VOTE AT: 
Thomas College Field House (Harold Alfond Athletic Center, rear entrance) 180 W. River Rd. (enter through south entrance on Vining Drive, follow signs) 
On November 3rd , polls will be open from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. 
*****************************************************************
Voter Registration is available for new voters to register or for existing Waterville voters to change their residence address or name as follows: 
•    Online at https://registertovote.sos.maine.gov (available until 10/13/26)
•    By mail by submitting a completed voter registration card along with a photocopied ID and proof of residency. (After October 13th, voters must register in person).
•    At the polling place on election day (there may be lines so please plan accordingly)
•    At Waterville City Hall during regular business hours (M-TH 7 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.)
To register to vote you will need to provide:
Proof of identity (including, but not limited to a current and valid driver’s license, state ID, military ID, passport, certified birth certificate, signed social security card, verified unique identifying numbers OR other official documents) 
Proof of residency (any piece of mail or document showing your Waterville residence address, or an oath of residency when proof of identity can be provided) 
 Voter Registration is Available at Online 
State of Maine Online Voter Registration Service
*****************************************************************
 Absentee Ballots may be requested by:
 •    The voter by telephone by calling 680-4209 (deadline 10/29/26)
•    Written request to City Clerk, 1 Common Street, Waterville (deadline 10/29/26)
•    Visiting the City Hall Annex (46 Front St.) in-person (M - TH 8 am – 5 pm) (deadline 10/29/26)
•    Online at http://www.maine.gov/cgi-bin/online/AbsenteeBallot/index.pl (deadline 10/29/26) 
Check the status of your absentee ballot here: https://absenteeballotrequest.sos.maine.gov/
The deadline to vote an in-person absentee ballot is Thursday, October 29, 2026 
(with the exception of incapacitation/illness or unexpected absence on election day sworn under oath).
 
Completed absentee ballots must be received by 8 p.m. on November 3rd and may be returned by mail, in person to the City Hall Annex, 46 Front Street, or deposited in the secure drop box located outside of the rear (elevator) entrance of City Hall.
 
 Polling Location for the November 3, 2026 Elections will be at: Thomas College
All Waterville voters vote at the Harold Alfond Athletic Center at Thomas College.
180 West River Road, Waterville, Maine (enter through the athletic field entrance on Vining Drive)
