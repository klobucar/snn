                                                    MINUTES
                                                  July 20, 2021
                                                   12:00 P.M.
                                          Wake County Board of Elections

Chair Greg Flynn called the regularly scheduled meeting of the Wake County Board of Elections to order at 12:00
p.m. on July 20, 2021. Present for the meeting were Erica Porter, Angela Hawkins, Gerry Cohen, Keith Weatherly,
Greg Flynn, Director Gary Sims, Deputy Director Olivia McCall, and staff members Spencer Berg, Evan Wingate,
Chris Brown, Sarah Malcolm, Nick Marriam, Luwam Zeray, Riley Chater, Morgan Chance, Tiana Threatt, Lauren
Nelson, and Jacqueline Cameron. A list of members of the public in attendance is attached.

The Board led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Judge Keith Gregory administered the Oath of Office to Erica Porter, Angela Hawkins, Gerry Cohen, Keith
Weatherly, and Greg Flynn, copies of which are attached. All members were previously appointed by the State
Board of Elections.

Member Greg Flynn nominated Angela Hawkins as the Secretary of the Board, second by Keith Weatherly, the
Board unanimously agrees.

On motion by Angela Hawkins, second by Greg Flynn, the Board approved the agenda as presented.

On motion by Greg Flynn, second by Gerry Cohen, the Board approved the Minutes from the January 7, 2021
board meeting.

Chair Erica Porter opened the floor for public comments.

After discussion and review, on motion by Greg Flynn, second by Gerry Cohen, the Board unanimously approved
the resolutions for the use of satellite precincts 01-07A and 07-07A pursuant to G.S. 163-130. Chair Erica Porter
signed the resolutions.

After discussion and review, on motion by Angela Hawkins, second by Greg Flynn, the Board approved the
continued use of Out of Precinct polling places for the following precincts pursuant to G.S. 163-130.1. Chair Erica
