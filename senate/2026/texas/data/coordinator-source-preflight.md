The [Legislative Council election collection](https://data.capitol.texas.gov/topic/about/elections) explicitly says its data is developed for redistricting, derived from county/SOS returns and may differ from official results. It directs users to SOS/county officials for official counts. The [2024 General dataset](https://data.capitol.texas.gov/dataset/2024_general) actually links CSV resources:

- Senate: `https://ted.capitol.texas.gov/api/Offices/510/2/vtd`.
- President: `https://ted.capitol.texas.gov/api/Offices/510/1/vtd`.
- Voter data: `https://ted.capitol.texas.gov/api/Offices/510/1428/vtd`.

All three downloaded successfully, with 9,712 distinct CNTYVTD/VTDKEY rows across 254 county-code prefixes. They are **VTD geography**, not an authenticated native precinct roster. The [precinct catalog](https://data.capitol.texas.gov/dataset/precincts) has distinct 2026-primary, 2024-general/primary and older actual precinct shapefiles. Its county-adoption/reference caveat remains. The [VTD catalog](https://data.capitol.texas.gov/dataset/vtds) describes noncontiguous splits and consolidation when precincts do not match census geography. VTD election/count conversions cannot establish November approval or a one-to-one native precinct crosswalk.
