Overview of SB 2753
 Eliminates the three-day gap between early voting and election day
 Mandates weekend voting on holidays, Saturdays and Sundays
 Redefines reporting groups as “Personal Appearance” rather than in-person
 early voting and election day
 Requires polling places used during early voting to be used as election day
 locations, and may also require some election day-only locations

                       Provisions apply after Secretary of State

SB 2753 – Voting System Procedures
 Same voting system equipment can be used for both EV and ED
 Early voting results are reported with election day results as one category (in-
 person voting)
 Must generate precinct-by-precinct returns, and separately generate results
 by polling place
 Cannot generate a report of results by precinct at each polling place, to
 protect ballot secrecy
 Zero tapes printed at polling place on first day of voting at that location
 Results tape printed at close of polls on election day with EV and ED results
 Pollbooks may require updates between end of EV and ED–especially precinct-
 based counties                                                                     13

Implementation Checklist
 Rebuild county calendars to reflect continuous voting periods
 Confirm facility availability for required hours and weekend operations
 Adjust equipment deployment schedules
 Fiscal and budgetary concerns must be included in the implementation plan
 Update reporting software and templates for location-based results
 Train staff on new procedures and terminology changes

                                       Priority Timeline
     Complete calendar rebuilding and facility contracts first. Staff training and equipment
   updates follow. Begin budgetary forecasting before SOS publishes implementation report.
